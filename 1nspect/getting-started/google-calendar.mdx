Connecting Google Calendar is the one integration setup most subscribers care about on day one. Once an inspector connects their calendar, booking honors their real availability and inspection events are written to the calendar automatically. Nothing double-books, nothing shows up on a day they already blocked for a dentist appointment.

<Info>
The integration is optional. Without it, booking offers every slot inside each inspector's Working Schedule regardless of what else is on their calendar.
</Info>

## Per-inspector connection (recommended)

Each inspector connects their own Google account. This is the setup covered in [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team).

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Users tab">
Top nav **Business Ops**, then the **Users** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit the inspector">
Click edit on the inspector's row. The Google Calendar section appears only when editing an existing user, so create the user first, then edit.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect in the Google Calendar section">
Under **Google Calendar**, click Connect. You are redirected to Google in the same browser tab. Approve 1nspecT's access.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
After approval, Google sends you back to 1nspecT, which returns you to **Business Ops › Users**. The inspector's Google Calendar section now shows a **Connected** badge. The inspector's bookings will now check availability on this calendar.
</Step>
</Steps>

Repeat for every inspector. Each inspector controls their own connection.

## Single-inspector firms

Calendar sync is per inspector. There is no shared company-wide booking calendar today. A one-person firm gets "one calendar for everyone" simply by having that inspector connect their own Google account on their user profile.

## What syncs

When Calendar is connected:

- **Availability** — bookings on the public form read every calendar the inspector owns or can write to, and only show open slots inside their Working Schedule
- **Event creation** — when an order is created (order wizard or Field Quote, or when a public-booking lead is converted to an order; a public booking on its own creates only a lead, so no event is written until it becomes an order), 1nspecT writes an event to the inspector's primary calendar titled `Order No- {id}`, with the clients, agents, services and fees in the description
  - **Converted quotes:** converting a single-inspection quote writes the event, if the quote names an inspector and has a booked date. A multi-phase order gets one event per phase from **Review & Send**.
  - **Length:** the event lasts the inspection's estimated duration, plus any travel/setup buffer added to that order. An inspection with no booked date gets no event.
- **Event updates** — rescheduling an inspection updates the event; cancelling removes it
- **Reassigning** — on an inspection with a booked date, assigning a different inspector moves the event: it's deleted from the old inspector's calendar and created on the new one's. If the new inspector hasn't connected Google Calendar, the old event is still removed and no new one is written.

Calendar writes never block your work. If Google can't be reached, the conversion or assignment still goes through without the event.

## What does not sync

- Non-Google calendars (Outlook, iCal, Apple Calendar) are not supported today
- Events are one-way from 1nspecT to Google. Editing an event in Google does not change the 1nspecT inspection.
- There is no per-user scheduling buffer. The hours concept is the inspector's Working Schedule. You can add a travel/setup buffer to an individual order instead: **Add buffer (travel / setup)** in the Order Creation Wizard's Inspection Time step (None, +15, +30, +45 or +60 minutes). It's included in the calendar event's length. The public booking form doesn't offer it.
- 1nspecT asks Google for two scopes: `calendar.events` and `calendar.readonly`.

## Troubleshooting

- **Clients see slots on days the inspector blocked.** The inspector probably has not connected their calendar yet, or the connection is to a different Google account than where the block is.
- **Inspections aren't appearing on the calendar.** Confirm the OAuth is still valid; if you changed Google passwords, you may need to reconnect.
