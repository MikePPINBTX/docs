Contracts Cockpit is where you build the service agreements your clients sign. 1nspecT has built-in e-signature, so clients sign in a browser and the signed PDF is stamped and stored on the order. You can have one agreement for every order or several agreements for different service types.

<Info>
**Required** to run your first inspection: at least one agreement set to apply to All orders. The agreement must be signed before the inspection can start on the phone.
</Info>

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Business Ops**, then click the **Contracts** tab (fourth of seven tabs). The section inside is labeled **Agreements**.

## Add an agreement

Click the add action to open the Agreement editor. Fill in:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Agreement name | What subscribers see. Placeholder: `Agreement name (e.g. Primary Service Agreement)`. |
| Send this agreement when | The trigger: when the agreement is sent to the client (for example, after booking confirmed). |
| Agreement Body | The rich-text body of the agreement. Use **Insert Tag** to insert dynamic fields. |

### Insert Tag

Click **Insert Tag** to open the tag picker. Tags are grouped:

- **Contract Date** — the date on the agreement itself
- **Client** — primary client details
- **Co-Client** — co-signer details
- **Address** — property address
- **Service** — primary and ancillary services on the order
- **Scheduling** — inspection date, time, inspector
- **Pricing** — the fee breakdown
- **Signature — Client** — the required signature block for the primary client
- **Signature — Co-Client** — the required signature block for the co-signer
- **Company** — your company name, address, logo

Tags render at send time, so each client sees their own name, address, fee and signature block.

### Save

Click **Save Agreement** at the bottom of the editor.

## Test the agreement

Use **Test Send** (next to Save) to preview the agreement filled in with a real order's data without sending it to the client.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Test Send">
At the bottom of the Agreement editor, click **Test Send**. A panel opens.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste an Inspection ID">
Enter the ID of an existing inspection. Placeholder: `Inspection ID (e.g. 2026-0062-RS)`.
</Step>
<Step title="Send">
Click **Send**. 1nspecT sends every applicable agreement for that inspection in test mode. You see exactly what the client would see.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Multiple agreements

For companies that run multiple service types, you can have more than one agreement:

- A primary service agreement that applies to **All orders**
- A reinspection agreement that applies only to **Reinspections**
- An ancillary agreement for a specific ancillary (if that ancillary has **Own Agreement** turned on in [Ancillary services](/1nspect/getting-started/ancillary-services))

1nspecT sends every applicable agreement as a bundle when the triggering event fires.

## Deleting an agreement

Click the delete action on an agreement card. The confirmation reads: _"Delete this agreement? This cannot be undone."_
