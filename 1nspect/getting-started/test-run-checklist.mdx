Before your first real client, do a full dry run with your onboarding specialist. One test order carried end to end (booking, agreement, payment, inspection, published report, delivered to client) will surface anything you missed in Stages 1 through 4. Fix it in the test and then you are live.

## Setup checklist

Confirm every item is in place before you create the test order.

**Stage 1 — Identity**

- Company name is set and the logo displays on the web admin
- Brand colors look right in the web admin after you save them on Business Ops › Company Info
- At least one inspector is in Business Ops › Users with the **Inspector** toggle on

**Stage 2 — Services and pricing**

- Primary Services tab has at least one service with a valid price (Save Registry committed)
- Ancillary Services, Packages, Discounts, Surcharges and Settings have been reviewed (Save Fees & Surcharges committed)
- Any surcharge you want applied has its **Active** toggle on

**Stage 3 — Reports**

- The template you plan to use is Active on the Universal Template Builder
- At least one narrative collection exists (required to start an inspection on mobile). A caption library is optional but gives you autocomplete; set company defaults in Business Ops › Company Info › Inspection Defaults
- Your cover page design is set

**Stage 4 — Client paperwork and comms**

- At least one agreement in Business Ops › Contracts applies to **All orders**
- Every workflow step you want to fire is Enabled in Business Ops › Communications › Workflow (starter steps are seeded disabled)
- Any review-request step uses the **Review Request** trigger, not Report Delivered
- The booking link in Company Info works
- The inspector you'll use has Google Calendar connected

## Test run steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a test order">
From the top bar, open **Inspections** and click **Create Inspection**. Walk through the Order Creation Wizard with yourself as the client and a real property address. Confirm the price looks right.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the inspection details">
On the Inspection Details screen for the test order, confirm these are set:

- **Inspector** (assigned, with a display name that exactly matches)
- **Template** (your template)
- **Narrative Collection** (your narrative library)
- **Caption Library** (optional)

Setting the **Inspection Type** is optional but sharpens the AI narratives, so set it. Three things hide a job from the phone: the agreement has not been sent yet, the inspection is more than 30 days out, or the inspector's display name does not exactly match the assigned name. A missing template shows a Setup Required card instead.
</Step>
<Step title="Send yourself the agreement">
Send the agreement (the job stays hidden on the phone until it has been sent). Open your email, click the agreement link, and sign in the client portal. Starting the inspection on mobile requires the signed agreement.
</Step>
<Step title="Pay the test order">
If your workflow charges at signing or at publishing, process the test payment (use a Stripe test card for sandbox).
</Step>
<Step title="Open the inspection on mobile">
Sign in to the 1nspecT mobile app as the assigned inspector. Confirm the test inspection appears in your Assigned Inspections list and marks as Ready offline.
</Step>
<Step title="Run the inspection">
Capture a few findings on the Capture screen. Try the AI buttons: AI Generate, Vision Scan, Scan Label / Reading. Rate every area. Finish and Draft the report when you're done.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish the report">
From the Report Workspace in the web admin, run **AI Draft & Polish** (50 credits for the drafting pass plus 10 for Polish Report), review the narratives, then Publish.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the delivery">
Check the client email you receive. Confirm:

- The report link opens the mobile-first web viewer
- The PDF opens with your branding, your cover design, your logo, your colors
- Any secondary emails fire (payment receipt; the review request fires on publish with its Send Offset)
</Step>
<Step title="Clean up the test order">
Mark the test order cancelled or deleted so it doesn't appear in your real reports.
</Step>
</Steps>
