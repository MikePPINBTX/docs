Your logo and company name carry through reports, emails, agreements, portals and cover pages. The main branding controls live on the **Settings** page, with a secondary set of logo and color controls also available in Business Operations. White-label mode is separate and affects only the web admin's own top bar.

<Info>
White-label mode is included on every plan. There is no separate white-label tier.
</Info>

## Where branding lives

In the top navigation, click **Settings**. The page header reads **Settings**, and the **Company** tab (the first of three tabs: Company, Users and Billing) holds the branding controls:

| Section | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| Color Scheme | Primary, Secondary and Accent colors with native color pickers. Live-updates the Live Preview panel on the right. |
| Preset Themes | Pick a ready-made theme as a starting point, then tweak. |
| Embed Your Wizard | An embed snippet that is currently **broken**: it points at `/quote?embed=true`, which lands on the login page. Use the Public Booking Form in Business Ops › Company Info instead. See [online booking](/1nspect/getting-started/online-booking). |
| White Label Mode | Toggle **Enable White Label**. Changes only the web admin's own top bar. This toggle is saved in your browser only. |
| Live Preview | Hidden until you click **Show Preview**. Shows sample navigation and buttons in your current colors. |

## Logo

Upload your logo file in **Settings › Company**. Business Ops › Company Info only has a **Logo URL** field. The logo appears on the cover of every report PDF, the top of client emails, the client portal, the realtor portal, and the agreement PDF. PNG or SVG works best; roughly 200 by 50 pixels is a good size.

<Note>
Color and logo controls also appear inside Business Ops › Company Info. Either place saves the same values. The Settings page is the recommended spot because it has the preview and the preset themes. The Business Ops white-label checkbox saves to your account; the Settings toggle is saved in the browser only.
</Note>

## White-label mode

Turn on **Enable White Label** on the Settings page. The toggle changes **only the web admin's own top bar** (the 1nspecT logo and trade dress in your logged-in view). Reports, PDFs, emails, agreements and the client and realtor portals are **not** affected by this toggle. Nothing in the backend reads the setting. If you expected white-label to strip 1nspecT branding from client-facing documents, that is not what the switch does.
