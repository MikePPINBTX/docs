Your brand carries through the whole product: reports, emails, agreements, portals, cover pages. 1nspecT applies your logo and colors globally. The main branding controls live on the **Settings** page, with a secondary set of logo and color controls also available in Business Operations.

<Info>
White-label mode is included on every plan. There is no separate white-label tier.
</Info>

## Where branding lives

In the top navigation, click **Settings**. The page header reads **Settings**, and the **Company** tab (the first of two tabs: Company and Users) holds every branding control:

| Section | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| Color Scheme | Primary, Secondary and Accent colors with native color pickers. Live-updates the Live Preview panel on the right. |
| Preset Themes | Pick a ready-made theme as a starting point, then tweak. |
| Embed Your Wizard | A copy-pasteable embed snippet for the public booking widget. See [online booking](/1nspect/features/public-booking) for how it displays on your website. |
| White Label Mode | Toggle **Enable White Label**. Hides 1nspecT branding from reports, emails, agreements and the client portal, leaving only your logo and colors. |
| Live Preview | A live render of how a sample report cover and client email will look with your current settings. |

## Logo

Upload your logo in Business Ops › Company Info (Logo URL field). The logo appears on the cover of every report PDF, the top of client emails, the client portal, the realtor portal, and the agreement PDF. PNG or SVG works best; roughly 200 by 50 pixels is a good size.

<Note>
Color and logo controls also appear inside Business Ops › Company Info. Either place saves the same values. The Settings page is the recommended spot because it has the Live Preview and the preset themes.
</Note>

## White-label mode

Turn on **Enable White Label** on the Settings page. Reports, emails, agreements and the client portal will drop the 1nspecT trade dress and show only your branding. The toggle takes effect immediately: no publish step and no rebuild of past reports. New PDFs and client-facing pages generated after you flip the toggle use the new branding.
