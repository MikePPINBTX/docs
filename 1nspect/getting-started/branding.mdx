Your logo and company name carry through reports, emails, agreements, portals and cover pages. All branding controls live in one place: **Business Ops › Company Info**. White-label mode is on the same page and affects only the web admin's own top bar.

<Info>
White-label mode is included on every plan. There is no separate white-label tier.
</Info>

## Where branding lives

In the top navigation, click **Business Ops** (the page header reads **Business Operations**). You land on the **Company Info** tab, the first of the tabs across the top. The palette icon in the header (tooltip **Company & Branding**) opens the same tab. The branding controls are:

| Section | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| Company Logo | Click **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo** once one is set). PNG or JPEG. |
| Brand Colors | Primary, Secondary and Accent colors with color pickers. |
| Preset themes | **1nspecT (Default)**, **Professional Blue**, **Forest Green** and **Corporate Gray**. Clicking one fills in the three brand colors; tweak from there. |
| Installed App Status-Bar Color | The status-bar color when the web app is installed on a phone or tablet. |
| Enable White-Label Mode | Hides the 1nspecT branding in the web admin's own top bar. |
| Public Booking Form | Your direct booking link and the iframe embed code. See [online booking](/1nspect/getting-started/online-booking). |

Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the tab. Colors, company name and white-label apply as soon as you save; there is no need to reload.

## Logo

Upload your logo with the **Upload logo** button on Company Info. The logo is **saved as soon as it uploads**; you do not need to click Save Changes for it. The logo appears on the cover of every report PDF, the top of client emails, the client portal, the realtor portal, and the agreement PDF. PNG or JPEG are accepted; roughly 200 by 50 pixels is a good size.

## White-label mode

Turn on **Enable White-Label Mode** on Business Ops › Company Info and click **Save Changes**. This is the only white-label switch, and it is saved to your account. The toggle changes **only the web admin's own top bar** (the 1nspecT logo and trade dress in your logged-in view). Reports, PDFs, emails, agreements and the client and realtor portals are **not** affected by this toggle. Nothing in the backend reads the setting. If you expected white-label to strip 1nspecT branding from client-facing documents, that is not what the switch does.
