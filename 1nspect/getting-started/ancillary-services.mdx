Ancillary services are the add-ons you bill on top of a primary inspection: sewer scope, pool and spa, radon, mold, WDI, thermal imaging, and so on. Each ancillary has its own fee, duration, tax treatment, and category.

## Where to find it

**Business Ops › Pricing**, then the **Ancillary Services** tab (second tab, labeled `ancillary` under the hood).

## Fields per ancillary

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| ID | Short identifier, auto-filled. |
| Name | What subscribers and clients see. New rows default to `New Service`. |
| What this does | Holds capability tags that describe what the service covers. |
| Priced by square footage | Checkbox that switches the service to sqft-tier pricing (this is how detached structures work). See [Detached structures](/1nspect/getting-started/detached-structures). |
| Internal Only | Toggle to hide from the public booking form. Useful for crew-only extras. |
| Category | Standard, Environmental, Structural, Custom. See below. |
| Base Fee ($) | The regular price per inspection. |
| New Construction ($) | Optional override for new-construction orders. Placeholder: `same` means it uses Base Fee. |
| Duration (min) | How many minutes this service adds to the on-site time. |
| Taxable | Whether sales tax applies. |
| Pass-Through | Grosses up the fee for card processing: (fee + flat) ÷ (1 − rate). It has nothing to do with tax or third parties. |
| Standalone | Whether this can be sold on its own without a primary service. |
| Standalone Price ($) | Price when sold standalone. |
| Own Agreement | Whether this service needs its own agreement sent separately. |
| Revenue Model | In-House, Managed (3rd Party), or Referral. See below. |
| 3rd Party Cost ($) | Shown when Revenue Model is Managed or Referral. What you pay the outside vendor. See below. |

## Categories

Four categories are available for organizing your ancillaries. The category is a label for your own reference in Pricing Manager; it does not change how the service is priced, offered or shown on reports:

| Category | Typical services |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | Thermal imaging, sewer scope, WDI, pool and spa, lawn irrigation |
| Environmental | Radon, mold, air quality, water quality |
| Structural | Detached garage, outbuilding, guest house, barn (see [Detached structures](/1nspect/getting-started/detached-structures)) |
| Custom | Anything not covered by the three above |

## Revenue model

Records who does the work for the ancillary:

| Model | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **In-House** | Your inspectors do the work. |
| **Managed (3rd Party)** | You bill it, an outside vendor does the work. |
| **Referral** | You pass the client to an outside vendor. |

The revenue model itself is a label; no report reads it. What matters for reporting is **3rd Party Cost ($)**, which appears when you pick Managed or Referral. The monthly reconciliation report uses that amount as your cost for the service.

## Pass-through fees

Turn on **Pass-Through** when you want the client to cover the card-processing cost of this fee. The price is grossed up using your Processing Rate and Flat Fee (Pricing › Settings): (fee + flat fee) ÷ (1 − rate). Pass-Through is not about tax, and it does not mean the money goes to a third party.

## Standalone sales

Orders can be ancillary-only (no primary service) when the ancillary has **Standalone** turned on. Use this for recall inspections, re-inspection add-ons, or an on-demand pool-only visit. The Standalone Price often differs from the Base Fee because there's no primary service to piggyback on.

## Save

**Save Fees & Surcharges** at the top right or the bottom of the page commits every ancillary change along with any changes you made on the Packages, Discounts, Surcharges and Settings tabs.
