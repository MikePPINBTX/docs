Your template defines the structure of an inspection: sections, subsections, fields and ratings. Your **caption library** feeds the caption autocomplete on mobile, and your **narrative library** feeds AI drafting and Smart Search. Libraries are chosen **per inspection** (on Inspection Details), with company defaults in **Business Ops › Company Info › Inspection Defaults**. The association with a template is only a fallback.

<Info>
**Required** to start an inspection on mobile: a template and a narrative collection. The caption library is optional.
</Info>

<Warning>
A missing caption library does not block the inspection from syncing; it just means the caption autocomplete is empty on mobile. A missing narrative collection stops the inspector from starting.
</Warning>

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Narrative Management** (fifth top-nav item). The page header reads **Intelligence & Library Hub**.

Five tabs run across the page:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Narrative Library** | Your caption and narrative collections, with the Associate-Template picker |
| **Supplemental Documents** | Disclosure PDFs offered as client-portal downloads by rule (year built, service, property type) |
| **AI Persona (Voice DNA)** | Your company's writing voice (owners and admins only) |
| **Option Lists** | Reusable pick lists for template fields |
| **Reference Snippets** | Short citations or links inspectors can attach to findings |

For this step, stay on the **Narrative Library** tab.

## Create collections

Inside the Narrative Library tab, you'll see every collection on your account. Collections are groups of captions and narratives organized by template, property type, era, or any other grouping you want. New subscribers typically start with:

- A caption collection and a narrative collection for the primary residential SoP you selected at signup (TREC or InterNACHI)
- One or more collections for new-construction phase inspections (Phase 1 Pre-Pour, Phase 2 Pre-Drywall)

To add captions and narratives, open a collection and click into the Collection Detail view. You can also import an existing library in XML format (including HomeInspectorPro exports) from the **Import** tab.

## Associate a collection with your template

On a collection card, click **Associate Template** and pick the template. This sets the fallback library for that template. There is also a **Sync Structure from Templates** button on the page. The libraries actually used by an inspection are the ones set on that inspection's Inspection Details, or the company defaults.

<Tip>
**One template can carry multiple collections of each type.** Same compliant ratings, different wording. A single TREC template can serve a 2026 new build and an 1881 historical home, each with its own deficiency language. You pick which collection applies per inspection, on Inspection Details.
</Tip>

## Confirm your libraries before your test run

Before your first inspection:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Narrative Library tab">
Top nav Narrative Management, Narrative Library tab (first tab).
</Step>
<Step title="Find each collection you expect to use">
At minimum, one caption collection and one narrative collection per template.
</Step>
<Step title="Verify the template picker shows your template name">
If the picker shows no template, the fallback association didn't save. Reopen the picker and click **Associate Template**.
</Step>
</Steps>
