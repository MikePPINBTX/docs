A template is the blueprint for an inspection: the sections and subsections that run across the top of the mobile Capture screen, the fields the inspector fills in, the rating scale that follows the standard, and the layout the finished report prints with. 1nspecT seeds your account with the templates most subscribers need on day one, so you can run your first inspection without authoring anything.

<Info>
  **Required** to run your first inspection: at least one template available on your account. The seeded templates below satisfy this.
</Info>

## What ships with your account

When you sign up and pick your inspection standard, 1nspecT seeds your account with the matching residential template plus two phase-inspection templates. Each template name starts with your account ID (shown as `{account}` below):

| Template | When it's seeded | Based on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **{account} TREC 7-6** (residential) | You chose TREC at signup | TREC 7-6 (Texas Real Estate Commission) |
| **{account} InterNACHI** (residential) | You chose InterNACHI (or anything other than TREC) at signup | InterNACHI Residential standard |
| **{account} Phase 1 Pre-Pour** | Always seeded | Phase 1 Pre-Pour standard |
| **{account} Phase 2 Pre-Drywall** | Always seeded | Phase 2 Pre-Drywall standard |

You can use these as-is, clone them to customize, or build a completely custom template of your own.

## Where to find your templates

In the web admin top bar, click **Templates**. The page header reads **Universal Template Builder**. Each of your templates appears as a card. On every card you'll see:

- The template name
- A green **Active** badge when the template is currently usable on orders
- A standard badge (for example, TREC, InterNACHI, Phase 1 Pre-Pour) and the matching `SoP:` identifier
- A short description
- The version
- A blue **Schema Linked** badge when the template is bound to a schema
- Created-by and date
- Action icons at the bottom of the card: Edit, Rename, Upload Schema, Clone, Preview and Delete (there is no Design icon; Preview opens a read-only view of the template's sections, subsections and fields, with each field's type, whether it is required, and its options)

## Starting a new template

Top-right of the page, two buttons (Professional and Team plans only; on Solo you see a note linking to the plans):

- **Initialize from SoP** clones a platform library bundle (one of the standards 1nspecT already supports) into a new template on your account. Use this when you want a working starting point from an existing standard and plan to tweak it.
- **Create Custom Template** starts a blank template that you build up from scratch. Use this when none of the included standards fits what you need.

<Tip>
  For most subscribers, the right first move is to work with the templates that shipped with the account rather than making new ones. Open an existing template with the edit icon, confirm the sections match how you inspect, and only create a new template if you have a specific need (a specialty inspection type, a non-standard rating scale, or a workflow that cannot be expressed by customizing an existing template).
</Tip>

## Customizing a template

Click the **Edit** icon on any template card to open its overview page. From there you can:

- Open the **Schema** page to edit sections, subsections and fields (the field editor lives on the Schema page)
- Use the **Visual Designer** tab to adjust the printed layout
- Rename the template with the card's **Rename** icon
- Edit the cover page separately in [Cover Designer](/1nspect/admin-operator/cover-designer)

<Note>
  Ratings are edited on the Standard of Practice, not on the template. Primary ratings on TREC and InterNACHI are locked even on clones; only the reinspection ratings can be edited. See [SoP Manager](/1nspect/admin-operator/sop-manager) and [Choose your SoP](/1nspect/getting-started/choose-your-sop).
</Note>

## Smart equipment fields

On the Schema page, you can add fields that compute values from captures: temperature rise (supply minus return), equipment age (this year minus manufacture year), amperage draws, and so on. Set an advisory range on any computed field and the mobile app will flag out-of-range readings in the field, before the inspector moves to the next room. Covered in detail in [Smart equipment fields](/1nspect/getting-started/smart-equipment-fields).

## Caption and narrative libraries

Starting an inspection on the phone needs a **narrative collection**; the caption library is optional (a missing one only means empty caption autocomplete). Libraries are set per inspection, with company defaults in Business Ops › Company Info › Inspection Defaults. See [Attach caption and narrative libraries](/1nspect/getting-started/attach-libraries).
