The Settings tab of Pricing Manager carries the account-wide numbers every other price reads from: your sales tax rate, your payment-processing rate and flat fee, and the duration formula 1nspecT uses to block the right amount of time on each inspector's calendar.

## Where to find it

**Business Ops › Pricing**, then the **Settings** tab (sixth tab, labeled `settings` under the hood).

## Financial Settings (Texas)

The `Financial Settings` card has three fields:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Tax Rate (%) | The sales tax rate applied to taxable services. Example: `8.25` for Texas. |
| Processing Rate (%) | The percentage portion of your card-processing fee. Example: `2.9` for standard Stripe. |
| Processing Flat Fee ($) | The per-transaction flat fee. Example: `0.30` for standard Stripe. |

Together, Processing Rate and Flat Fee drive the **Pass-Through gross-up**, (fee + flat) ÷ (1 − rate), on ancillary services that have Pass-Through turned on. The Monthly Reconciliation report does not use them: it uses the actual Stripe fee when it has one, and otherwise estimates 2.9% + $0.30. Note that the invoice PDF carries no tax line.

## Duration Settings

1nspecT uses a linear formula to work out how long each inspection takes on-site, which drives booking-slot length and calendar blocking. The formula:

```text
duration = base_duration + (sqft − base_sqft) × slope / 1000
```

Result is rounded up to the nearest 30 minutes, with a 60-minute absolute floor.

| Field | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base Duration (min) | How long the inspection takes at the base sqft | `150` (two and a half hours) |
| Base Sq Ft (anchor) | The sqft at which the Base Duration applies | `1500` |
| Slope (min per 1000 sqft) | Extra minutes added per 1,000 sqft above the base | `60` |
| Absolute Floor (min) | The minimum any booking slot can be | `60` |

These numbers apply to every primary service by default. Each service in the registry can override any of them under its own Duration section.

## Schedule Metadata

`Version` and `Effective Date` label the pricing snapshot you are editing. Bump these when you make a material pricing change; past orders retain the schedule they were booked under.

## Save

**Save Fees & Surcharges** at the top right or the bottom of the page commits Settings changes along with changes on the Ancillary, Packages, Discounts and Surcharges tabs.
