1nspecT gives you a public booking page where clients enter the property address, pick services, see the live price, and book an inspection in one flow. You can share the link directly, text it from the field, or embed it on your website so clients book without ever leaving your site.

## Your booking link

Every subscriber gets a public booking URL under `app.1nspect.app/book/`. The slug is set from your Company Info (see [Company profile](/1nspect/getting-started/company-profile)).

### Where to copy it

Open **Business Ops › Company Info**. The booking link is shown with a copy action.

Share the link directly in email signatures, business cards, agent-partner materials, and SMS. Clients open the link and see your branded booking page.

## Embed the booking form on your website

For subscribers who want booking right on their company website, 1nspecT provides a copy-paste iframe snippet.

### Where to copy the embed snippet

Open **Business Ops › Company Info** and find the **Public Booking Form** section. It provides an iframe of your booking page (`/book/{slug}`). Paste it into any HTML page on your site.

<Warning>
  The **Embed Your Wizard** snippet on Settings › Company points at `/quote?embed=true`. That path has no public route, so the iframe shows the login page. Use the Public Booking Form snippet in Business Ops › Company Info instead.
</Warning>

### Sizing

The default height is 800px. You can adjust `height` to fit your layout. The iframe is responsive on `width="100%"`.

## What clients see

The public booking page walks a client through:

1. Property address (with autocomplete)
2. Property questions (bed and bath, year built, square footage)
3. Service selection (primary services and ancillaries from your Pricing Manager)
4. The live price (from your pricing rules)
5. Client details and a date and time (checked against inspector Working Schedules and calendars)
6. Submission, which creates a lead (pending review) and fires the Quote Submitted trigger. No order is created and no agreement is sent until your office converts the lead.

The page uses your logo, your colors and your branding (set in [Branding and white-label](/1nspect/getting-started/branding)).

## Internal booking (admin-only)

If you want a booking link that only your admins can submit against (for over-the-phone bookings), create orders directly in the Order Creation Wizard (**Inspections** top nav). That path is covered in detail later and does not go through the public booking page.
