Your Standard of Practice (SoP) is the rulebook your templates follow. It defines the rating scale (for example TREC's I / NI / NP / D), the layout family your reports are built on, and the AI narrative language your inspections use. Pick the right SoP once and every template, every narrative draft, and every published report lines up with it.

<Info>
  You chose your primary SoP during signup. The matching template and libraries were seeded on your account. You can view every SoP that ships with the platform, and (on Professional and Team plans) clone one to customize it.
</Info>

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **SoP Manager** (fourth top-nav item). The page header reads **Standards of Practice**.

The page lists every SoP available to you: the platform standards (TREC 7-6, InterNACHI, Phase 1 Pre-Pour, Phase 2 Pre-Drywall) and any custom SoPs you have cloned.

## View a rating scale

On any SoP card, click **View Ratings** to open the Rating Scale Viewer. This shows every rating value that SoP uses, its icon, its meaning, and whether it counts as a deficiency. Great for understanding what a non-standard SoP (say an InterNACHI commercial variant) actually prescribes.

## Clone to customize

Primary ratings on TREC and InterNACHI are locked, including on clones, to keep you compliant with the official standard. A clone lets you edit the reinspection ratings (with **Edit Ratings**) and inherits narrative rules live from the base. Phase SoPs are not locked.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Clone to Customize">
    On the SoP card, click **Clone to Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your clone">
    A dialog opens titled with the original SoP's name. The default clone name appends `(Custom)` to the original name. Change it to something meaningful, like `Acme TREC 7-6`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clone">
    Click **Clone**. The custom SoP appears on the Standards of Practice list. Use **Edit Ratings** to change what is editable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  There is no screen to re-point an existing template to a different SoP. The only SoP picker is in **Initialize from SoP** on the Templates page, so to use your clone, initialize a new template from it.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Custom SoP cloning is a Professional and Team feature (Solo 0, Professional 1, Team 3 custom SoPs). If your plan does not include customization, the Standards of Practice page shows a note saying you can view and use every standard provided, but not customize. Open Business Ops › Billing to see your plan.
</Note>

## Delete a clone

Click the delete icon on a custom SoP card. Platform SoPs cannot be deleted.
