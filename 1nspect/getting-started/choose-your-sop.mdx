Your Standard of Practice (SoP) is the rulebook your templates follow. It defines the rating scale (for example TREC's I / NI / NP / D), the layout family your reports are built on, and the AI narrative language your inspections use. Pick the right SoP once and every template, every narrative draft, and every published report lines up with it.

<Info>
You chose your primary SoP during signup. The matching template and libraries were seeded on your account. You can view every SoP that ships with the platform, and (on paid plans) clone one to customize its rating scale.
</Info>

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **SoP Manager** (fourth top-nav item). The page header reads **Standards of Practice**.

The page lists every SoP available to you: the platform standards (TREC 7-6, InterNACHI, Phase 1 Pre-Pour, Phase 2 Pre-Drywall) and any custom SoPs you have cloned.

## View a rating scale

On any SoP card, click **View Ratings** to open the Rating Scale Viewer. This shows every rating value that SoP uses, its icon, its meaning, and whether it counts as a deficiency. Great for understanding what a non-standard SoP (say an InterNACHI commercial variant) actually prescribes.

## Clone to customize

Platform SoPs (TREC, InterNACHI) are locked for editing to keep you compliant with the official standard. If you need to adjust ratings for how your company works, clone the SoP into your own copy and edit the clone.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Clone to Customize">
On the SoP card, click **Clone to Customize**.
</Step>
<Step title="Name your clone">
A dialog opens titled with the original SoP's name. The default clone name appends `(Custom)` to the original name. Change it to something meaningful, like `Acme TREC 7-6`.
</Step>
<Step title="Clone">
Click **Clone**. The custom SoP appears on the Standards of Practice list, editable.
</Step>
<Step title="Point your template at the clone">
Open the template that should use the custom SoP (**Templates** top-nav item), and set its SoP to the clone. Future inspections against that template will follow your customized ratings.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Custom SoP cloning is a paid-plan feature. If your plan does not include customization, the Standards of Practice page shows a note saying you can view and use every standard provided, but not customize. Open [Billing](/1nspect/features/it-tokens) to see your plan.
</Note>

## Delete a clone

Click the delete icon on a custom SoP card. Platform SoPs cannot be deleted.
