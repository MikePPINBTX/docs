Almost every new subscriber hits one of these on their first test run. None of them are complicated to fix, but they are easy to miss. Scan this page before you start your dry run so you recognize the symptom when you see it.

## The inspection doesn't sync to the phone

**Symptom:** You created the test order on the web but the inspector cannot find it in Assigned Inspections on mobile.

**The fix:** On the Inspection Details screen, confirm these four fields are set:

- **Inspector** (assigned)
- **Template**
- **Narrative Collection**
- **Caption Library**

Any of the four missing will block the sync. Set them, save, and the inspection will appear on the phone within a few seconds.

## The caption dropdown is empty on mobile

**Symptom:** The inspector gets to the Capture screen but the caption autocomplete has no suggestions.

**The fix:** The caption library is not associated with the template. Open **Narrative Management › Narrative Library**, find your caption collection, and use the template-picker action to associate it with your template. See [Attach caption and narrative libraries](/1nspect/getting-started/attach-libraries).

## A surcharge isn't charging

**Symptom:** You set up a mileage or age-of-home surcharge but the quote and the order don't include it.

**The fix:** The surcharge's **Active** toggle is off. Open **Business Ops › Pricing › Surcharges**, confirm Active is on for the surcharge, and click **Save Fees & Surcharges**.

## An inspector doesn't appear on the booking form

**Symptom:** You added an inspector but they are not listed on the public booking page or when scheduling internally.

**The fix:** The **Inspector** toggle on their user profile is off. Open **Settings › Users**, edit the inspector, and turn on **Inspector**.

## An automated email isn't firing

**Symptom:** You expected a booking confirmation, agreement sent, payment receipt or review request, and it never arrived.

**The fix 1:** The workflow step is not enabled. Open **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow**, open the step, and turn on **Enabled**.

**The fix 2:** For reminders ("2 days before the inspection"), the delay must be anchored to the inspection date, not to the trigger time. Open the workflow step and switch the delay anchor if needed.

**The fix 3:** The template is empty or has a typo in a required field. Open the matching template under **Email Templates** and preview with sample data.

## The agreement renders a tag literally

**Symptom:** The signed PDF shows `$ClientNames` or `[[sig|req|signer1]]` as plain text instead of filling in.

**The fix:** The agreement text contains a typo'd tag the backend doesn't recognize. For contracts, 1nspecT normally blocks a send on an unrecognized `$Tag`, so this almost always means a signature block tag (double-bracket syntax) that got slightly off. Open the agreement in **Business Ops › Contracts**, delete the broken tag, and reinsert it from the **Insert Tag** picker. See [Variable tags reference](/1nspect/getting-started/variable-tags-reference) for valid tag names.

## I can't start the inspection on the phone

**Symptom:** The inspector taps Start Inspection and gets blocked.

**The fix:** The agreement is unsigned. 1nspecT blocks the inspection from starting on mobile while the agreement for that order is unsigned. Open the client portal link, sign the agreement, and retry.

## Clients see a day I had blocked on my calendar

**Symptom:** The booking page offers slots on a day the inspector blocked for a personal appointment.

**The fix:** Google Calendar is not connected for that inspector. Open **Settings › Users**, edit the inspector, and connect Google Calendar. See [Google Calendar connection](/1nspect/getting-started/google-calendar).

## The report delivered-email went out before the client could read the report

**Symptom:** The review-request email arrived while the report was still locked behind payment.

**The fix:** Your workflow step is on the wrong trigger. Review-request emails should fire on **Report Delivered — after payment unlock**, not on **Report Published**. Open **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow**, change the trigger, and save.

## I can't find the Billing page

**Symptom:** You looked under Business Ops for Billing and did not find it.

**The fix:** Billing lives under **Settings**, not Business Ops. Go directly to `/settings/billing`, or open **Settings** and look for the Billing section.

## The AI stopped working on the phone

**Symptom:** AI Generate shows an "insufficient AI credits" alert.

**The fix:** Your company's AI credit balance is at zero. The alert links to the Billing page. Open **Settings › Billing** and buy a credit pack. The inspector can keep capturing photos, ratings and field notes without the AI; publish and library search also still work without credits. See [AI Credits](/1nspect/features/it-tokens).

## Photos aren't saving to the inspector's camera roll

**Symptom:** You expected inspection photos to appear in the inspector's phone photo library and they are not there.

**The fix:** The company setting is off by default. Open **Business Ops › Company Info**, find **Save Inspection Photos to Inspectors' Camera Rolls**, and turn it on.

## Still stuck

If you worked through the dry run and something still isn't right, email `support@mymavtech.com` with the test order number and a description of what you saw. Your onboarding specialist can also walk through any step with you live.
