Your company profile is the foundation that the rest of 1nspecT builds on. The name you set here goes on every report, email, agreement and portal page the client sees. Set it up before you touch any other section.

<Info>
**Required** to run your first inspection: Company name.
</Info>

## Where to find it

In the web admin top bar, open **Business Ops**. You land on the **Company Info** tab (the first of seven tabs across the top of the Business Operations page). The page header reads **Business Operations**.

## Fields to set

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Company name | Required. Appears on reports, emails, agreements, portals, invoices. Placeholder: `Your Company Name`. |
| Office address | Street address, City, State, ZIP. Used to calculate mileage surcharges when you charge for travel. |
| Support email | Appears on client-facing notifications and portal pages. Placeholder: `support@yourcompany.com`. |

<Tip>
Use a monitored inbox for the support email. Clients reply to automated emails from this address when they have questions, so make sure someone sees them.
</Tip>

## Save

At the bottom of the Company Info tab, click **Save Company Info**. Save confirmation appears inline.
