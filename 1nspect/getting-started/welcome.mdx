Welcome aboard. This section walks you through setting up 1nspecT end to end: identity and branding, services and pricing, your report template and libraries, the client-facing paperwork and emails, and a dry-run before your first real inspection. Items marked **Required** are what you need to run that first inspection. Everything else you can add now or come back to later.

<Info>
An onboarding specialist from MAVTech runs this with you on your first call. The essentials usually take 1 to 2 hours together. Fine-tuning (custom equipment fields, polished email templates, multi-phase contract bundles) is a quick follow-up session.
</Info>

## The five stages

You will set up in this order, because each stage builds on the one before it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Business identity">
Company profile, branding (logo, colors, white-label), and your team of inspectors and admins. See [Company profile](/1nspect/getting-started/company-profile), [Branding](/1nspect/getting-started/branding), and [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team).
</Step>
<Step title="Services and pricing">
Your primary and ancillary services, packages, discounts, surcharges, rates, taxes and processing fees. Done before contracts and online booking, because everything downstream refers to your services.
</Step>
<Step title="Reports">
Your inspection standard, your report template, the smart equipment fields that do math for you, your cover page, and your caption and narrative libraries.
</Step>
<Step title="Client paperwork and comms">
Your service agreement, your automated emails and workflows, your online booking link, Google Calendar sync and review requests.
</Step>
<Step title="Go live">
One full practice run with your specialist before your first real client.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How much time this takes

| Phase | Rough time |
| --- | --- |
| Essentials with your specialist (minimum to run inspection #1) | 1 to 2 hours |
| Custom equipment fields, nice-to-have email templates, multi-phase contracts | A follow-up session |
| Caption and narrative libraries | Ongoing (you add to them as you work) |

## How 1nspecT is organized

All of the setup you do here lives in the web admin at `app.1nspect.app`. Three parts of the platform share the same data in real time: the web admin (where you set everything up and manage orders and reports), the mobile inspector app (where field capture happens), and the client-facing web pages (booking, quote, portal, report). Clients and agents never need an app or an account. See [How 1nspecT works](/1nspect/getting-started/how-1nspect-works) for the big picture.

## When you get stuck

Your onboarding specialist is your first stop while you set up. Once you are live, email `support@mymavtech.com` or search this help site. The in-app Help button in the top-right of the web admin opens this site.
