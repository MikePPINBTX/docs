Your team of inspectors and admins is managed on the Business Ops page under the Users tab. The Add Team Member dialog captures what each user needs to work in the field: licenses and certifications, role, working hours, calendar connection, TREC sponsor information and mileage origin.

<Tip>
Before adding your team, click **License Types** on the Users tab and pick the licenses your company uses (TREC, InterNACHI, ASHI, mold, NAWT, State, or your own, such as a commercial license). Every team member's form then offers the same list with the same names. See [Licenses and certifications](/1nspect/features/inspector-licenses).
</Tip>

<Info>
**Required** to run your first inspection: at least one user with the **Inspector** toggle turned on. The toggle controls who appears in the inspector roster on your booking form and when creating orders. It is on by default for new users.
</Info>

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Business Ops**, then click the **Users** tab. The section header reads **User Management**. The Users tab is visible only to admins and owners.

## Add a team member

Click **Add Team Member**. The dialog is titled **Add Team Member** (it switches to **Edit Team Member** when you edit an existing user). The submit button reads **Create Team Member** for a new user and **Update Team Member** when editing. Fill in:

### Account

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Display Name | Required. The name shown in the UI and on reports. |
| Email | Required. The address the user signs in with. Immutable after creation. |
| Temporary Password | Required for a new user, at least 6 characters. Share it with the user; they can change it with **Forgot Password** on the login screen. An invite email with a password-setup link is also sent. |
| Role | Inspector or Admin. The owner role cannot be assigned. |

### Licenses & Certifications

One row for each license type your company offers. Fill in the licenses this person holds and leave the others blank.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Number | The license or certification number. |
| Issuer | Optional. Pre-filled from the license type. |
| Expiry date | Optional. Shows **Expires soon** / **Expired** on the Users list and a renewal notice on the Dashboard. |
| Primary | The default **License #** on reports, contracts and emails. Defaults to the first license entered. |
| Show on reports | On by default. Includes the license in the "all licenses" list (`$InspectorLicenses`). |

A license type can be set to apply to certain inspection types, such as a commercial license for Commercial inspections. That license then prints instead of the primary on those inspections. See [Licenses and certifications](/1nspect/features/inspector-licenses).

### Toggles

- **Inspector**: on by default. Leave it on for anyone who conducts inspections and should appear on your booking form and the order inspector roster; turn it off for office-only admins.
- The second toggle is **mobile publishing**: a per-user opt-out that matters only when the company switch **Allow Publishing Reports from Mobile** (Business Ops › Company Info, off by default) is on.

### Google Calendar

The Google Calendar section appears only when you are **editing** an existing user, so create the user first and then edit. Click connect and you are redirected to Google in the same tab. Once connected, booking availability reads every calendar the user owns or can write to, and new inspections are added to their primary calendar automatically.

### Working Schedule

Set working hours per day of the week, with a **Timezone** dropdown (US timezones: Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska, Hawaii). Booking honors this Working Schedule.

### TREC Sponsor Information (Required for Texas)

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsor Name | The sponsoring company or individual. Placeholder: `Sponsoring Company or Individual`. |
| Sponsor License Number | The sponsor's TREC license number. Placeholder: `Sponsor's TREC License #`. |

### Mileage Calculation

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Home Address | The address mileage surcharges are calculated from first (the office address is the fallback). Placeholder: `123 Main St, Austin, TX 78701`. |

### Signature

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Signature Image URL | A URL to the inspector's signature image. It is saved on the user's profile but is not currently used on reports. Placeholder: `https://storage.googleapis.com/signatures/signature.png`. |

## Seats and your plan

Each plan includes a set number of seats:

- **Solo**: 1 seat
- **Professional**: 1 seat included, add more at a per-seat price
- **Team**: 3 seats included, add more at a per-seat price

Check your current seat count and buy additional seats on **Business Ops › Billing**. To remove a seat, first disable a user in Business Ops › Users, then remove the seat on Billing.

## Disabled users

The Users tab shows disabled users in a separate section below active ones. Disabling a user frees up a seat but keeps their history on past orders and reports intact.
