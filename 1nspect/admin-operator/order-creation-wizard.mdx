The Order Creation Wizard is where admins create orders. Open it from the **Create Inspection** button on the Inspections list or at `/inspections/new`. Despite the name, it is a **single long form**, not a five-step flow. You fill it top to bottom, and it ends with one of two buttons. Handles single-phase inspections and multi-phase new-construction orders.

## Where to find it

**Inspections** top-nav › **Create Inspection**. Or direct URL `/inspections/new`.

## What's on the form

Working top to bottom:

- **Clients.** Primary client (name, email, phone). Optional co-client. Optional buyer's agent and listing agent (search existing contacts or add inline).
- **Property.** Address with Google Places autocomplete. Year built, square footage, foundation type and so on. The **Property Baseline** is a button you click to pull site conditions and construction-era materials; it is not automatic. Risk questions depend on your SoP.
- **Services and detached structures.** Pick primary services (one for single-phase, multiple for a multi-phase new-construction bundle). Add ancillaries. Add detached structure rows. The live price updates as you build.
- **Scheduling.** Pick an inspector and a time slot (from that inspector's Working Schedule and Google Calendar). Mileage surcharge calculates from the inspector's home address, falling back to the office address. A per-phase schedule summary shows above the submit buttons.
- **Pricing and review.** For multi-phase bundles, assign ancillaries to specific phases.

## The two ending buttons

- **💾 Save Quote (Lead)** creates a lead the client can accept through a quote link. See [Quote page](/1nspect/features/quote-page).
- **✅ Confirm & Create Order** creates the order.

Above **Confirm & Create Order**, tick **No charge (comped)** to make every inspection on the order $0. They count as paid. See [No charge (comped)](/1nspect/workflows/managing-orders#no-charge-comped).

**Review & Send** is not a step on this form. It is the contract modal that opens after the order is created, where you review and send the agreements per your Communications workflow.

## Client already contracted externally

Tick **Client already contracted externally** when the client signed an agreement somewhere else, for example a booking you're moving over from another platform. 1nspecT then skips **only its own agreement send**. Everything else runs as usual:

- the booking confirmation, if you have one set up
- the inspection reminder and any reschedule notices
- payment, inspection completed and review request emails
- the report emails

Then fill in **Signed by** (required, the client's name), **Date signed** (required) and **Notes** (optional: the agreement date, or a link to the signed agreement, for example on Google Drive). There's no file upload. This records the agreement for your [Signed agreement policy](/1nspect/features/signed-agreement-policy), so the report isn't locked waiting for a signature.

The job shows on the inspector's phone and can be started without an agreement. On Inspection Details, the agreement line reads **Agreement handled outside 1nspect**.

<Note>
Orders booked with this box before November 4, 2026 keep the old behavior: automated emails stay off, except report and review-request emails. They show **Externally contracted (automation off)**.
</Note>

## Multi-phase orders

Selecting more than one primary service turns the order into a multi-phase bundle. Each phase has its own date, inspector and inspection record. (The **Bundle Agreement Group** field on primary services is saved but not used today.)

## Mark as Lost

**Mark as Lost** is on the quote's **Overview** tab, not in the wizard. Use it with a reason to record why the client didn't book.

## Firebase connection required

The wizard needs Firebase connectivity to load services, calendars and pricing. If Firebase is offline, the page shows a Firebase Connection Required error with troubleshooting notes.
