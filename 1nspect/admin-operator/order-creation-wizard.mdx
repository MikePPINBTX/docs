The Order Creation Wizard is where admins create orders. Opens from the Inspections list's add action or at `/inspections/new`. Walks through client details, property, services, scheduling and the Review & Send step. Handles single-phase inspections, multi-phase new-construction orders, and quote-to-order conversions.

## Where to find it

**Inspections** top-nav › the add order action. Or direct URL `/inspections/new`.

## The high-level steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clients">
    Primary client (name, email, phone). Optional co-client. Optional buyer's agent and listing agent (pulled from Contacts or added inline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Property">
    Address with Google Places autocomplete. Year built, square footage, foundation type, cladding, roof covering (auto-filled by Property Baseline, editable). Risk questions depending on your SoP (crawlspace, pool, well, septic). The Property Baseline panel shows Site Conditions and Construction-era materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Services and detached structures">
    Pick primary services (one for single-phase, multiple for a multi-phase new-construction bundle). Add ancillaries. Add detached structure rows for garage/workshop/etc. Live price updates as you build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scheduling">
    Pick an inspector and a time slot from that inspector's Google Calendar. Mileage surcharge calculates automatically from the inspector's home address. A per-phase schedule summary shows above the submit action.
  </Step>
  <Step title={"Review & Send"}>
    Final review of the whole order: clients, property, services, pricing, scheduling. For multi-phase bundles, assign ancillaries to specific phases here. Save as a quote (don't send yet), or create the order (sends the agreement per your Communications workflow).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-phase orders

Selecting more than one primary service turns the order into a multi-phase bundle. Each phase has its own date, inspector and inspection record. If the primary services share a **Bundle Agreement Group**, one agreement covers the whole bundle.

## Quote vs. order

Two outcomes at Review & Send:

- **Save as Quote** — creates a lead the client can accept via a texted or emailed quote link. See [Quote page](/1nspect/features/quote-page).
- **Create Order** — commits the booking, fires the Booking Confirmed trigger, sends the agreement.

## Mark Lost

On a quote, use **Mark Lost** with a reason to record why the client didn't book. Shows in the Agent Referral Report's close-rate calculation.

## Firebase connection required

The wizard needs Firebase connectivity to load services, calendars and pricing. If Firebase is offline, the page shows a Firebase Connection Required error with troubleshooting notes.
