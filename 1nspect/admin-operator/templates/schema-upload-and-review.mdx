If you already have an inspection form as a PDF, 1nspecT can extract a template schema from it with AI. Upload the PDF, the AI reads it into sections, subsections and fields, you review and edit, and the template is built. Saves hours vs. building a long template from scratch by hand.

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Templates** › pick the template to attach a schema to › the schema editor opens. From the editor, use the schema upload action to open the import flow. The upload route is `/templates/builder/{templateId}/schema`.

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the PDF">
    Drop in the PDF of your inspection form. 1nspecT accepts a form with any layout: TREC 7-6, InterNACHI, a state-specific form, or your own custom form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI extraction">
    The AI reads the PDF and extracts a schema: sections, subsections and fields. OCR handles scanned PDFs; text extraction handles born-digital PDFs. Cost: **3 AI credits** for OCR extraction, **free** for structured-text extraction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the extraction">
    The schema appears on-screen as a draft. Every section, subsection and field the AI found is listed. Expand each section to see its fields and verify the data types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit anything off">
    Rename sections, drop fields the AI picked up by mistake, add fields it missed, change Data Formats where needed. The usual schema-editor controls work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit">
    Save the schema to attach it to the template. The template becomes Schema Linked (the blue outlined badge on its card).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the AI can't extract cleanly

If the PDF is unusual, the AI's extraction may be incomplete. The screen shows a message like _"AI response did not contain a 'sections' array. Try re-uploading, or build the schema by hand with 'Add Section'."_

Two options:

- **Re-upload** the PDF (sometimes a clearer or slightly different PDF produces a better extraction)
- **Build the schema by hand** with the standard Add Section / Add Subsection / Add Field controls

## Cost

The schema extraction call is where the AI credits live:

| Operation | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| OCR-based extraction (scanned PDF) | 3 AI credits |
| Structured-text extraction (text-based PDF) | Free |

## What this is not

- Not a cover design extractor. The cover is designed separately in [Cover Designer](/1nspect/admin-operator/cover-designer).
- Not a layout extractor. The AI extracts the schema (what to capture), not how it prints. For layout, use [Visual Page Designer](/1nspect/admin-operator/templates/template-designer) or Guided Layout.
- Not a one-shot migration. Plan to review and adjust the extraction before you ship the template.
