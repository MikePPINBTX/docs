Communications Cockpit is the Business Ops tab where you build the automated emails clients receive from 1nspecT on your behalf. Two sub-tabs: Email Templates (what the emails say) and Workflow (when they fire and to whom).

## Where to find it

**Business Ops** top-nav › the **Communications** tab (fifth of seven tabs).

## Two sub-tabs

- **Email Templates** — list and editor for every email template
- **Workflow** — list and editor for every workflow step

Both covered in detail in the setup pages:

- [Communication templates](/1nspect/getting-started/communication-templates)
- [Communication workflows](/1nspect/getting-started/communication-workflows)
- [Variable tags reference](/1nspect/getting-started/variable-tags-reference)

## Email Templates sub-tab

Left panel: `Templates (N)` with a count. Click a template to open it in the editor. Click add to create a new one; the editor header reads **New Template** until saved.

Editor supports: template name, subject, rich-text body with **Insert Hyperlink** and **Insert Variable** dropdowns, and **Save Template**. There is no preview. Unknown tags are refused on save and dropped on send.

## Workflow sub-tab

Left panel: `Workflow Steps (N)` with a count. Click a step to edit; add to create a new one.

Each step has:

- Trigger (about 27 triggers plus Manual and Custom; see [Communication workflows](/1nspect/getting-started/communication-workflows) for the full list)
- Template (pick from Email Templates)
- Recipients (Primary Client, Secondary Client, Buyer's Agent, Listing Agent, Assigned Inspector, Subscriber (You)), plus **Also Send To** for extra addresses
- **Send Offset** plus an **Offset Anchor** (how long after the trigger, or relative to the inspection date)
- A service-type filter
- Enabled toggle (starter steps are seeded disabled)

Save with **Save Step**.
