Business Operations is the container for everything you set up to run the business: company identity and branding, your team, billing, pricing, payments, contracts, communications, quotes, and integrations. Open from the **Business Ops** top-nav. The page header reads **Business Operations**.

<Note>
  The separate Settings page is gone; everything it held now lives here. Old `/settings` links open **Company Info**, and `/settings/billing` still opens **Billing**. The palette icon in the header (tooltip **Company & Branding**) also opens Company Info.
</Note>

## The nine tabs

Across the top of the page, in this order:

| Tab | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| [**Company Info**](/1nspect/admin-operator/company-and-pricing/company-info) | Company name, account ID, booking slug, office address, support email, phone, inspection defaults, logo upload, review links, brand colors and preset themes, installed-app status-bar color, white-label, Dynamic Rating UI, mobile publishing, camera roll, Public Booking Form |
| [**Users**](/1nspect/admin-operator/users) | User Management: add team members, roles, working hours, Google Calendar connections. Admins and owners only. |
| **Billing** | Your plan, AI credit balance and credit purchases. Admins and owners only. See [AI Credits](/1nspect/features/it-tokens). |
| [**Pricing**](/1nspect/admin-operator/company-and-pricing/pricing-manager) | Pricing Manager with 6 sub-tabs: Primary Services, Ancillary Services, Packages, Discounts, Surcharges, Settings |
| **Payments** | [Stripe Connect setup](/1nspect/admin-operator/payments-and-contracts) and [Manual Payment Options](/1nspect/features/manual-payments) |
| [**Contracts**](/1nspect/admin-operator/contracts-cockpit) | Service agreements, dynamic tags, and the [Signed agreement policy](/1nspect/features/signed-agreement-policy). Test Send emails the agreement PDFs only to you. |
| [**Communications**](/1nspect/admin-operator/comms-cockpit) | Email Templates and Workflow sub-tabs |
| **Quotes** | Quote-specific settings |
| [**Integrations**](/1nspect/features/integrations-overview) | Haven integration (plus other integrations that have per-tenant configuration) |

Inspectors see the same page without the Users and Billing tabs.

## What is not here

- **Templates, Narrative Management, SoP Manager, Cover Designer, Reports** — each has its own top-nav item
