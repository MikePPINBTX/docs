Business Operations is the container for everything you set up to run the business: company identity, pricing, payments, contracts, communications, quotes, and integrations. Open from the **Business Ops** top-nav. The page header reads **Business Operations**.

## The seven tabs

Across the top of the page:

| Tab | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| [**Company Info**](/1nspect/admin-operator/company-and-pricing/company-info) | Company name, phone, address, support email, brand colors, logo URL, white-label, inspection defaults, mobile publishing, camera roll, booking slug, review links, Public Booking Form |
| [**Pricing**](/1nspect/admin-operator/company-and-pricing/pricing-manager) | Pricing Manager with 6 sub-tabs: Primary Services, Ancillary Services, Packages, Discounts, Surcharges, Settings |
| **Payments** | [Stripe Connect setup](/1nspect/admin-operator/payments-and-contracts) and [Manual Payment Options](/1nspect/features/manual-payments) |
| [**Contracts**](/1nspect/admin-operator/contracts-cockpit) | Service agreements, dynamic tags (Test Send sends a real email) |
| [**Communications**](/1nspect/admin-operator/comms-cockpit) | Email Templates and Workflow sub-tabs |
| **Quotes** | Quote-specific settings |
| [**Integrations**](/1nspect/features/integrations-overview) | Haven integration (plus other integrations that have per-tenant configuration) |

## What is not here

- **Billing** — lives at `/settings/billing` under the **Settings** top-nav, not under Business Ops
- **Users** — lives in **Settings › Users**, not under Business Ops
- **Branding (color scheme, white-label, embed)** — these also exist in **Business Ops › Company Info** (brand colors, white-label, and the working booking embed under Public Booking Form), as well as in **Settings** (Company tab). The Settings embed snippet is broken; use the Business Ops one.
- **Templates, Narrative Management, SoP Manager, Cover Designer, Reports** — each has its own top-nav item
