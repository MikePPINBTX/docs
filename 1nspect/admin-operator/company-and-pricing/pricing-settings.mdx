Pricing Settings is the Settings tab inside Pricing Manager. It carries account-wide pricing values the rest of the pricing system reads from: sales tax rate, payment processing rate and flat fee, the inspection-duration formula and schedule metadata.

## Where to find it

**Business Ops** top-nav › **Pricing** tab › **Settings** sub-tab (sixth of six).

## Covered in detail

[Rates, taxes and processing fees](/1nspect/getting-started/rates-taxes-fees) is the full walk-through:

- Financial Settings (Texas) — Tax Rate, Processing Rate, Processing Flat Fee
- Duration Settings — Base Duration, Base Sq Ft, Slope, Absolute Floor (and the formula)
- Schedule Metadata — Version, Effective Date

## Save

**Save Fees & Surcharges** at the top right (and at the bottom of the page) commits Settings changes along with changes on the Ancillary Services, Packages, Discounts and Surcharges tabs.
