Pricing Manager is where you define what you sell and how it costs. Six tabs across the top: Primary Services, Ancillary Services, Packages, Discounts, Surcharges, Settings. The page is covered in detail in the Start Here pricing walk-through.

## Where to find it

**Business Ops** top-nav › **Pricing** tab. Page header: **Pricing Manager**. Also at `/pricing`.
