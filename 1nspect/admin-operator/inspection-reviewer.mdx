The Report Workspace is the per-inspection review and publish surface. Open any inspection from Inspection Details, then open its **Reports** tab to land here. Two top-level tabs across the page, every finding reviewable inline, AI tools above and per-finding, and the Publish and Deliver actions at the top.

## Where to find it

**Inspections** top-nav › order › the child inspection › **Reports** tab.

## The two tabs

- **Findings** — every finding on the inspection grouped by section and subsection, with ratings, captions, narratives, photos, field notes, and the Narrative Review Panel
- **Legal Preamble** — the SoP-required legal preamble that prints on the report

Everything else you touch (AI Draft, Polish, pre-publish checks, Publish, Deliver) is on the Findings tab or in the top action bar.

## Top action bar

- **AI Draft & Polish** — drafts every eligible finding and runs Polish Report in one trip; the Draft portion is a flat 50 credits per report, and Polish Report adds 10 more
- **Spell Check** — fixes spelling and dictation stutters report-wide (5 credits per report)
- **Preview PDF** — generate the PDF without locking the inspection
- **Preview client view** — open the online report in a new tab as the client will see it once it's published. Nothing is published or shared
- **Publish Report** — lock the inspection, write the final PDF, generate the client and agent links
- **Republish vN** — after the first publish, the Publish button becomes **Republish v2**, **Republish v3**, etc.
- **Deliver** — email the report to the client or agent (opens the Deliver dialog)

## Findings tab

Each finding is editable inline:

- Rating (from your SoP: TREC shows I / NI / NP / D)
- Caption
- Narrative (edit, **AI Polish**, re-draft with AI Generate)
- Field note (private; feeds AI drafting)
- Photos and video (add, replace, reorder, move between sections, link)
- Reference snippet (if attached)
- Summary flag

## Multiple units

Sections that can hold several pieces of the same equipment (Heating Equipment, Cooling Equipment, water heaters) show a column per unit on their **Technical Specifications** card, once the section has two or more units.

- **+ Add Unit** (next to the card title) adds a unit. Adding a unit to Heating also adds it to the matching Cooling section, with the same labels, and the other way round. That only happens when the other section's units run up to the number just below: adding Cooling Unit 3 adds Heating Unit 3 if Heating has Units 1–2, but not if Heating has only Unit 1, since unit numbers never skip. If the other section already has that unit, it only picks up labels it doesn't have yet.
- **✎ Edit** (in a unit's column header) opens a dialog with **Area served** and **Location** for that unit. Clicking a unit's **Area Served** or **Location** cell opens the same dialog; both rows always show, even when empty. The table updates as soon as you save. The change applies to this section only. The matching Heating or Cooling section picks up the labels only where it has none yet.
- **Remove Unit N** (red, in the Edit dialog) appears only for the section's last unit, never Unit 1. After you confirm, it clears that unit's values in this section only.

Heating and Cooling keep separate unit lists, so a house with one wall heater and three window units shows one heating column and three cooling columns. Units are no longer copied to **Duct Systems, Chases, and Vents** or **Other**.

On the PDF and the published report, Heating and Cooling each print their own unit columns, with their own **Location** and **Area Served** rows. Inspections done earlier look the same until someone edits a section's units.

## Narrative Review Panel

The panel evaluates each narrative against the SoP's writing rules and flags violations: unsupported numeric claims, overclaimed causation, dropped hedging, trade-specialist rule violations. Each flag links to the finding so you can act on it. See [Pre-publish checks](/1nspect/workflows/pre-publish-checks).

## Pre-publish checks

Before Publish will accept:

- Every required SoP item is resolved
- Every finding has a narrative, or has been explicitly signed off as Publish without narrative
- Required ratings and area comments are complete

If anything's missing, Publish refuses with a clear message naming the blockers. The "Cannot publish - N finding(s) have no narrative" message gives you the two paths to resolve: Draft or Publish without narrative.

## Publishing

Click Publish. What happens:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lock the inspection">
    Immutable from this point without a Republish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the final PDF">
    Written to secure cloud storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate links">
    Client's permanent report link and agent's view are generated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the version">
    A report version is saved with the time and version number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fire the workflow trigger">
    Report Published (already paid) or Report Published (awaiting payment), depending on order state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deliver

Separate action from Publish. Opens the Deliver dialog with role (Client / Buyer's Agent; Agent is disabled when the order has no agent email), email, name, custom message, and a preview of every delivery email that will fire. See [Publishing and delivery](/1nspect/workflows/publishing-and-delivery).

## Republish

If you need to change a published report, Republish creates a new version. The permanent URL stays the same, and each publish saves its own version record.
