Set up how you accept money from clients. 1nspecT supports two paths: card payments through Stripe Connect that land in your own bank account, and manual payments where you share your Venmo, Zelle or Cash App handles with clients. Most firms turn both on.

## Where to find it

**Business Ops** top-nav › the **Payments** tab (third of seven tabs).

## Stripe Connect

The first section on the Payments page. Header reads **Stripe Connect**.

### What it does

Stripe Connect routes card payments straight into your own Stripe account (not through 1nspecT). Money moves from the client's card to your Stripe account, and from Stripe to your bank on the standard Stripe payout schedule. 1nspecT never holds your money.

### Connecting

Click **Connect Stripe Account**. The flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Stripe (or sign up)">
    1nspecT redirects to Stripe's hosted onboarding. If you already have a Stripe account for your business, sign in with it; otherwise create a new one through the hosted flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stripe's business verification">
    Stripe asks for your business details (legal entity, address, EIN or SSN, bank account for payouts). This is Stripe's standard Connect onboarding; 1nspecT does not see or store the sensitive data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to 1nspecT">
    Stripe redirects back to 1nspecT. The Payments page shows the Connected status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Processing rate

Stripe charges its standard processing rate on every transaction. 1nspecT does not take a cut. Set your Processing Rate (%) and Processing Flat Fee (\$) in **Business Ops › Pricing › Settings** so your fee is captured in the Monthly Reconciliation report.

### What you can do once connected

- Clients pay by card from the client portal
- Payment auto-unlocks the report when payment lands (if payment-locking is on)
- Payment Received workflow triggers fire
- Stripe Payouts and Monthly Reconciliation reports populate with real deposit data
- Refunds through 1nspecT reach Stripe

## Manual Payment Options

The second section on the Payments page. Header reads **Manual Payment Options**.

### What it does

Shows your Venmo, Zelle and Cash App handles to clients in their client portal. Clients pay you directly through their app of choice; you then mark the order paid in the web admin.

### Fields

| Field | Placeholder | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Venmo | `yourhandle` | Your Venmo username (without the `@`) |
| Zelle | `email@example.com or 555-000-0000` | Email address or phone number registered with Zelle |
| Cash App | `yourcashtag` | Your Cash App cashtag (without the `$`) |

Leave a field blank to hide that payment method from the client portal. Only configured methods show up.

### Marking an order paid

When a manual payment comes in:

1. Open the order on the Inspections list
2. Click **Mark as Paid** on the Inspection Details page
3. The Mark Paid modal captures amount, date, and an optional note
4. Save — the order is marked paid

The Payment Received trigger fires, the report link unlocks (if payment-locking is on), and the Monthly Reconciliation report picks up the payment.

See [Manual payments](/1nspect/features/manual-payments) for the client-side experience and [Managing orders](/1nspect/workflows/managing-orders) for the Mark as Paid flow.

## Payment-locked reports

Every published report is locked until the inspection is marked paid; both the client and the agent link return a Payment Required page. There is no setting to disable this. See [Payment-locked reports](/1nspect/features/payment-locked-reports).
