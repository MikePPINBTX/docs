User management is where you add and manage every team member on your account: inspectors, admins, owners. Lives under the Settings page as the Users tab.

## Where to find it

**Settings** top-nav (last of ten items) › the **Users** tab (second of two tabs: Company and Users). The page header inside the Users tab reads **User Management**.

## The user list

The tab shows two sections:

- **Active users** — current team members
- **Disabled users** — team members who have been disabled; shown below Active users in a separate section

Each row shows: display name, email, role chip, connected calendar status.

## Add Team Member

Top of the list: **Add Team Member** button. Opens the Add Team Member dialog (same dialog switches to **Edit Team Member** when you click into an existing user).

Fields covered in [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team):

- Display Name (required)
- Email (required; immutable after creation)
- Temporary Password (required for new users; minimum 6 characters)
- License Number
- Role
- Inspector toggle
- Google Calendar (connect per user)
- Working Schedule (per-day hours + Timezone from a US dropdown)
- TREC Sponsor Information (Sponsor Name, Sponsor License Number — required for Texas)
- Mileage Calculation (Home Address)
- Signature Image URL

## Disabling vs. deleting

- **Disable** moves the user to the Disabled section and frees their seat for a new user. Past history on their orders stays intact.
- **Delete** is not supported; disabling is the final state. This preserves the audit trail of inspections they completed.

## Seats

Each plan includes a set number of seats. Open **Settings › Billing** to see your current seat count and buy additional seats. To remove a paid seat, first disable the user here, then remove the seat on Billing.

## Roles

- **Owner** — full access, including Billing and dangerous actions
- **Admin** — can access everything except Billing and some owner-only settings
- **Inspector** — can access their own assigned inspections on mobile; limited web access

Fine-grained role gating is covered in each feature's doc where it applies.
