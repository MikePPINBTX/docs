User management is where you add and manage every team member on your account: inspectors, admins, owners. Lives under Business Ops as the Users tab.

## Where to find it

**Business Ops** top-nav › the **Users** tab (second of nine tabs, after Company Info). Only admins and owners see this tab. The page header inside the Users tab reads **User Management**.

## The user list

The tab shows two sections:

- **Active users** — current team members
- **Disabled users** — team members who have been disabled; shown below Active users in a separate section

Each row shows: display name, email, role chip, connected calendar status.

## Add Team Member

Top of the list: **Add Team Member** button. Opens the Add Team Member dialog (same dialog switches to **Edit Team Member** when you click into an existing user).

Fields covered in [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team):

- Display Name (required)
- Email (required; immutable after creation)
- Password (new users receive an **invite email** with a password-setup link; they set their own password)
- License Number
- Role
- Inspector toggle
- Mobile publishing option: a per-user opt-out that applies only when the company switch **Allow Publishing Reports from Mobile** (Business Ops › Company Info) is on
- Google Calendar (connect per user; the connect button appears only when editing an existing user)
- Working Schedule (per-day hours + Timezone from a US dropdown)
- TREC Sponsor Information (Sponsor Name, Sponsor License Number — required for Texas)
- Mileage Calculation (Home Address)
- Signature Image URL

## Disabling vs. deleting

- **Disable** moves the user to the Disabled section and frees their seat for a new user. Past history on their orders stays intact.
- **Delete** is not supported; disabling is the final state. This preserves the audit trail of inspections they completed.

## Seats

Each plan includes a set number of seats. Open **Business Ops › Billing** to see your current seat count and buy additional seats. To remove a paid seat, first disable the user here, then remove the seat on Billing.

## Roles

You can assign two roles: **Inspector** and **Admin**. The **Owner** role cannot be assigned to a new user.

- **Admin** — can access everything except Billing and some owner-only settings
- **Inspector** — can access their own assigned inspections on mobile; limited web access

The **Inspector** toggle controls whether the person appears on the booking roster. It does not affect whether jobs show on mobile.

Fine-grained role gating is covered in each feature's doc where it applies.
