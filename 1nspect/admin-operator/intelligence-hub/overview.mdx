The Intelligence & Library Hub is where all of the text your reports draw on lives: your reusable narratives and captions, the supplemental PDFs that attach by rule, your company's writing voice, your reusable pick lists, and your curated reference snippets. One page, five tabs.

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Narrative Management** (fifth top-nav item). The page header reads **Intelligence & Library Hub**.

## The five tabs

| # | Tab | What's inside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Narrative Library** | Your caption and narrative collections. Associate collections with templates. Import from XML. |
| 2 | **Supplemental Documents** | PDFs that attach to published reports automatically by rule (year built, foundation, service, property type). |
| 3 | **AI Persona (Voice DNA)** | Upload sample reports so the AI learns your company's writing voice. Owners and admins only. |
| 4 | **Option Lists** | Reusable pick lists that feed template Select fields across every template. |
| 5 | **Reference Snippets** | Short curated reference text (code articles, manufacturer caveats, safety advisories) inspectors can attach to findings on mobile. |

## What belongs where

- **A deficiency wording your inspectors reuse** → Narrative Library (narrative collection on the right template)
- **A caption your inspectors reuse** → Narrative Library (caption collection on the right template)
- **A disclosure PDF the report should attach for every slab home** → Supplemental Documents
- **Your company's writing voice** (so AI drafts sound like you wrote them) → AI Persona (Voice DNA)
- **A dropdown of foundation types that every template uses** → Option Lists
- **A code article your inspectors reference on specific findings** → Reference Snippets
- **An "additional information" block your SoP allows** → AIPI (per narrative collection, inside Narrative Library)
