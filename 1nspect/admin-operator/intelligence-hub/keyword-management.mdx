Keyword Management is where you define rules that classify findings by their text content. Each rule has a set of keywords; when a finding's caption or narrative contains those keywords, the rule's classification applies. Powers Smart Comments and other content-driven features.

## Where to find it

**Narrative Management** top-nav › **Intelligence & Library Hub** › the Keyword Management surface. Direct URL: `/keyword-management`. Also surfaced within the Smart Comments page.

## Rules

Each rule carries:

- **Keywords** — one or more words or phrases, comma-separated (`wdi, wdo, termite, wood-destroying`)
- **Classification** — what to tag matching findings with
- **Scope** — where the rule applies (all templates, specific template(s), specific section(s))

## Add a new rule

Click the add action. Enter keywords (comma-separated) and the classification to apply. Save.

## Edit a rule

Click a rule to open the editor. Change keywords or classification. Save.

## Common rule patterns

- **Termite / WDI classifier** — keywords: `wdi, wdo, termites, wood-destroying` → classifies the finding as pest-related
- **Electrical safety classifier** — keywords: `GFCI, double-tap, exposed wiring, ungrounded` → classifies as electrical safety
- **Moisture classifier** — keywords: `moisture, mold, mildew, water stain, condensation` → classifies as moisture

## Where classifications surface

- **Smart Comments** can use the classification to pick the right template comment
- **AI drafting** reads classifications as additional context
- **Reports** can be filtered or styled based on classifications
