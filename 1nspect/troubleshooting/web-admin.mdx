Fixes for the web admin issues most offices hit. Try the fix listed; if nothing works, email `support@mymavtech.com`.

## Firebase Connection Required error

Shows on the Order Creation Wizard and sometimes on Report Workspace. The browser can't reach Firebase. Try:

- Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R)
- Clear cookies for `app.1nspect.app` and sign in again
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
- Confirm your network isn't blocking Firebase domains

If it persists across browsers and networks, there may be a Firebase outage. Check with support.

## An automated email didn't fire

Multiple causes:

- **Workflow step disabled** — **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow**, open the step, flip Enabled on
- **Reminder time anchored wrong** — reminders ("2 days before") must be anchored to the inspection date, not to the trigger time
- **Template empty or has a typo** — open the matching template under Email Templates and preview with sample data
- **Trigger doesn't fire for this order type** — some triggers (Report Published - awaiting payment) only fire when payment-locking is on

Confirm the email fired by checking the order's Communications log and the Activity Log.

## Publish won't let me publish

The Publish action refuses with a message naming the blockers. Common ones:

- **"Cannot publish - N finding(s) have no narrative"** — draft them (AI Draft & Polish) or Publish without narrative per finding
- Missing required ratings on a subsection
- Missing required area comments
- SoP-required items marked incomplete

See [Pre-publish checks](/1nspect/workflows/pre-publish-checks) for the full gate.

## Agent magic-link email didn't arrive

- Check the agent's spam / junk folder
- The email the agent entered must match the one on at least one order
- Magic links are one-time; a second request invalidates the first
- Resend from the Realtor Portal page

## Haven report didn't send

- Confirm Haven is enabled at the tenant level (**Business Ops › Integrations › Haven card** showing Active)
- Confirm the per-order **Send to Haven on publish** toggle is on
- Confirm all four Haven credentials are filled in (API Key, Partner Name, Binder Template ID, Partner Email)
- Confirm the order has a client email

See [Haven integration](/1nspect/features/haven-integration).

## A surcharge isn't being added to orders

The surcharge's **Active** toggle is off. **Business Ops › Pricing › Surcharges**, enable, **Save Fees & Surcharges**.

## Report viewer shows "Report Not Found"

- Confirm the report has been published (not just previewed)
- The URL slug might have a typo
- The report may have been unpublished or the inspection deleted

## PDF download in the viewer is grayed out

In sample mode (de-identified sample reports), the PDF export is disabled. Message: _"Sample mode - PDF export is disabled for de-identified sample reports."_ Published real reports have the PDF enabled.
