Fixes for the web admin issues most offices hit. Try the fix listed; if nothing works, email `support@mymavtech.com`.

## Firebase Connection Required error

Shows on the Order Creation Wizard, the Narrative Library and Collection Detail. The browser can't reach Firebase. Try:

- Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R)
- Clear cookies for `app.1nspect.app` and sign in again
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
- Confirm your network isn't blocking Firebase domains

If it persists across browsers and networks, there may be a Firebase outage. Check with support.

## An automated email didn't fire

Multiple causes:

- **Workflow step disabled** — **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow**, open the step, flip Enabled on
- **Reminder time anchored wrong** — the Inspection Reminder needs **Send Offset** `-24h` (or similar) with **Offset Anchor** set to **From inspection date**, not from the trigger time. A same-day booking whose send time has already passed gets no reminder, and a reminder for an inspection that was moved or cancelled is skipped (its Communications row says why)
- **Template has an unknown tag** — unknown tags are refused on save and dropped on send; reinsert tags from the **Insert Variable** dropdown
- **Wrong trigger** — Report Delivered fires only on a Stripe payment after publish or the manual Deliver button; use the Review Request trigger for review emails
- **Email bounced** — see below

Confirm the email fired by checking the Communications tab on the child inspection and the Activity Log (a company-wide report of workflow and email events, the 200 most recent).

## Emails didn't arrive (bounce alert)

If an email bounces or fails to deliver, 1nspecT shows a **bounce banner** at the top of Inspection Details, lists per-recipient delivery status on the **Communications** tab, sends you a bounce notice email, and shows an **"emails didn't arrive"** alert on the Dashboard. Fix the recipient's address on Client & Realtor Info, then use **Fire Workflow Trigger** on the Communications tab to send again. There is no per-email Resend.

## Publish won't let me publish

The Publish action refuses with a message naming the blockers. Common ones:

- **Unresolved narrative flags** — these block Publish until you acknowledge them
- **"Cannot publish - N finding(s) have no narrative"** — draft them (AI Draft & Polish)
- Missing required ratings on a subsection
- SoP-required items marked incomplete

See [Pre-publish checks](/1nspect/workflows/pre-publish-checks) for the full gate.

## Agent magic-link email didn't arrive

- Check the agent's spam / junk folder
- Confirm the agent typed their email address correctly; the link goes to whatever address was entered
- Resend from the Realtor Portal page

## Haven report didn't send

- Confirm Haven is enabled at the tenant level (**Business Ops › Integrations › Haven card** showing Active)
- Confirm the per-order **Send to Haven on publish** toggle is on. Haven sends only when the report is **published and paid**, and skips reinspections and masters
- Confirm the Haven fields are filled in: **Haven API Key**, **Partner Name**, **Account Email**, **Default Binder Template ID** and **Confirmation CC Email**
- Confirm the order has a client email

See [Haven integration](/1nspect/features/haven-integration).

## A surcharge isn't being added to orders

The surcharge's **Active** toggle is off. **Business Ops › Pricing › Surcharges**, enable, **Save Fees & Surcharges**.

## Report viewer shows "Report Not Found"

- Confirm the report has been published (not just previewed)
- The URL slug might have a typo
- The report may have been unpublished or the inspection deleted

## PDF download in the viewer is grayed out

In sample mode (de-identified sample reports), the PDF export is disabled. Message: _"Sample mode - PDF export is disabled for de-identified sample reports."_ Published real reports have the PDF enabled.
