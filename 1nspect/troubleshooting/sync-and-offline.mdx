What's offline-capable, what queues until you reconnect, and the recovery paths for a sync that's stuck.

## Expected offline behavior

For the authoritative behavior reference, see [Offline and sync](/1nspect/inspector/offline-and-sync). The short version:

| What | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Photos, video, captions, ratings, field notes, caption-library autocomplete | Fully offline |
| Scan Label / Reading with typed `label: value` | Fully offline (parses on-device) |
| Scan Label / Reading photo-based | Queues, finishes on reconnect |
| Media uploads | Queue in the background, upload on connection |
| AI Generate, Vision Scan, Smart Search, Finish & Draft, Publish | Need a live connection |

## Reconnecting after being offline

As soon as the app has a connection, the sync engine starts pushing queued data. You can keep capturing while earlier data uploads.

Default is Wi-Fi only. For cellular uploads, flip **Upload Media over Cellular** in **Config › Sync Preferences**.

## Upload-fail banner

If an upload fails, a banner shows at the top of Review: _"N Media file(s) failed to upload, tap to manage (retry / export / delete)"_. Tap into it to open the Failed Uploads screen:

- **Retry** — queue for another upload attempt
- **Export** — save a copy to the camera roll (so the photo isn't lost)
- **Delete** — drop it from the inspection

Nothing is silently lost. Keep capturing. Don't fall back to your phone's camera roll.

## Captured but not synced for a long time

- Open the Review screen and tap **Sync to Cloud**. This forces a sync without drafting or publishing.
- If Sync to Cloud errors, check connectivity and retry.
- If a specific photo keeps failing in Failed Uploads, Export it to the camera roll and re-add it from scratch.

## I lost signal in the field; what happens to my work?

Nothing you've captured. Photos, captions, ratings, field notes and typed values are stored on the device. When you reconnect, the sync engine pushes everything up. The only AI operations that need the network at tap time (AI Generate, Vision Scan, Smart Search) are not available; use typed captures and Finish & Draft on reconnect.

## Scan Label / Reading photo queued and never finished

- Confirm the queue is working: pull down to refresh on the Review screen
- Check the Failed Uploads screen for a stuck item
- If the scan hangs after reconnect, Export the photo and try Scan Label / Reading on it fresh

## Reinspection checklist shows no items

The parent inspection's findings may not have synced yet. Pull down to refresh on Home. If the parent still shows missing data after a sync, confirm the parent inspection has been fully synced by its original inspector.
