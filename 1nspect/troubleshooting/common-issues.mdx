Most things that go wrong in 1nspecT trace back to the same handful of causes. Scan this page first; the chance one of these matches what you're seeing is high.

## The top five

| What's happening | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| An inspection isn't showing up on my phone | [Inspection didn't sync](/1nspect/troubleshooting/inspection-didnt-sync) |
| An automated email never arrived | Workflow step is disabled (starter steps are seeded disabled), or trigger / recipient misconfigured. If the Dashboard shows an "emails didn't arrive" alert or Inspection Details shows a bounce banner, the email bounced; check the Communications tab for per-recipient status. See [Communication workflows](/1nspect/getting-started/communication-workflows) and [Resend email](/1nspect/features/resend-email). |
| The AI is blocked for insufficient credits | [Billing and AI Credits](/1nspect/troubleshooting/billing-and-credits) |
| A surcharge isn't charging | The surcharge's Active toggle is off. **Business Ops › Pricing › Surcharges**, enable, **Save Fees & Surcharges**. |
| Clients see slots on days the inspector blocked | Google Calendar isn't connected for that inspector. **Settings › Users** on the inspector, connect Calendar. |

## When nothing on this page matches

Email `support@mymavtech.com` with:

- The order number (if the issue is on a specific order)
- A short description of what you tried and what happened
- A screenshot if the issue is visual
- The approximate time it happened (helps us check logs)
