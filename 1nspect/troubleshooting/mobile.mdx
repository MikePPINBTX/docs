Fixes for the mobile app issues most inspectors hit. Try the fix listed; if nothing works, email `support@mymavtech.com` with the app version (shown at the bottom of Config) and a short description.

## The app won't open or keeps crashing

- Force-quit and relaunch
- Confirm you're on the latest version (over-the-air updates deliver automatically; a full relaunch sometimes applies a pending update)
- Restart your phone
- If still broken, uninstall and reinstall. Captured but unsynced data in a stuck install may be lost; sync first if you can

## An inspection isn't on Home

See [Inspection didn't sync](/1nspect/troubleshooting/inspection-didnt-sync).

## AI Generate is unavailable

- **Offline alert**: _"AI Generate Unavailable Offline. You're offline. Save this finding now (Save + New) and use Finish & Draft once you're reconnected."_ Reconnect to a network, then use Finish & Draft on the Review screen for batched drafting.
- **Insufficient credits alert**: see [Billing and AI Credits](/1nspect/troubleshooting/billing-and-credits).

## Vision Scan refuses to run

Alert: _"Vision Scan requires a photo. Capture a photo first."_ Attach a photo to the finding, then arm Vision Scan and tap AI Generate.

## A photo won't upload

- Check the Review screen for the upload-fail banner. Tap into Failed Uploads for per-item Retry / Export / Delete.
- Confirm you have signal (Wi-Fi or cellular if **Upload Media over Cellular** is on in Config)
- If a specific photo keeps failing, Export it to the camera roll from Failed Uploads, then re-add it to the finding

## HEIC photos aren't rendering in the published report

Shouldn't happen: 1nspecT converts HEIC to JPEG on upload. If you see this, email support with the inspection number.

## Publish Report doesn't appear on Review

Mobile publishing is per-user. Your firm hasn't enabled it for you. Ask your admin to turn it on under your user profile.

## Field Quote booking fails

Needs a live connection. If you're offline, save the quote (it syncs when you reconnect) or text the client a quote link and complete booking later from the web admin.

## Caption dropdown is empty

The caption library isn't associated with your template. Not something you can fix on mobile; ask your admin to open **Narrative Management › Narrative Library** and associate a caption collection with your template.
