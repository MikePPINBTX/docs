An inspection was created on the web but the inspector cannot find it in Assigned Inspections on mobile. This is the single most common first-run problem and almost always comes down to one of four causes. Walk this page top to bottom.

## 1. The inspection setup fields are incomplete

Open the inspection on the web admin (**Inspections** top-nav › the order). Confirm **all four** of these are set on the inspection:

- **Inspector** (assigned)
- **Template**
- **Narrative Collection**
- **Caption Library**

Any one missing blocks the sync. Set them, save, and the inspection appears on the phone within a few seconds.

Setting the **Inspection Type** is optional but recommended; it sharpens the AI narratives.

## 2. The caption or narrative library isn't associated with the template

If you've just created a brand-new template and the four fields above look correct, the library might not be bound to the template.

- Open **Narrative Management** › **Intelligence & Library Hub** › **Narrative Library** tab
- Find the caption collection and the narrative collection you expect this template to use
- On each one, use the template-picker action to confirm it's associated with your template

If the picker shows no template, the association didn't save. Reopen and set it.

## 3. The inspector isn't marked as an Inspector

If the Inspector field above shows an inspector who doesn't appear on the mobile sign-in, their user profile may not have the Inspector toggle on.

- Open **Settings** › **Users** tab
- Edit the inspector
- Confirm the **Inspector** toggle is on

## 4. The agreement is unsigned (blocks starting, not syncing)

If the inspection is on the phone but Start is disabled, the agreement hasn't been signed yet. The sync works; the start-guard is separate.

- The client needs to open their portal link and sign the agreement
- Once signed, the inspector can tap Start on the phone

## Mobile-side verification

On the phone:

- Pull down on Home to refresh
- Confirm the inspector is signed in as the right user (bottom of Config shows the account)
- Confirm the inspection is scheduled within the next 30 days (older or far-future jobs don't appear in Assigned Inspections)

## Still not working

Email `support@mymavtech.com` with:

- The order number
- The expected inspector's email
- Confirmation of each of the four fields (Inspector, Template, Narrative Collection, Caption Library)
