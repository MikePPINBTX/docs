Field Quote is the on-site quoting flow. Enter an address, pick services, see the price, and send a quote link to the client in seconds. If the client wants to book, you can do that from the same screen without opening the web admin.

<Info>
  The button on the Home screen is labeled **Field Quote**. The screen's own title is **Quick Quote**. Same thing.
</Info>

## Where to find it

**Quote** tab in the bottom bar.

The screen opens with the header **Quick Quote** and a subheader: _"Generate and text a quote in seconds."_

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Property Address">
    Type the address. Address autocomplete suggests matches as you type. Pick one to drop in the property details (year built, square footage, foundation type) via the Property Baseline lookup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Services">
    Pick primary services and ancillaries. Pricing calculates live from your Pricing Manager rules: tiers, surcharges, discounts, taxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Units and structures (if applicable)">
    For multi-unit properties, set the unit count and building total. For properties with detached structures (garage, workshop, guest house, barn), add rows with structure type and sqft. 1nspecT prices each per its sqft-tier table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client details">
    Name and email (and co-client if there is one). Phone number if you want to text the quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the price">
    The live price is shown at the top of the screen as you build the quote. Confirm it looks right before you send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or book">
    Three paths: text the quote, book on the spot, or save as a lead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Text a quote

Tap **Text a Quote** to send the client an SMS with a link to accept the quote online. The link opens on their phone, lets them pick the services they want, and accepts the quote with one tap.

## Book on the spot

If the client is ready to go, tap **Book Now**. You see available slots from each inspector's Google Calendar for the estimated duration. Pick a date and time, confirm, and the booking is created. The agreement sends automatically per your Communications workflow.

## Manual discounts and surcharges

If you need to add a one-off discount or surcharge not in your rules (an agent referral discount, a special-case surcharge), you can add it inline. This action is limited to admins and owners. Inspectors without admin rights see the standard rules only.

## Converts to an order

A quote that is accepted or booked becomes an order. The order appears in the Inspections list on the web admin and in your Home list on mobile once the inspection is assigned to you.
