Field Quote is the on-site quoting flow. Enter an address, pick services, see the price, and send a quote link to the client in seconds. If the client wants to book, you can do that from the same screen without opening the web admin.

<Info>
  The button on the Home screen is labeled **Field Quote**. The screen's own title is **Quick Quote**. Same thing.
</Info>

## Where to find it

**Quote** tab in the bottom bar.

The screen opens with the header **Quick Quote** and a subheader: _"Generate and text a quote in seconds."_

## The screen top to bottom

The Quick Quote screen is one scroll. Section headers in order:

1. **Property Address** — type the address; Google Places autocomplete fills the **address only**. To pre-fill year built, square footage and foundation type, tap the **Property Insights** button that appears after you set the address.
2. **Primary Service** — pick the inspection type (Residential Resale, New Construction, Reinspection, etc.) from your Pricing Manager.
3. **Add-On Services** — pick ancillaries (sewer scope, radon, pool, WDI, etc.).
4. **Detached Structures** — for properties with a detached garage, workshop, guest house, barn: add one row per structure with type and sqft. Each prices per its own sqft tier.
5. **Discount (Optional)** — apply a discount from your Discounts list.
6. **Manual Adjustments** — admins and owners only. A one-off discount or surcharge not in your rules.
7. **Client Phone** — the client's phone number. The quote link gets texted to this number from your personal number via SMS. In book mode, this is saved on the order.
8. **Book this as an order** — toggles book-on-the-spot mode (asks for client name, email and co-client if applicable). In quote mode (toggle off), the quote is texted to the client to accept.

The **Estimated Total** at the bottom of the screen updates live as you build the quote, so you always see the running total.

## The flow

Two paths through the screen:

- **Send a quote** — fill in Property, Services, (Structures, Discount) and a Client Phone number (the only required client field for a quote). Tap **Send Quote via SMS** — this opens **your own SMS app** with the quote link pre-filled, so you send from your personal number. Sending the quote creates a lead on your account.
- **Book on the spot** — tick **Book this as an order**, fill in client name and email (and co-client details if needed). Pick a date and time from available slots for the one selected inspector. Tap **Create Order**. The agreement sends automatically per your Communications workflow.

The running total sits at the bottom of the screen.

## Send a quote

Tap **Send Quote via SMS**. The app opens your phone's SMS app with the quote link pre-filled; the message sends from your own number. Sending creates a lead on your account that the client can accept from the link.

## Book on the spot

If the client is ready to go, tick **Book this as an order**, fill in client details, pick a date and time from available slots for the selected inspector, then tap **Create Order**. The agreement sends automatically per your Communications workflow.

If the client has already signed an agreement outside 1nspecT, tick **Client already contracted externally** in book mode. 1nspecT then doesn't send its own agreement, and the client email is not required. Your other emails still go out as usual (to the client email, if one is on file): booking confirmation, reminder and report emails. You can also fill in **Signed by**, **Date signed** and **Notes** (all optional here). If your company's [Signed agreement policy](/1nspect/features/signed-agreement-policy) is **Signed before the inspection starts**, fill in who signed and when, or the job can't be started until someone records the outside agreement on the web. Under the other policies, the job can be started without an agreement.

## Manual discounts and surcharges

If you need to add a one-off discount or surcharge not in your rules (an agent referral discount, a special-case surcharge), you can add it inline. This action is limited to admins and owners. Inspectors without admin rights see the standard rules only.

## Converts to an order

A quote that is accepted or booked becomes an order. The order appears in the Inspections list on the web admin and in your Home list on mobile once the inspection is assigned to you.
