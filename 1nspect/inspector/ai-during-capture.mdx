The AI tools on the Capture screen split into two jobs: getting data into fields (Scan Label / Reading) and building the narrative (AI Generate, Vision Scan, Smart Search). Add to Summary flags findings for the report summary. The one-button Finish & Draft Report on the Review screen handles batch drafting on the drive home. This page covers each tool in detail.

**Mobile cost summary.** Smart Search, typed `label: value` reads, Save to Narrative Library and the Property Baseline lookup are **free** on mobile. The paid mobile capabilities are: AI Generate (6 credits text-only, 10 with Vision Scan armed), Scan Label / Reading from a photo (4 credits), System Performance Opinion (10 credits) and Finish & Draft Report (50 credits per report).

## Scan Label / Reading

The workhorse. One button handles three things: reading a data plate, reading an instrument, or capturing a value you type yourself. It always shows a confirm screen before anything is applied.

### Reading a data plate or instrument

1. Photograph the plate or reading. A furnace data tag, a thermometer in a supply register, an IR gun display, a Fieldpiece screenshot.
2. Caption it plainly: `Furnace supply air temperature` or `Condenser data plate`.
3. Tap **Scan Label / Reading**. The AI reads the values off the photo and matches them to the right fields using your caption plus the section and subsection you are in. The match is forgiving: `Furnace Supply Air Temperature` finds `Furnace Tested Supply Air Temperature`.
4. **Confirm**. A Confirm sheet shows what the AI found. Review, edit anything off, uncheck anything wrong, then Apply.

Cost: 4 AI credits per photo.

### Typing a value yourself

Type the caption as `field label: value` (for example, `Furnace supply air temp: 114.5°F`), then tap **Scan Label / Reading**. Everything after the colon goes to the matching field, same confirm sheet.

Cost: **free**. Typed `label: value` reads are parsed on the phone and never deduct credits. Works fully offline.

### Screenshots count as photos

Using a Fieldpiece or similar tool app? Screenshot the reading display, caption it `Furnace temperatures`, and tap Scan Label / Reading. It extracts every reading on screen and routes each to its own field. No transcription.

### Capture two, get the third free

With calculated fields turned on in your template, capture the supply and the return and the app computes the temperature rise / delta-T for you. The same goes for equipment age from a manufacture year.

### The Co-Inspector moment

Once the app has a computed value (temp rise, delta-T, equipment age), it checks against the advisory range on the field and drafts the matching narrative:

- **Out of range** → prompts a deficiency narrative draft
- **Within range** → prompts an acceptable-performance narrative draft
- **Aged equipment** → flags the age against the service life you've defined

You still approve every word.

## AI Generate

Writes the finding's narrative on the spot, from your caption and field notes, in your SoP's voice.

- **Where**: Capture screen, on the finding
- **When**: Tap when the caption and field note together describe the finding, and you want the narrative done now
- **Cost**: 6 AI credits (text-only); 10 if Vision Scan is armed

### Offline guard

If you tap AI Generate with no connection, the app shows: _"AI Generate Unavailable Offline. You're offline. Save this finding now (Save + New) and use Finish & Draft once you're reconnected — it will batch-draft all pending narratives at once."_

## Vision Scan

Vision Scan is not a standalone button. It is a **modifier** on AI Generate. Toggle Vision Scan on (its label flips from `Vision Scan` to `Vision Armed`), then tap AI Generate. The whole payload (photo + caption + field notes) goes up for analysis. The AI drafts the narrative from what it sees in the photo.

### Co-Inspector pass

After the primary narrative is drafted, Vision Scan runs the **Co-Inspector**: the AI re-reads the photo and surfaces up to three additional findings it noticed. For each, accept into a new finding or dismiss. No separate cost — included in the 10-credit Vision Scan call.

### Requires a photo

The alert appears when you **arm the toggle** without a photo attached: _"Vision Scan requires a photo. Capture a photo first."_ Attach a photo first, then arm Vision Scan and tap AI Generate.

## Smart Search

Toggle at the top of the narrative field (`🔍 Smart Search`). When open, a search bar lets you pull a narrative you have already written from your cached library. **Free, offline keyword search.** Mobile Smart Search never calls the server, so there is no credit cost and no network dependency.

Label flips to `✕ Close Search` when the search bar is open. Tap to close.

## Add to Summary

Not AI, but it works hand in hand. Flags a finding into the report summary. Use it on safety items and potentially costly repairs. Label flips from `Add to Summary` to `In Summary` when on.

## Finish & Draft Report (on Review)

The batch call. One tap on the Review screen syncs everything from the phone and asks the AI to draft every blank narrative on the server.

Cost: 50 AI credits per report. Most inspectors use Finish & Draft on the drive back to the office. When drafts finish, Review shows a 6-second banner (there's no push notification).

## What's offline-capable

| Tool | Offline behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Scan Label / Reading (text, `label: value`) | Parses on the device; free; no network needed |
| Scan Label / Reading (photo) | Queues on the device; finishes automatically when you reconnect. The deferred result auto-applies and shows a review pill. |
| Smart Search | Works fully offline (keyword search of the cached library) |
| Save to Narrative Library | Direct write to the library; free |
| AI Generate | Needs a live connection (or defer with Finish & Draft) |
| Vision Scan | Needs a live connection |
| Finish & Draft Report | Start it with a live connection. The app does not check for one first: offline, it still shows "Report Drafting Started" but the drafting never runs, and no error is shown. |
