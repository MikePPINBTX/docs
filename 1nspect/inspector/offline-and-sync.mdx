1nspecT is offline-first. Crawlspaces, rural new builds, dead-signal basements: keep working. Everything you capture is stored on your device: photos, captions, narratives, field notes, ratings. The AI needs a connection, so for those features the app either defers (queued and finishes when you reconnect) or asks you to try again later.

## What works fully offline

| Capability | Offline |
| --- | --- |
| Photo and video capture | Yes |
| Photo markup | Yes |
| Captions | Yes |
| Ratings | Yes |
| Field notes | Yes |
| Caption library autocomplete | Yes |
| Smart Search (free keyword search of the cached library) | Yes |
| Scan Label / Reading with a typed `label: value` caption | Yes (parses on the device) |
| Reinspection checklists (original photo and narrative visible) | Yes |

## What queues offline and finishes on reconnect

| Capability | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Scan Label / Reading (photo) | Queues on the device; finishes automatically when you reconnect |
| Media uploads (photos, videos) | Queue and upload in the background when connectivity returns |
| Save to Narrative Library | A free direct write to your library. There is no AI de-identification on mobile |

## What needs a live connection

| Capability | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| AI Generate | Shows an offline alert: _"AI Generate Unavailable Offline. You're offline. Save this finding now (Save + New) and use Finish & Draft once you're reconnected — it will batch-draft all pending narratives at once."_ |
| Vision Scan | Requires a live connection when you tap AI Generate with Vision armed |
| Finish & Draft Report | Server-side batch; start it with a live connection. The app does not check first: offline, it still shows "Report Drafting Started" but the drafting never runs, and no error is shown. |
| Publish Report (from the phone) | Server-side; needs a live connection |
| Field Quote booking | Needs a live connection to confirm the slot |

## The upload queue

The app's sync engine runs in the background: when it sees a connection, it pushes photos and videos to the cloud. You can keep capturing while earlier media uploads.

### Default: Wi-Fi only

By default the app uploads over Wi-Fi only. Turn on **Upload Media over Cellular** in **Config › Sync Preferences** if you want cellular uploads too.

### Upload failures surface clearly

If an upload fails, a banner on the **Review** screen reads _"N Media file(s) failed to upload, tap to manage (retry / export / delete)"_ (where N is the count). Tap into it to open the Failed Uploads screen and work through the queue. Nothing is silently lost.

## Save without overwriting

Multiple inspectors on the same team inspection, each capturing offline, come back online and sync. Findings record who captured them, but ratings are stored **per area, not per inspector**, so two inspectors rating the same area overwrite each other.

## Finish & Draft on the drive home

The standard workflow for most inspectors:

1. In the field, lean on captures and ratings. AI Generate when it saves time; skip it when it doesn't.
2. On the drive back, once you have signal, open Review and tap **Finish & Draft Report**. The AI drafts every blank narrative on the server (50 AI credits per report) while you drive.
3. At the office, polish and publish from the Report Workspace on the web.
