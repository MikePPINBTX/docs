Archived inspections is a read-only list of your completed and published inspections. Use it to review past work, pull up a captured photo, or jump into a reinspection on a job you did before.

## Where to find it

From the Home tab, scroll to the Archived section (or tap the Archived view action). The list shows jobs you have completed, in reverse-chronological order.

## What's shown per inspection

Each row shows:

- Client name
- Property address
- Date of inspection
- Status (Completed, Published, Delivered)

Tap into any inspection to open it in read-only mode: your original captures, ratings, narratives and photos are all there.

## Starting a reinspection from an archived inspection

Creating a reinspection happens in the **web admin**, not on mobile. If a client comes back with a repair request:

1. On the web admin, open the parent order (**Inspections** top-nav).
2. On the Overview tab, click **Create Reinspection**.
3. The reinspection is created as a new phase on the same order, linked to the original. When it's assigned to you, it appears in your active Home list on mobile.

See [Reinspections](/1nspect/workflows/reinspections) for the full web-admin flow and the mobile capture flow.

## Local storage note

Photos from inspections older than 15 days are cleared from the device to free space. The data on an archived inspection stays in the cloud; what you lose locally is the quick-access copy. When you open an archived inspection older than 15 days, photos load from the cloud as you scroll.
