Signing in to the 1nspecT mobile app is a plain email-and-password sign-in. Your company's owner or admin creates your account on the web admin; you receive an email with a temporary password and change it on first sign-in.

## The sign-in screen

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Email | Your work email as added in Settings › Users on the web admin. Placeholder: `you@example.com`. |
| Password | Your current password. Hidden by default; a show/hide icon toggles visibility. |

Tap **Sign In** to continue.

## Forgot password

Tap **Forgot Password** below the password field. The app sends a password reset link to the email address you typed. Enter your email in the Email field first, then tap Forgot Password.

If you tap Forgot Password with the Email field blank, the app prompts you to enter it first.

## New account

The mobile app does not have a self-service sign-up. If your company has not yet added you as a user, email `info@mymavtech.com` to request an account. Your company's owner can add you from the web admin at any time; see [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team).

## After signing in

The app opens on the **Home** tab, which shows your assigned inspections for the next 30 days. See [Home and assigned inspections](/1nspect/inspector/home-and-assigned-inspections).

## Signing out

Go to **Config** (last bottom tab). Scroll to the bottom and tap **Sign Out**. The app shows a confirmation; tap Sign Out again to confirm.

Sign out when you hand the phone to another inspector, or when you need to switch between personal and work use.

## Session

The app keeps you signed in across restarts. You stay signed in until you tap Sign Out or your account is disabled on the web admin by an owner or admin.
