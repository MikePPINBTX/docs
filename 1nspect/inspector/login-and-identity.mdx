Signing in to the 1nspecT mobile app is a plain email-and-password sign-in. Your company's owner or admin creates your account on the web admin, and you receive an invite email with a password-setup link. You set your own password on first sign-in.

## The sign-in screen

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Email | Your work email as added in Business Ops › Users on the web admin. Placeholder: `you@example.com`. |
| Password | Your current password. It is hidden, and there is no show/hide icon. |

Tap **Sign In** to continue.

## Forgot password

Tap **Forgot password?** below the password field. The app sends a password reset link to the email address you typed. Enter your email in the Email field first, then tap Forgot password?.

If you tap Forgot password? with the Email field blank, the app prompts you to enter it first.

## New account

The mobile app does not have a self-service sign-up. If your company has not yet added you as a user, **contact your company's owner or admin** and ask them to create your account. The sign-in screen's "Contact your administrator" link emails [info@mymavtech.com](mailto:info@mymavtech.com). They do this from **Business Ops › Users** in the web admin; see [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team).

<Note>
  New to 1nspecT and signing your company up? Start on the web at `app.1nspect.app/signup`. The self-service signup flow creates your company's tenant and your owner account; from there you add inspectors on the Users tab.
</Note>

## After signing in

The app lands on the **Home** menu (Assigned Inspections, View Completed Inspections, Field Quote). See [Home and assigned inspections](/1nspect/inspector/home-and-assigned-inspections).

## Signing out

Go to **Config** (last bottom tab). Scroll to the bottom and tap **Sign Out**. The app shows a confirmation; tap Sign Out again to confirm.

Sign out when you hand the phone to another inspector, or when you need to switch between personal and work use.

## Session

The app keeps you signed in across restarts. You stay signed in until you tap Sign Out or your account is disabled on the web admin by an owner or admin.
