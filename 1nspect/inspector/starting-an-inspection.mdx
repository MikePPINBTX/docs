Open **Assigned Inspections** from Home and tap a job; the job setup screen opens. Confirm or edit the pre-filled information, review the Property Baseline, and tap **Start Inspection** to open the Capture screen.

## The setup screen top to bottom

The setup screen is one scroll with four main sections, in this order:

1. **Client Information** — primary client name, email, phone (pre-filled from the order; edit if the office captured anything wrong)
2. **Additional Client (Optional)** — the co-client row; show it when there is a co-signer or spouse
3. **Property Address** — the inspected property's address, with Google Places autocomplete if you need to edit
4. **Property Baseline (for AI Findings)** — Year Built, Square Feet (required), Foundation Type, On-Site Systems, Exterior Cladding and Roof Covering. These fields always show. When an AI baseline exists, a **🤖 Review Full AI Baseline** button opens the **🤖 AI Property Baseline** view with Site Conditions and Construction-era materials. Intelligence for you, not shown to the client.

The setup screen also has its own **Template**, **Narrative Collection** and **Caption Library** pickers, and **Start Weather** and **End Weather** fields. Then tap **Start Inspection**.

## What's pre-filled

The office set up the order on the web, so most of the information is already in:

- Client name (and co-client)
- Property address
- Year built
- Square footage
- Scheduled date, time and inspector
- Primary service and ancillaries
- Template, caption library, narrative library, inspection type

Confirm each field is correct. Anything that's off, tap to edit.

## On-site details

Four fields are typically completed on arrival:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Parties present | Who is at the inspection (client, agent, seller, contractor) |
| Client concerns | Anything the client flagged at scheduling or on arrival |
| Weather | Current conditions (temperature, precipitation, wind) |
| Cover photo | A photo of the front of the house for the report cover (optional; set it here on the setup screen, since Review has no cover-photo option) |

These fields print on the report and feed context to the AI narrative drafting.

## Property Baseline

An AI-assembled snapshot of the inspected property. It does not appear on the setup screen itself: tap **🤖 Review Full AI Baseline** (shown when a baseline exists) to open the **🤖 AI Property Baseline** view. Two subsections:

- **Site Conditions** — soil map unit, dominant component, shrink-swell potential, FEMA flood zone, EPA radon zone and termite pressure (the web adds climate zone and attic insulation)
- **Construction-era materials** — construction materials typical for this build year and region (water supply piping, drain/waste/vent, cladding, roof covering), each with prevalence and severity

This is intelligence for you, not a finding. It is not shown to the client. Use it to know what to look for before you walk the home.

## Property attributes

The Property Baseline section on the setup screen always carries these property attributes:

- Year Built and Square Feet
- Foundation Type (slab, pier and beam, basement)
- On-Site Systems
- Exterior Cladding (brick, wood, fiber cement, stucco)
- Roof Covering (asphalt shingle, metal, tile, flat)

These feed the AI's narrative framing and the aged-systems check.

## What blocks the start

The inspection won't open to Capture until:

- A **template** is assigned to the inspection (otherwise the job shows a Setup Required card)
- A **narrative collection** is set. The caption library is optional.
- The **agreement** has been signed by the client

If any is missing, Start shows the specific blocker and a note on who can fix it. See [First-run gotchas](/1nspect/getting-started/first-run-gotchas).

## Tapping into Capture

Once setup is complete, tap **Start Inspection**. The Capture screen opens on the first section, ready to work. See [Capture screen](/1nspect/inspector/capture-screen).
