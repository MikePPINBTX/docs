Config is the mobile app's settings page. The title at the top of the screen reads **Config**. It holds sync and capture preferences, local photo storage, help links and the Sign Out action. If an inspection is in progress, a Resume Active Inspection button is at the top for one-tap return.

## Where to find it

**Config** tab in the bottom bar (last of five tabs: Home, Review, Ratings, Quote, Config).

## Resume Active Inspection

If you have an inspection open and in progress, a prominent button at the top of Config reads **🚀 RESUME ACTIVE INSPECTION** with a subtitle naming the property. One tap takes you back to the Capture screen for that job.

## Sync Preferences

Settings that control how media uploads behave:

- **Upload Media over Cellular** — off by default. Turn on if you want photos and videos to upload over your cellular connection instead of waiting for Wi-Fi.

## Capture Preferences

Settings that affect how the Capture screen behaves:

- **Auto-Scroll After Capture** — on by default. The screen auto-scrolls to the next field after each capture.
- Other capture-related toggles your version may show.

## Local Photo Storage

Settings and reports about the photos on your device:

- A storage report showing how much space 1nspecT is using
- A clean-up status (automatic every 15 days)
- Manual cleanup action if you want to free space now

## Help and about

At the bottom of Config:

- The current app version
- Links to the help site ([https://help.1nspect.app](https://help.1nspect.app))
- A pre-filled email link to `support@mymavtech.com` with your version and tenant ID for diagnostics

## Sign Out

Scroll to the bottom and tap **Sign Out**. A confirmation alert appears; tap Sign Out again to confirm.

Use this when you hand the phone to another inspector, or when you need to switch accounts.

## AI Credits

Your AI credit balance is not shown on Config. The balance lives on the web admin (**Settings › Billing** for owners and admins). The mobile app only surfaces credit information when an AI call is blocked for insufficient credits. The alert links to the web Billing page.

See [AI Credits](/1nspect/features/it-tokens).
