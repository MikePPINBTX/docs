The 1nspecT mobile app is where field capture happens. One tool, one screen for most of the work, five tabs along the bottom. You pick up your assigned inspection, drive out, work the Capture screen, and head back to the office. The AI drafts your narratives while you drive.

## The five bottom tabs

| Tab | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A menu of three buttons: Assigned Inspections, View Completed Inspections and Field Quote |
| **Review** | The inspection you're working on: section-by-section review, finish actions |
| **Ratings** | Area ratings and comments for the current inspection |
| **Quote** | Field Quote — price and book an inspection on site |
| **Config** | Settings, sync preferences, storage, help and sign-out |

The main inspection work happens on the Capture screen, which opens when you tap **Start Inspection** on the inspection's setup screen. The Capture screen is a full-screen workspace with its own navigation, not one of the bottom tabs.

## What the app does

- **Field capture** — photos, video, captions, ratings, field notes, equipment data
- **AI drafting** — narratives written from your capture, in your voice, with your SoP
- **Offline work** — capture goes on with no signal; everything syncs when you reconnect
- **Reinspections** — repair-item checklists with before and after photos
- **Field Quote** — price a job on site, text the client, book on the spot
- **Publishing from the phone** — your company turns this on first in Business Ops › Company Info (**Allow Publishing Reports from Mobile**, off by default), then individual inspectors can be opted out in Business Ops › Users

## What the app does not do

- **No in-app report viewer.** Published reports open in any browser from the client link. The app is a capture tool, not a document reader.
- **No separate voice engine.** The app does not have its own speech-recognition engine. It does carry a robust dictionary of common inspection terms and phrases, and auto-corrects frequent voice-to-text misspellings as you dictate (stutters removed, typos like "freeze board" → "frieze board" fixed) on top of your phone keyboard's own dictation.
- **No separate AI Credits meter.** Your AI credits balance lives in the web admin. You see the balance only in an alert when a call is blocked for insufficient credits.

## Supported platforms

iOS (iPhone, iPad) and Android (phones, tablets). The current version is 2.4.0.

## What's offline-first

Capture, ratings, library autocomplete, reinspection checklists all work with no signal. Typed `label: value` captures parse on the device. Photo-based scans queue on the device and finish automatically when you reconnect. AI Generate, Vision Scan, publishing and booking need a live connection when you tap them.
