The Capture screen is the heart of the mobile app. It is built so you never leave it: photo, caption, rating, AI tools and save all in one scroll. This page walks the screen top to bottom, then covers each piece in detail.

## The screen top to bottom

In order, as you scroll down the Capture screen:

1. **Media area** at the top — the camera / video / files picker (or the thumbnails of what you've captured so far)
2. **Section selector** — one button per inspection section (STRUCT, ELEC, HVAC, PLUMB, and so on). The selected section highlights.
3. **Subsection selector** — one button per subsection inside the current section (FOUND, GRADE, ROOF_COV, and so on). The selected subsection highlights.
4. **Fields chip** — a collapsible `📋 Fields · N of M` chip with an orange badge showing how many required fields remain (when the subsection carries system-info fields)
5. **Caption field** — placeholder `Enter caption for this deficiency...`
6. **Add field note** — a collapsible row (`▸ Add field note`) that reveals a private notes field
7. **Narrative field** — the full narrative text area with `🔍 Smart Search` and formatting chips (`• Start`, `1. Start`, `Clear`) above it
8. **Condition Rating** — the SoP rating buttons (TREC shows I / NI / NP / D with their full labels — Inspected / Not Inspected / Not Present / Deficient)
9. **AI Tools** — the AI action buttons (covered in detail below)
10. **Save + New / Save + Finish / 🗑** — the save row at the very bottom of the finding form
11. **Bottom tabs** — Home, Review, Ratings, Quote, Config (fixed below everything)

The section and subsection buttons change with your SoP. A TREC job, an InterNACHI job, and a custom Pre-Pour foundation job each show their own sections, subsections, and ratings. Same screen, different shape.

## The core loop

For each finding (deficiency, observation, or system detail), work the screen in this order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the section and subsection">
    Tap the section (STRUCT, ELEC...) and then the subsection (FOUND, GRADE...). The screen scopes to that subsection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture media">
    Tap the camera, video, or files icon in the media area at the top. Add as many photos as the finding needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill required fields (if any)">
    Tap the `📋 Fields · N of M` chip to open the system-info fields. Fill the required ones; the orange badge counts down. Smart (computed) fields update live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a caption">
    A short description of what you see. The caption is what the AI reads to draft the narrative, and the title of the deficiency card on the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a field note (optional)">
    Expand **▸ Add field note** and drop any private context you want the AI to read but the client to never see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft the narrative">
    Type it yourself, pull one from your library with **🔍 Smart Search**, or tap **AI Generate** in the AI Tools section below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a rating">
    Tap the rating button that matches (TREC: I / NI / NP / D).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Save + New** clears the form and keeps you on the screen for the next capture. **Save + Finish** saves and leaves. The 🗑 at the end deletes the finding without saving. Lean on Save + New.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Media capture (top of screen)

Above the section selector, the media area shows one of two states:

- **Empty**: a camera icon with _"No media captured. Choose an option below to add media"_, and three **icon-only** options below it: 📷 (camera), 🎥 (video, clips up to 60 seconds), and 📁 (files picker from camera roll or files).
- **With media**: the captured photos and videos tile here. Tap any thumbnail to open it full-screen. For photos, [Photo markup](/1nspect/inspector/photo-markup) opens from the **Edit** control under the photo (not by tapping the thumbnail).

## Fields counter

For sections with system-info fields (equipment specs, locations, counts, computed values), a **📋 Fields · N of M** chip appears above the Caption. The chip reads `N of M answered`, with an orange badge showing the number still unanswered. Tap to expand the fields and fill them in. Smart fields (computed, like temperature rise or equipment age) update live as you enter the inputs they depend on.

## AI Tools (bottom of the capture area)

Under the Caption, Narrative and Condition Rating, an **AI Tools** section carries the five main action buttons in this order (top to bottom):

| Order | Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Vision Scan** (toggle) | Modifier on AI Generate. When armed, the AI reads the photo and uses it; also runs the Co-Inspector pass for additional findings. Button label flips from `Vision Scan` to `Vision Armed` when on. Costs 10 AI credits per AI Generate call. |
| 2 | **AI Generate** | Writes the finding's narrative from your caption and field notes. Costs 6 AI credits (text-only) or 10 (with Vision Scan armed). |
| 3 | **Scan Label / Reading** | Reads a data plate, instrument, or a typed `field label: value` caption. Always confirms before applying. 4 credits for a photo; **typed reads are free**. |
| 4 | **🔗 Add to Existing Finding** | Opens a two-mode panel: 📷 Extra photo or 📍 Another location. Replaces the older Location Tag button. |
| 5 | **⚠️ Add to Summary / In Summary** | Flags the finding into the report summary. Label flips from `Add to Summary` to `In Summary` when on. |

Above the Narrative field is a separate control:

- **🔍 Smart Search** (toggle) — Opens an offline keyword search of your cached narrative library. Label flips to `✕ Close Search` when open. **Free.**

See [AI during capture](/1nspect/inspector/ai-during-capture) for how each tool works in detail.

## Caption library

As you type the caption, the caption library suggests completions from the collection associated with your template. Suggestions work offline. Pick a suggestion to drop in a known-good caption. Saving a new caption back to the library is **manual** — tap **💾 Save to Caption Library** on the finding to add it.

## Field notes

A private, unformatted note under the caption. The AI reads field notes when it drafts, so this is where you drop any context the AI should have but the client should not see. Field notes never print on the report.

## Multi-instance subsections

Some subsections are multi-instance (two HVAC units, three water heaters). The subsection header shows an instance switcher. Each instance has its own location and system info; findings are tagged to the right instance.

## Reference snippets

If your Intelligence Hub has reference snippets attached to the template, a snippets picker lets you attach a short reference (a code article, a manufacturer caveat, a safety advisory) to the finding. The snippet prints alongside the narrative in the report.

## Report summary flag

Tap **Add to Summary** on any finding to flag it into the report summary section. Use it for safety items and potentially costly repairs. The label flips to **In Summary** when on. Tap again to remove.

## Library pick jumps to the right place

If you tap a library entry that lives in a different subsection, the app jumps to that subsection, pre-fills the finding, and offers an Undo if that wasn't what you wanted.

## Inspection-aware autocorrect

The app carries a robust dictionary of inspection terms and phrases and auto-corrects common voice-to-text misspellings as you dictate ("the the" → "the", "freeze" → "frieze"). This runs on top of your phone's built-in keyboard dictation; there is no separate 1nspecT voice-recognition engine.
