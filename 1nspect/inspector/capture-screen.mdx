The Capture screen is the heart of the mobile app. It is built so you never leave it: photo, caption, rating, AI tools and save all on one screen. Section and subsection buttons run across the middle to move you through the inspection; the bottom bar carries Home, Review, Ratings, Quote and Config.

## The screen at a glance

- **Top**: current section header and the field tools row
- **Middle**: section and subsection buttons for the SoP you're inspecting against
- **Below middle**: the capture area (photo, caption, rating, field notes, narrative)
- **Bottom**: the five tabs (Home, Review, Ratings, Quote, Config)

The section and subsection buttons change with your SoP. A TREC job, an InterNACHI job, and a custom Pre-Pour foundation job each show their own sections, subsections, and ratings. Same screen, different shape.

## The core loop

For each finding (deficiency, observation, or system detail):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take a photo">
    Tap the camera to capture, or pick from your camera roll. Video and file attachments work the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a caption">
    A short description of what you see. The caption is what the AI reads to draft a narrative, and the title of the deficiency card on the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a rating">
    Rate the subsection. The rating options come from your SoP (TREC shows I / NI / NP / D; other standards show their own).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Save + New** clears the form and keeps you on the screen for the next capture. **Save + Finish** saves and leaves. Lean on Save + New.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The field tools

Across the top of the capture area:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scan Label / Reading** | Reads a data plate, instrument, or a typed `field label: value` caption. Always confirms before applying. |
| **Add to Existing Finding** | Opens a two-mode panel: 📷 Extra photo or 📍 Another location. Replaces the older Location Tag button. |
| **AI Generate** | Writes the finding's narrative from your caption and field notes. Costs 2 AI credits. |
| **Vision Scan** (toggle) | Modifier on AI Generate. When armed, the AI reads the photo and uses it; also runs the Co-Inspector pass for additional findings. Button label flips from `Vision Scan` to `Vision Armed` when on. Costs 8 AI credits per AI Generate call. |
| **Add to Summary / In Summary** | Flags the finding into the report summary. Label flips from `Add to Summary` to `In Summary` when on. |

Just above the narrative field is a sixth control:

- **🔍 Smart Search** (toggle) — Opens a search bar for your narrative library. Label flips to `✕ Close Search` when open. 1 AI credit per search.

See [AI during capture](/1nspect/inspector/ai-during-capture) for how each tool works in detail.

## Caption library

As you type the caption, the caption library suggests completions from the collection associated with your template. Suggestions work offline. Pick a suggestion to drop in a known-good caption. New captions you type save back to the library automatically.

## Field notes

A private, unformatted note under the caption. The AI reads field notes when it drafts, so this is where you drop any context the AI should have but the client should not see. Field notes never print on the report.

## Multi-instance subsections

Some subsections are multi-instance (two HVAC units, three water heaters). The subsection header shows an instance switcher. Each instance has its own location and system info; findings are tagged to the right instance.

## Reference snippets

If your Intelligence Hub has reference snippets attached to the template, a snippets picker lets you attach a short reference (a code article, a manufacturer caveat, a safety advisory) to the finding. The snippet prints alongside the narrative in the report.

## Report summary flag

Tap **Add to Summary** on any finding to flag it into the report summary section. Use it for safety items and potentially costly repairs. The label flips to **In Summary** when on. Tap again to remove.

## Library pick jumps to the right place

If you tap a library entry that lives in a different subsection, the app jumps to that subsection, pre-fills the finding, and offers an Undo if that wasn't what you wanted.

## Inspection-aware autocorrect

The app carries a robust dictionary of inspection terms and phrases and auto-corrects common voice-to-text misspellings as you dictate ("the the" → "the", "freeze" → "frieze"). This runs on top of your phone's built-in keyboard dictation; there is no separate 1nspecT voice-recognition engine.
