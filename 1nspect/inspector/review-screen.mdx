The Review tab is where you check your work before you leave the inspection. Every finding grouped by area, a summary-only filter so you can scan the deficiencies you flagged, inline edits for anything you want to tweak, and four actions at the bottom to finish the job and get the report moving.

## Where to find it

**Review** tab in the bottom bar.

## What it shows

A section-by-section list of every finding on the inspection:

- Section → area → subsection → finding
- The rating, caption and (if any) narrative for each finding
- A **Summary-only** filter toggle at the top (shows only findings flagged into the report summary)
- Inline edit on any finding to tweak the caption, narrative or field note without going back to the Capture screen

Also at the top: the **upload-fail banner** (if any), which jumps to the Failed Uploads screen when tapped. See [Photos and media](/1nspect/inspector/photos-and-media).

## The four finish actions

At the bottom of the Review screen:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **📷 Resume Inspection** | Jump back into the Capture screen for this inspection. |
| **☁️ Sync to Cloud** | Push your captures and data to the server without drafting or publishing. The alert reads: _"Pending photos and videos uploaded to the cloud. The inspection is not marked complete, use Finish & Draft or Publish when ready."_ |
| **✨ Finish & Draft Report** | Send everything for batch narrative generation. The AI drafts every blank narrative on the server. 50 AI credits per report. Most inspectors use this on the drive back to the office. |
| **🚀 Publish Report** | Runs the QA audit (ratings, narratives) and publishes the report straight from the phone. Only shown when your company has enabled per-user mobile publishing (see note). |

### Publishing from the field (per-user)

**Publish Report** may be switched off for you. Publishing from the mobile app is enabled per user by your firm, so report QA stays with whoever owns it. If you don't see Publish Report, that's by design — talk to your admin.

## QA that runs on Publish

When you tap Publish Report, the app:

- Confirms every area that takes a rating has one
- Confirms every finding has a narrative (or has been signed off as Publish without narrative)
- Confirms required SoP items are complete
- Then publishes the report to the client's permanent URL and fires the Report Published workflow trigger

If any item is unresolved, the audit shows the blockers so you can resolve them before Publish will go.

## Publish prompt

Before the report leaves the device, the app shows: _"Publish & send to client?"_ with a **Publish** action. Confirm to proceed. If something fails during the publish, the alert explains what: _"Could not publish —_ {reason}_."_
