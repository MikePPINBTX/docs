The Review tab is where you check your work before you leave the inspection. Every finding grouped by area, a summary-only filter so you can scan the deficiencies you flagged, an Edit button on each finding, and five actions at the bottom to finish the job and get the report moving.

## Where to find it

**Review** tab in the bottom bar.

## What it shows

A section-by-section list of every finding on the inspection:

- Section → area → subsection → finding
- The rating, caption and (if any) narrative for each finding
- A **📋 Summary Items Only** filter toggle at the top (shows only findings flagged into the report summary)
- An **✏️ Edit** button on each finding that opens the Capture screen (there is no inline editing)

Also at the top: the **upload-fail banner** (if any), which jumps to the Failed Uploads screen when tapped. See [Photos and media](/1nspect/inspector/photos-and-media).

## Findings at a Glance

Once at least one finding has been drafted, a strip of tiles appears above the list: **Total**, **Safety**, **Structural & Movement**, **Functional** and **Maintenance & Monitoring**, plus a smaller **Limitations** count. The labels and colours match [Findings at a Glance](/1nspect/admin-operator/templates/template-designer#findings-at-a-glance) in the PDF.

- **Before drafting** there's no strip at all. On a partly drafted inspection, it counts only the drafted findings, and the numbers go up as more are drafted.
- **Tap a tile** to show only those findings. Only sections with matches are listed, and they open automatically. Informational fields are hidden while the filter is on. Tap the tile again, or the **✕** on the "Showing…" chip, to see everything. It works together with **Summary Items Only**.
- **What's counted** is the same as in the PDF and the client's online report. **Total** is every reported deficiency. Limitations are counted on their own line and aren't in the total. Findings tagged Acceptable Performance, Informational or Aged Equipment Advisory aren't counted, and neither are captures with no narrative, such as data-plate and spec captures. A reading drafted into a deficiency, such as a low temperature rise, is counted.

The strip is a tool for the inspector. It always appears after drafting, whether or not your template prints Findings at a Glance, and on the phone it's always grouped by kind, not by report section.

## The five actions

At the bottom of the Review screen:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **✓ Readiness Audit** (shown as Complete Inspection on older app versions) | Only runs the readiness check. It does not complete the inspection, upload anything or notify anyone. |
| **📷 Resume Inspection** | Jump back into the Capture screen for this inspection. |
| **☁️ Sync to Cloud** | Push your captures and data to the server without drafting or publishing. The alert reads: _"Pending photos and videos uploaded to the cloud. The inspection is not marked complete, use Finish & Draft or Publish when ready."_ |
| **✨ Finish & Draft Report** | Send everything for batch narrative generation. The AI drafts every blank narrative on the server. 50 AI credits per report. This is what marks the job completed and moves it to Archived. Most inspectors use this on the drive back to the office. |
| **🚀 Publish Report** | Runs the QA audit (ratings, narratives) and publishes the report straight from the phone. Only shown when mobile publishing is enabled (see note). |

### Publishing from the field

**Publish Report** is controlled by a company switch first: **Allow Publishing Reports from Mobile** in Business Ops › Company Info, which is **off by default**. When it is on, an admin can still opt a specific user out in Business Ops › Users. If you don't see Publish Report, that's by design — talk to your admin.

## QA that runs on Publish

When you tap Publish Report, the app:

- Confirms every area that takes a rating has one
- Confirms every finding has a narrative (or has been signed off as Publish without narrative)
- Confirms required SoP items are complete
- Runs the narrative compliance review
- Then publishes the report to the client's permanent URL and fires the matching workflow trigger: Report Published when the order is paid, or the awaiting-payment variant (`report.published.payment_pending`) when it is not

If any item is unresolved, the audit shows the blockers so you can resolve them before Publish will go.

## Publish prompt

Before the report leaves the device, the app shows: _"Publish & send to client?"_ with a **Publish** action. Confirm to proceed. If something fails during the publish, the alert explains what: _"Could not publish —_ {reason}_."_
