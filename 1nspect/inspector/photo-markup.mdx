Photo markup lets you draw on inspection photos: arrows pointing at a defect, a circle around the issue, a rectangle framing an area, or a line showing a crack path. Markup is baked into the uploaded image so it travels through to the published report.

## Opening markup

Tap any attached photo on the Capture screen to open it full-screen in the markup editor.

## The screen top to bottom

- **Tool indicator** at the top — shows the currently selected tool and color (and a touch indicator dot that follows your finger as you draw)
- **Canvas** in the middle — the photo you're marking up; drawn markups layer on top
- **Tools row** below the canvas — the four shape tools: **Arrow** (↗️), **Circle** (⭕), **Rectangle** (▭), **Line** (📏)
- **Colors row** below tools — six color swatches
- **Edit actions** — Undo (removes the last markup) and Clear (removes every markup from this photo)
- **Main actions** — Save (bakes markup into the image) and Cancel

## The four shapes

- **Arrow** — point at a defect
- **Circle** — ring around a feature
- **Rectangle** — frame an area
- **Line** — straight line (useful for a crack path or an alignment issue)

## How to draw

Pick a tool, pick a color, drag on the photo. Repeat as many times as the photo needs. Undo removes the last stroke; Clear removes all strokes from this photo.

## Save

Save bakes the markup into the image. Once saved, markup becomes part of the image. There is no separate layer to turn off later; the photo on the report has the markup drawn in.
