Photo markup lets you draw on inspection photos: arrows pointing at a defect, a circle around the issue, a rectangle framing an area, or a line showing a crack path. Markup is baked into the uploaded image so it travels through to the published report.

## Opening markup

Tap any attached photo on the Capture screen to open it full-screen. The markup toolbar is at the bottom.

## Tools

Four shapes:

- **Arrow** — point at a defect
- **Circle** — ring around a feature
- **Rectangle** — frame an area
- **Line** — straight line (useful for showing a crack path or an alignment issue)

Six colors to pick from in the color row.

## Editing

- **Draw**: pick a tool, pick a color, drag on the photo
- **Undo**: last action
- **Clear**: remove every markup from this photo
- **Save**: bake the markup into the image

Once saved, markup becomes part of the image. There is no separate layer to turn off later; the photo on the report has the markup drawn in.
