The Ratings tab is the overview of your inspection: every area with its rating, its progress bar, and the editable system-info fields. Add area comments with library suggestions. Clean areas get a "no deficiencies" comment automatically. The Complete Inspection check runs from here (and from Review).

## Where to find it

**Ratings** tab in the bottom bar.

## What it shows

A list of every area in the inspection, with:

- Area name
- Current rating (from your SoP: TREC shows I / NI / NP / D)
- Progress bar showing how many required items are filled
- System-info fields for the area (equipment specs, locations, counts)

## Setting ratings

Tap an area to set its rating. The rating options come from your SoP. Tap to pick, tap again to change.

## System-info fields

Each area carries its own system-info fields: equipment type, manufacturer, model, age, location, counts. Edit inline. Smart fields (computed, like temperature rise or equipment age) update live as you enter the inputs they depend on.

## Area comments

Below the area header, tap **\+ Add Comments** to open the area comment editor. Add, edit and delete comments with library suggestions.

### "No deficiencies" is automatic

If an area has no deficiencies by the time you move on, 1nspecT automatically adds a "no deficiencies" area comment. You do not have to type it. This runs on the server when the inspection syncs.

## Completion check

The Ratings tab also carries a **Complete Inspection** action (also available on Review). It won't let you finish while:

- Any area that takes a rating is unrated
- A required field is empty
- Any finding without a narrative hasn't been resolved (drafted or signed off)

If any item is unresolved, the action reminds you with the specific count and the first blocker.
