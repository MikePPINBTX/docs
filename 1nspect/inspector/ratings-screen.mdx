The Ratings tab is the overview of your inspection: every area with its rating, its progress bar, and the editable system-info fields. Add area comments with library suggestions. An area rated I with no D and no findings gets a "no deficiencies" comment automatically. The readiness check runs from here (and from Review).

## Where to find it

**Ratings** tab in the bottom bar.

## The screen top to bottom

- **Area Ratings** — screen title at the top
- **Address subtitle** — the inspection property address
- **Rating System** — a quick-reference legend of the SoP's rating options (TREC: I / NI / NP / D with their colors and labels)
- **Area list** — one row per area in the inspection, in SoP section order. Each area shows its current rating, a progress bar for required items, and (expanded) the system-info fields and area-comment editor.

## What each area row carries

- Area name
- Current rating (from your SoP: TREC shows I / NI / NP / D)
- Progress bar showing how many required items are filled
- System-info fields for the area (equipment specs, locations, counts)

## Setting ratings

Tap an area to set its rating. The rating options come from your SoP. Tap to pick, tap again to change.

## System-info fields

Each area carries its own system-info fields: equipment type, manufacturer, model, age, location, counts. Edit inline. Smart fields (computed, like temperature rise or equipment age) update live as you enter the inputs they depend on.

## Area comments

Below the area header, tap **\+ Add Comments** to open the area comment editor. Add, edit and delete comments with library suggestions.

### "No deficiencies" is automatic (comment only)

When you rate an area **I** with no **D** rating and no findings, 1nspecT automatically adds the area **comment**: _"[area name]: inspected following current standards of practice, no deficiencies noted."_ You do not have to type it.

This auto-adds the comment only. **It does not auto-set the rating.** You still rate the area yourself from the mobile Ratings tab or the Capture screen. If an area is unrated, the completion check blocks finishing until you set its rating.

## Completion check

The Ratings tab also carries the completion check (also on Review). Only these block completion:

- Any subsection that takes a rating is unrated
- A required field is empty

A missing narrative is only a soft warning. The alert lists up to 15 items.

## Generate SPO

The screen offers **Generate SPO** (System Performance Opinion), which costs 10 AI credits.
