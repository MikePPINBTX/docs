The Home tab is the first thing you see after signing in. It lists your assigned inspections for the next 30 days with the client, the schedule, the template, and the status of each job. From Home you start an inspection, resume one you have in progress, join a teammate's team inspection, or jump to Field Quote.

## The inspection list

Each card shows:

- Client name (and co-client if there is one)
- Property address
- Scheduled date and time
- Primary service and ancillaries
- Inspector (you, or your name plus a teammate's for team inspections)
- Status (Scheduled, Ready to Start, In Progress, Completed, Published)
- Offline readiness badge

Jobs are listed earliest first. Pull down to refresh.

## Offline-ready badges

Each job tells you whether its template and libraries have been downloaded to the device:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready offline** | Template + caption library + narrative library are all cached. You can inspect this job without a connection. |
| **Needs download** | At least one of the three is not cached. Open the job once on cellular or Wi-Fi before heading to a dead-signal area. |

Tap into any job to download the missing pieces automatically.

## Start guards

A job won't start until these conditions are met:

1. The **agreement** has been signed by the client
2. A **template**, a **narrative collection**, and a **caption collection** are all assigned to the inspection on the web admin

If anything is missing, the Start button is disabled with a clear message naming the missing piece. See [Starting an inspection](/1nspect/inspector/starting-an-inspection) for how to resolve.

## Resume Active Inspection

If you have an inspection already in progress, a **🚀 RESUME ACTIVE INSPECTION** button appears at the top of the Config tab (and often at the top of Home). Tap to jump straight back into the Capture screen for that job.

## Team inspections

For jobs with more than one inspector:

- **The lead** opens the inspection and taps the team action to show a QR code
- **Each teammate** opens the app on their own phone, taps Join Team, and scans the lead's QR code
- Every finding records the inspector who captured it
- Ratings and comments are keyed per inspector so you cannot overwrite each other

The lead is marked as the inspector of record on the published report.

## Re-push

If an office admin re-pushes an assignment to your phone (for example, after fixing a template or re-assigning), the app re-acquires the inspection on the next sync. The job stays on your Home with its captured data intact. See the Office note in [First-run gotchas](/1nspect/getting-started/first-run-gotchas) if the re-push doesn't appear.
