Home is the first thing you see after signing in. It is a menu of four buttons: **Assigned Inspections**, **Emergency Inspection**, **View Completed Inspections** and **Field Quote**. The job list, readiness badges, the Team QR and Join live on the **Assigned Inspections** screen.

## The inspection list

On **Assigned Inspections**, each card shows:

- Client name (and co-client if there is one)
- Property address
- Scheduled date and time
- Primary service and ancillaries
- Inspector (you, or your name plus a teammate's for team inspections)
- Status badge: only **ASSIGNED** and **IN PROGRESS** appear
- Offline readiness badge

There is no guaranteed sort order. Pull down to refresh. Downloads happen automatically.

## Offline-ready badges

Each job tells you whether its template and libraries have been downloaded to the device:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready offline** | Template + caption library + narrative library are all cached. You can inspect this job without a connection. |
| **Needs download** | At least one of the three is not cached. Open the job once on cellular or Wi-Fi before heading to a dead-signal area. |

Downloads of the missing pieces happen automatically.

## Why a job might not appear on the phone

Jobs are hidden from the Assigned Inspections list when any of these are true:

1. **No agreement has been sent** (unless the inspection is marked externally contracted)
2. **The job is more than 30 days in the future** — jobs appear only within a 30-day window
3. **The inspector's display name doesn't exactly match the assigned name** on the inspection

If a job you expect isn't there, check all three. The display-name match is case- and whitespace-sensitive.

## Start guards

Once a job is visible, Start requires:

- A **template** and a **narrative collection** on the inspection (caption library is optional; if missing, the mobile autocomplete is empty but the inspection still runs)
- A **signed agreement**

If the template is missing, the card shows a "Setup Required" message rather than hiding the job.

See [Starting an inspection](/1nspect/inspector/starting-an-inspection) for the setup screen details.

## Resume Active Inspection

If you have an inspection already in progress, a **🚀 RESUME ACTIVE INSPECTION** button appears at the top of the Config tab (and often at the top of Home). Tap to jump straight back into the Capture screen for that job.

## Team inspections

For jobs with more than one inspector:

- **The lead** opens the inspection and taps the team action to show a QR code
- **Each teammate** opens the app on their own phone, taps Join Team, and scans the lead's QR code
- Every finding records the inspector who captured it
- Ratings are stored per area, not per inspector, so two inspectors rating the same area overwrite each other. Split the areas between you.

The lead is marked as the inspector of record on the published report.

## Re-push

If an office admin re-pushes an assignment to your phone (for example, after fixing a template or re-assigning), the app re-acquires the inspection on the next sync. The job stays on your Home with its captured data intact. See the Office note in [First-run gotchas](/1nspect/getting-started/first-run-gotchas) if the re-push doesn't appear.
