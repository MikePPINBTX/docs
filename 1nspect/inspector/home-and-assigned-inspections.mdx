Home is the first thing you see after signing in. It is a menu of three buttons: **Assigned Inspections**, **View Completed Inspections** and **Field Quote**. The job list, readiness badges, the Team QR and the team-join scanner live on the **Assigned Inspections** screen.

## The inspection list

On **Assigned Inspections**, each card shows:

- The inspection number (#id) and a status badge: only **ASSIGNED** and **IN PROGRESS** appear
- Property address
- Offline readiness badge
- **Client**
- **Scheduled** date and time
- **Template**
- **Notes** (when there are any)

There is no guaranteed sort order. Pull down to refresh. Downloads happen automatically.

## Offline-ready badges

Each job tells you whether its template and libraries have been downloaded to the device:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready offline** | Template + caption library + narrative library are all cached. You can inspect this job without a connection. |
| **Needs download** | At least one of the three is not cached. Open the job once on cellular or Wi-Fi before heading to a dead-signal area. |
| **Downloading…** | The missing pieces are downloading now. |
| **Setup incomplete — not assigned** | The office has not assigned a template or library yet. Downloading won't fix it; ask the office. |
| **Offline status unknown** | The app could not determine the job's offline status. |

Downloads of the missing pieces happen automatically.

## Why a job might not appear on the phone

Jobs are hidden from the Assigned Inspections list when either of these is true:

1. **The job is more than 30 days in the future** — jobs appear only within a 30-day window
2. **The inspector's display name doesn't exactly match the assigned name** on the inspection

An unsigned agreement doesn't hide a job. If a job you expect isn't there, check both. The display-name match is case- and whitespace-sensitive.

## Start guards

Once a job is visible, Start requires:

- A **template** and a **narrative collection** on the inspection (caption library is optional; if missing, the mobile autocomplete is empty but the inspection still runs)
- An agreement, depending on your policy (see below)

If the template or the narrative collection is missing, the card shows a "Setup Required" message rather than hiding the job.

Whether an unsigned job can be started depends on your [Signed agreement policy](/1nspect/features/signed-agreement-policy):

- **Signed before the client can view the report** or **Handled outside the app:** the agreement never blocks Start.
- **Signed before the inspection starts:** Start needs the client's signature in 1nspecT, a recorded outside agreement (**Signed by** and **Date signed**), or an exemption. Ticking **Client already contracted externally** without filling in who signed and when isn't enough. A blocked job is grayed out with an **Agreement Required** label, and tapping Start shows **Signed Agreement Required**.

A reinspection with no agreement of its own counts as signed when the original inspection's agreement is signed.

See [Starting an inspection](/1nspect/inspector/starting-an-inspection) for the setup screen details.

## Resume Active Inspection

If you have an inspection already in progress, a **🚀 RESUME ACTIVE INSPECTION** button appears on the Config tab and on the Quote tab. It is not on Home. Tap to jump straight back into the Capture screen for that job.

## Team inspections

For jobs with more than one inspector:

- **The lead** taps **Team QR** on the job's card in Assigned Inspections to show a QR code (the button appears only when team inspection is allowed for that job)
- **Each teammate** opens Assigned Inspections on their own phone, taps **⧉ Scan to Join a Team Inspection**, and scans the lead's QR code
- Every finding records the inspector who captured it
- Ratings are stored per area, not per inspector, so two inspectors rating the same area overwrite each other. Split the areas between you.

The lead is marked as the inspector of record on the published report.

## Re-push

If an office admin re-pushes an assignment to your phone (for example, after fixing a template or re-assigning), the app re-acquires the inspection on the next sync. The job stays on your Assigned Inspections list with its captured data intact. See the Office note in [First-run gotchas](/1nspect/getting-started/first-run-gotchas) if the re-push doesn't appear.
