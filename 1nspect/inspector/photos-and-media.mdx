Capture takes photos and up to 60-second videos directly from the Capture screen. Media is stored on the device and uploaded in the background. iPhone HEIC photos convert automatically. If an upload fails, a banner on the Review screen gives you Retry, Export and Delete.

## Photo capture

Tap the camera on the Capture screen. The native camera opens. Take the shot, confirm, and the photo attaches to the current finding as its main photo. To add more photos to the same finding, use [Extra photos](#extra-photos).

The media buttons are icon-only: 📷 (photo), 🎥 (video) and 📁 (import from the camera roll).

## Extra photos

A finding can have up to **4 extra photos** beside its main photo, such as a close-up or a wider context shot. You add them while you capture the finding, so you don't need **Save + New** or to find the finding again afterwards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take the main photo or video">
    A **＋ Photo** tile appears under the photo controls, with the note _"Add up to 4 more photos"_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap ＋ Photo">
    Choose **Take Photo** or **Choose from Library**. The photo joins a strip of thumbnails, and the note counts them (_"2 of 4 extra photos"_). Once there are 4 extras, the **＋ Photo** tile goes away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a thumbnail (optional)">
    A panel opens with an optional caption (for example, "Close-up of fitting"), **Markup** (or **Redo Markup**), **Remove** and **Done**. Nothing in it is required, and marking up an extra never changes the main photo's markup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the finding">
    **Save + New** or **Save + Finish** saves the extras with the finding. **Add to Existing Finding** attaches them to the finding you pick instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add extras to a finding you've already saved, open it from the Review screen with **Edit** and tap **＋ Photo**. This works only if the finding already has a main photo or video. A text-only finding doesn't offer **＋ Photo**.

When you edit a saved finding, its saved extras show first in the row with a green **✓**. They count toward the 4. Tap a saved extra to open a panel with:

- **Replace**: take or choose a new photo. It keeps the caption and position, but clears that photo's markup.
- **Remove**: after you confirm, it removes the photo from the finding and the report.
- **Close**.

This applies to extra photos only, not to locations added with **📍 Another location**.

Extras are printed with the finding on the report, the same as photos added with **Add to Existing Finding › 📷 Extra photo**. They save offline like any other capture and upload when you have a connection.

<Note>
  Extras are photos only, not video. **＋ Photo** doesn't appear on System Info tags or when you re-inspect a specific item from the original inspection.
</Note>

<Note>
  iPhone photos are HEIC by default. 1nspecT converts HEIC to JPEG when you import from the camera roll, so published reports render in every browser. You don't need to change your camera settings.
</Note>

## Video capture

Clips up to 60 seconds. In-app playback. Video attaches to the finding like a photo and plays in the published web report viewer via the Video Player.

## Add to Existing Finding

For situations where a new capture belongs with an existing finding, not on its own. Tap **Add to Existing Finding**. A panel opens below with two modes:

| Mode | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **📷 Extra photo** (default) | Attach an additional close-up or context photo to a finding that already exists. Add an optional caption. |
| **📍 Another location** | Record the same deficiency at a new location. The same finding card in the report groups the locations together. Needs a location typed as the caption; if you tap Save without a caption on this mode, the app prompts: _"Type the location as the caption, or choose 'Extra photo'."_ |

This button replaces the older **Location Tag** button; the behavior is similar, the UI shape is new.

## Photo markup

Tap **Edit** under an attached photo to open the markup editor: arrows, circles, rectangles and lines in six colors. See [Photo markup](/1nspect/inspector/photo-markup).

## Upload queue and background uploads

Photos and videos upload in the background as soon as the app has a connection:

- Over Wi-Fi by default
- Over cellular only if you turn on **Upload Media over Cellular** in Config › Sync Preferences

The upload queue runs even when the Capture screen is in the background. You can keep capturing while earlier media uploads.

## Failed uploads

If an upload fails, a banner appears at the top of the Review screen reading _"N Media file(s) failed to upload, tap to manage (retry / export / delete)"_ (where N is the count). Tap into it and the Failed Uploads screen opens. Per-item actions:

- **Retry** — queue it for another upload attempt (works once you have signal)
- **Retry All** — retry every failed item at once
- **Export** — save a copy to the camera roll
- **Delete** — removes the photo from the upload queue only

Nothing is silently lost. Keep capturing; do not fall back to your phone's camera roll.

## Automatic storage cleanup

Uploaded photos older than 15 days are removed from the phone to keep storage down. A storage report in Config shows how much space has been freed. Already-uploaded photos stay in the cloud and are served to the published report; the local copy is just a cache.

## Save to camera roll (company setting)

If your company has turned on **Save Inspection Photos to Inspectors' Camera Rolls** (Business Ops › Company Info on the web), every photo and video you capture is also saved to your phone's photo library.

This is a company-wide toggle, not a per-user one. If you want it on and don't have it, ask your owner or admin.
