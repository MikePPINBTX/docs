## November 2026 — Licenses, refunds, order History and reminders

### Reports

- **A finding's photos are grouped in a box.** When a finding has more than one photo, the PDF, the online report and the Report Workspace preview draw a border in your template's accent colour around them. In the PDF, the box is titled with the finding's headline. See [Template Designer › Photo groups](/1nspect/admin-operator/templates/template-designer#photo-groups).

### Licenses and certifications

- **Record every license an inspector holds.** Business Ops › Users has a new **License Types** button. Six types are built in (TREC, InterNACHI CPI, ASHI, TDLR Mold Assessor, NAWT, State), and you can add as many of your own as you need, for example a commercial license. Each team member gets a **Licenses & Certifications** section with number, issuer, expiry, **Primary** and **Show on reports**.
- **A tag for every license.** `$LicenseInterNACHI`, `$LicenseASHI`, your own `$LicenseCommercialPropertyInspector` and so on, plus `$InspectorLicenses` for all of them. They work in contracts, emails and on the report cover. `$InspectorLicense` now prints the license that applies to the inspection's type, so a commercial inspection prints the commercial license.
- **Published reports keep their licenses.** Changing an inspector's licenses later doesn't alter a report already out. A republish picks up the current ones.
- **Expiry warnings.** The Users list flags licenses expiring within 30 days, and admins see a renewal notice on the Dashboard.
- **Re-enabling a user keeps their details.** Re-enabling a disabled team member no longer clears their sponsor, home address and signature.

See [Licenses and certifications](/1nspect/features/inspector-licenses).

### Refunds

- **Issue Refund.** A new button on the order header and the inspection's Quick Actions, once paid. It refunds a card payment through Stripe, or records a refund you return yourself (cash, check, Zelle, Venmo). You can make several partial refunds up to the amount paid.
- **The report never locks again after a refund.** Each refund adds a History row and can send a **Refund Issued** email (`$RefundAmount`, `$RefundReason`). See [Refunds](/1nspect/features/refunds).
- **Refunds in your reports.** Monthly Reconciliation has a new **Refunded** column (also in the CSV) and, when the period has refunds, a **Refunded** card. **Net** and **Gross profit** are now net of refunds. Tax Summary revenue is net of refunds too. Each refund counts in the inspection's completion month, including card refunds made before November 2026.

### No charge (comped)

- **Comp an inspection.** Tick **No charge (comped)** in the Order Creation Wizard or in Add Service, or use **Mark as No Charge** in an unpaid inspection's Quick Actions. The inspection is $0 and counts as paid. The agreement shows the price and a **No charge** line, totalling $0.00. See [Managing orders › No charge (comped)](/1nspect/workflows/managing-orders#no-charge-comped).

### Quotes converted to orders

- **The order matches the quote.** Converting a quote used to drop its surcharges (mileage, multi-unit) and the excess square footage fee from the order price. The order now carries the full quoted total.
- **Converted inspections start as Pending**, like wizard orders, instead of Scheduled. The Dashboard no longer counts an unsigned converted order as contract-signed.
- **Older multi-phase quotes:** check each phase's price in Review & Send before sending. See [Quote page › Converting the quote to an order](/1nspect/features/quote-page#converting-the-quote-to-an-order).

### Google Calendar

- **Converted quotes get a calendar event.** Converting a single-inspection quote with an inspector and a booked date now writes the event to the inspector's Google Calendar, as wizard orders always have.
- **Reassigning moves the event.** Assigning a different inspector removes the event from the old inspector's calendar and adds it to the new one's, instead of leaving a duplicate.
- **Real event length.** Events last the inspection's estimated duration instead of a fixed 3 hours. See [Google Calendar](/1nspect/getting-started/google-calendar#what-syncs).

### Heating and cooling units

- **Each section keeps its own units.** Heating and Cooling now each print their own unit columns, with their own Location and Area Served, so one wall heater and three window units report correctly. Units are no longer copied into Duct Systems or Other.
- **Manage units in the Report Workspace.** **+ Add Unit**, **✎ Edit** (Area served and Location) and **Remove Unit N** sit on each section's Technical Specifications card. See [Report Workspace › Multiple units](/1nspect/admin-operator/inspection-reviewer#multiple-units).
- **Units on the phone too.** The unit strip on the Capture and Ratings screens adds and edits units, with **Remove** in the Edit dialog. It picks up edits made on the web, and alerts you if a change hasn't reached the cloud. See [Capture screen › Heating and cooling units](/1nspect/inspector/capture-screen#heating-and-cooling-units).

### Narratives

- **No R-values in insulation narratives.** The AI now recommends insulation "to achieve the recommended level for this climate region" instead of quoting an R-value. The recommended value is still shown in Property Baseline.
- **New review flag: "Passing finding is missing the action your note asks for".** It appears when a finding tagged Acceptable Performance has no Recommendation but your field note asks for an action. It's for review only and doesn't block Publish. When the note asks for nothing, the draft adds _"No action is required at this time."_ See [Pre-publish checks](/1nspect/workflows/pre-publish-checks#passing-finding-is-missing-the-action-your-note-asks-for).
- **More findings tagged correctly.** For example, a missing garage door emergency release, a water heater relief (TPR) discharge ending too high above the floor (scald risk), or blocked clear space at an electrical panel are now tagged as Safety Deficiencies, on every standard. "DIY" in a caption is tagged as Workmanship. Some tags depend on the report type (resale or new construction). A tag word you write in your field note still wins.
- **Tag phrases work in the caption too.** A tag phrase such as "(standards update)" in the caption now counts the same as one in the field note.
- **Finish & Draft skips a lone air temperature reading.** A supply or return air temperature with no note is no longer drafted on its own. The temperature-difference finding still gets its narrative.

### Mobile

- **"Belongs in" suggestion.** When a caption fits a different subsection than the one you're in, a **Belongs in:** chip under the caption moves you there in one tap, with Undo. See [Capture screen › "Belongs in" suggestion](/1nspect/inspector/capture-screen#belongs-in-suggestion).
- **Extra photos while you capture.** A **＋ Photo** tile adds up to 4 extra photos to a finding, each with an optional caption and markup, while you capture it or later from Review › Edit. See [Photos and media › Extra photos](/1nspect/inspector/photos-and-media#extra-photos).
- **Booking message fixed.** After booking with **Client already contracted externally**, the success message no longer says no confirmation is sent. Only the agreement is skipped.

### History

- **Order History works.** The order's History tab shows the order's own events plus every inspection's, with an **Inspection** filter and links back to each inspection.
- **Emails in History.** Every email shows as sent, scheduled, failed or skipped. A delayed email no longer shows twice.
- **More events, with names.** Report published and delivered, Finish & Draft from mobile, order created (web, mobile or from a quote), inspector assigned, reschedules and refunds are now logged, each under the name of the person who did it. Older orders aren't back-filled. See [Inspection Details › History](/1nspect/admin-operator/inspection-details#history).

### Reminders and rescheduling

- **Inspection reminders send.** Reminders are queued whenever a booking gets its date, and sent at the inspection's local time: set **Send Offset** `-24h` and **Offset Anchor** **From inspection date**. A reminder is skipped if the inspection moved or was cancelled.
- **Reschedule moves the reminder.** **Reschedule** now cancels the old reminder, queues a new one, fires **Inspection Rescheduled** and logs the change in History.

### Contracted externally

- **"Client already contracted externally" now skips only the agreement.** Confirmation, reminder, payment, review and report emails still go out, and the job still shows on the inspector's phone. Orders booked with the box before November 4 keep their old behavior: automated emails stay off, except report and review-request emails. See [Order Creation Wizard](/1nspect/admin-operator/order-creation-wizard#client-already-contracted-externally).

## November 2026 — Settings folded into Business Ops

### Business Ops

- **Settings is now part of Business Ops.** The separate Settings page and its top-nav item are gone. Business Ops has nine tabs: Company Info, Users, Billing, Pricing, Payments, Contracts, Communications, Quotes and Integrations. **Users** and **Billing** are visible to admins and owners only. Old `/settings` links open Company Info, and `/settings/billing` still opens Billing. See [Business Operations](/1nspect/admin-operator/company-and-pricing/company-settings).
- **Logo upload on Company Info.** Click **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo**) and pick a PNG or JPEG. The logo saves as soon as it uploads.
- **Preset themes on Company Info.** 1nspecT (Default), Professional Blue, Forest Green and Corporate Gray fill in your three brand colors in one click; Save Changes applies them, with no reload needed.
- **One White-Label switch.** **Enable White-Label Mode** on Company Info is now the only white-label setting, and it saves to your account.
- **Mark as Paid now emails a published report.** Recording a cash, check, Zelle, Venmo or offline-card payment on a published report sends the report to the client and agent, just like a Stripe payment. No need to click Deliver afterwards.
- **"Embed Your Wizard" removed.** The old embed snippet on the Settings page did not work and is gone. To put booking on your website, use the **Public Booking Form** embed (iframe) on Company Info. See [Company Info](/1nspect/admin-operator/company-and-pricing/company-info).
- The header palette icon (**Company & Branding**) now opens Company Info, and connecting a Google Calendar returns you to Business Ops › Users.

### Contracts and templates

- **Contracts Test Send emails only you.** It renders the agreements for the inspection ID you enter and emails the PDFs to the logged-in user. It writes nothing to the inspection and never contacts the client. See [Contracts](/1nspect/admin-operator/contracts-cockpit).
- **Template Preview works.** The Preview icon on a template card opens a read-only view of the template's sections, subsections and fields, with each field's type, whether it is required, and its options.

### AI credits

- **Clearer out-of-credit messages.** When you don't have enough AI credits, the message names the feature, what it costs and your balance, for example: "Not enough AI credits for Finish & Draft: it uses 50 and you have 12." It links to Billing and reminds you that purchased credits never expire. In the mobile app, Vision Scan shows this message with an **Open Billing** button; AI Generate shows an **AI Unavailable** notice and fills in a template narrative instead.
- Dashboard low-balance items now say "AI credits" and link straight to Billing.

### Mobile

- **Complete Inspection is now Readiness Audit (rolling out).** On the Review and Ratings tabs, the **✓ Complete Inspection** button is being renamed **✓ Readiness Audit** to match what it does: it runs the readiness check only. It does not complete, upload or notify anyone. Older app versions still show Complete Inspection until they update.

## November 2026 — Help center overhaul and audit catch-up

This release is primarily a documentation release that catches the help center up to the product state as of November 2026. Highlights from the past six months of product work covered in the refreshed docs:

### AI and automation

- **AI Insights** — Dashboard AI commentary from three advisor personas, refreshed at 6 AM, noon and 6 PM. See [AI Insights](/1nspect/features/iboss-insights).
- **Finish & Draft Report** — one-tap batch narrative drafting from the mobile Review screen. 50 AI credits per report. Standard workflow for most inspectors.
- **VoiceDNA** — the AI learns your company's writing voice from up to 8 sample reports. Future AI Generate calls match your voice. 50 AI credits per extraction.
- **Property Baseline** — pre-inspection snapshot of soil, flood, radon, termite and climate zone, plus construction-era materials. Sources: USDA NRCS SSURGO, FEMA NFHL, EPA Radon Zones, IRC R301.2(6), DOE/PNNL Climate Zones. See [Property Baseline reference](/1nspect/features/property-baseline).
- **Co-Inspector pass** — Vision Scan now runs a second-opinion pass that flags up to three additional findings per photo.
- **Specialist writing rules** — the Narrative Review Panel now flags SoP violations trade-by-trade (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, site and grading, gas, fire safety).

### Mobile

- **v2.4.0** — the current mobile version.
- **Add to Existing Finding** replaces the former Location Tag button. Two modes: 📷 Extra photo and 📍 Another location.
- **Multi-photo per finding** is now a proper first-class flow (no more typed-shortcut workaround).
- **Multi-instance subsections** — one subsection holds several instances of the same equipment (two HVAC units, three water heaters).
- **Reference snippets** — attach curated reference text to findings on the Capture screen.
- **Camera roll save** — company setting that saves every captured photo to the inspector's phone photo library.
- **Review screen** — the old Finalize Inspection button is gone; Finish & Draft Report is the standard path.
- **Inspection-aware autocorrect** — common voice-to-text typos (`freeze` → `frieze`, stutter cleanup) handled on top of your phone keyboard's dictation.

### Pricing and orders

- **Detached structures** — garages, workshops, guest houses as sq-ft-priced ancillaries. One row per structure on the order.
- **Billing recompute** — order edits regenerate the order's single invoice in place. A price drop only flags the order as needing a refund; Stripe refunds happen only from Cancel Order or Remove Ancillary.
- **Multi-unit surcharge** — supported on home-sqft primary services.
- **Order edit on signed orders** — Add Service without voiding the agreement; Resend Bundle handles the paperwork.

### Reports

- **All 9 financial and operational reports** are now available with CSV export: Monthly Reconciliation, Stripe Payouts, All Invoices, Outstanding A/R, Service Mix, Agent Referrals, Inspector Productivity, Activity Log, Tax Summary.
- **Dashboard tiles trimmed (#537)** — the AI hours saved tile and the NPS card were removed. The Dashboard now shows five KPI tiles: Revenue, Inspections completed, Average ticket, Collection rate, and Signed pipeline (with its own 7d / 30d / 90d / All toggle independent of the period filter). A Performance Metrics row (Ancillary attach, Quote close, Inspection time) sits below the tiles. Three charts (revenue trend, pipeline funnel, service mix) and the Action Items / Upcoming / Top Agents panels remain.

### Email delivery and bounces (#536)

- Per-recipient **delivery status** now appears on the Communications tab of each order (sent, delivered, bounced, failed).
- A **bounce banner** surfaces on Inspection Details when a client email bounced.
- A **bounce notice email** goes to the subscriber when a client email bounces, so you know to call the client.
- A **Dashboard alert** at the top of the home screen aggregates "emails didn't arrive" cases so you don't have to open each order to find them.

### SoP

- **SoP Manager** — browse platform SoPs, view ratings, clone to customize. See [SoP Manager](/1nspect/admin-operator/sop-manager).
- **Four platform SoPs** — TREC 7-6, InterNACHI, Phase 1 Pre-Pour, Phase 2 Pre-Drywall. Signup chooses your residential standard and seeds the matching template.
- **Reinspection rating scales** — each SoP ships its own; editable on a cloned SoP.

### Billing

- **Self-serve Billing page** (now Business Ops › Billing; `/settings/billing` still works) — plan change, credit packs, inspection packs (Solo only), seats.
- **Plans** — Solo / Professional / Team (names updated from Starter / Pro / Team).
- **AI Credits** is the rolling-out label for 1nspecTokens / IT. Billing already uses the new label; a few UI corners still show the old one during the transition.

### Integrations

- **Haven integration** (formerly HomeBinder) — tenant-level and per-order toggles for sending published reports to the client's Haven binder.
- **Climate zones** added to Property Baseline (DOE/PNNL), with recommended attic insulation for new construction per the 2024 IECC.

### Documentation

- **Help center refresh** — the help site at `help.1nspect.app` has been rewritten end-to-end to match the November 2026 product state. 150+ pages across Start Here, Daily Operations, Dashboard & Reports, Mobile, Templates & Libraries, Standards, Client Experience, Payments, Integrations, Admin Reference, and Troubleshooting.
