Plain-language definitions of every 1nspecT-specific term that appears in the rest of this guide. Sorted alphabetically.

### Add to Summary

The mobile button that flags a finding into the report's summary section. Use it for safety items and potentially costly repairs. Available on any finding on the Capture screen.

### Agreement

The signed service contract between your company and the client. 1nspecT has built-in e-signature, so clients sign in a browser and the signature is stamped onto the PDF. The agreement must be signed before the inspection can start on the phone.

### AI Credits

The metered currency for AI operations in 1nspecT. Each plan includes a monthly allowance; costs for each capability are in the [AI Credits reference](/1nspect/features/it-tokens). Previously called 1nspecTokens or IT.

### AI Draft

A web Report Workspace batch tool that generates narratives for every finding that has a caption and field note but no narrative yet. Costs 2 credits per drafted finding. See [AI Features](/1nspect/features/ai-features).

### AI Generate

The mobile button that writes a finding's narrative from your caption, field note and the inspection standard. Costs 2 credits text-only. When Vision Scan is armed, the same button runs the vision-aware draft for 8 credits.

### AIPI (Additional Information Provided by Inspector)

A reusable block of inspector-supplied information attached to a narrative collection. Some standards of practice allow or require inspectors to add context (installer qualifications, inspection scope caveats, product-specific notes) to the published report, and AIPI is where that content lives. Reinspection reports can carry their own AIPI with reinspection-specific limitations. Manage AIPIs in **Intelligence Hub › Narrative Management** and attach them to the relevant collection.

### Ancillary service

An add-on inspection service billed in addition to the primary service. Examples: Sewer Scope, Thermal Imaging, Pool and Spa, Mold Screen, WDI. Priced in **Business Ops › Pricing › Ancillary** and selectable at booking or quote time.

### Area comment

A plain-language note attached to an area of the report (as distinct from a finding). Areas with no deficiencies automatically get a "no deficiencies" area comment. You can add, edit and delete area comments on mobile from the Ratings screen and on web in the Report Workspace.

### CAR narrative

The recommended structure for inspection narratives: **C**ondition (what was observed), **A**nalysis (why it matters), **R**ecommendation (what should be done and by whom). AI Generate and AI Draft follow this format by default.

### Caption

The short (6 to 15 word) description of a finding that titles its card in the report PDF. The caption is also the input the AI uses when it drafts the narrative. Distinct from the **narrative** (full prose) and the **field note** (private observation).

### Caption library

The reusable collection of captions attached to a template. Appears as autocomplete in the Caption field on mobile and works offline. New captions you type save back to the library. Templates must have at least one caption library linked before the inspection will sync to the phone.

### Client portal

The customer-facing branded web page where your clients sign agreements, view invoices, pay, and open their reports. No account required: clients receive a magic link in their email.

### Co-client
A second client on the same order (typically a spouse or co-buyer). Has their own name and email, receives the same emails as the primary client, and signs the agreement independently.

### Co-Inspector

The second-opinion pass the AI runs after a Vision Scan. The model re-reads the photo and surfaces up to three additional issues it noticed. You accept each one into a new finding or dismiss it. No separate credit cost: it is part of the 8-credit Vision Scan.

### Collection (narrative or caption)

A reusable group of narratives or captions, typically scoped to a template, a property type or an era. The same template can carry several collections (for example, one for new builds and one for a specific era of homes), and you pick which one applies when you set up the inspection.

### Cover Designer

The visual tool for building branded report cover pages. Supports layers, frames, logos, backgrounds and dynamic text. Found at **Templates › Cover Designer**.

### Cover photo
The photograph displayed on the cover page of the report PDF. Inspectors set it on mobile from the Review screen; owners can also set it from the Report Workspace on web.

### Deficiency
A finding rated **D** per the TREC standard, or the equivalent in whichever standard the template follows. Generates a deficiency card on the report PDF with caption, narrative, photos and the section it belongs to. May also appear in the executive summary if flagged with Add to Summary.

### Detached structure

A separate building on the inspected property (garage, workshop, guesthouse, barn). Each detached structure is a separately priced square-footage line on the order. Set up in **Business Ops › Pricing › Primary Services** and attached per order.

### Era-typical materials

Site intelligence that lists the construction materials and techniques typical for a home of this age in this region. Shown on the order and in the web admin for the inspector to review before heading out. Not a finding and not shown to the client.

### Field note

A private observation the inspector writes on mobile. Separate from the caption and the narrative. The AI reads field notes when it drafts, but field notes are never printed in the report.

### Field Quote

The mobile Quick Quote screen. Lets an inspector price a job on site: address autocomplete pulls property details, services and add-ons are selected, and the price is calculated from your pricing rules. The inspector can text the client a quote link or book the inspection directly from the same screen.

### Finalize Inspection

The mobile action that locks the inspection once every rating is set and every finding has a narrative. Required before publishing from the field.

### Finding
A single record on an inspection: a captured observation of one condition. Carries a section, a subsection, a rating, an optional caption, an optional narrative, optional photos and video, an optional field note, and an optional summary flag.

### Finish & Draft Report

The mobile batch action that syncs everything to the cloud and asks the AI to draft every blank narrative on the server. Costs 50 credits per report. Most inspectors use Finish & Draft on the drive back to the office and polish from the web later.

### Haven

A third-party home-management binder for homeowners (formerly HomeBinder). When Haven is enabled on an order, the published report is sent to the client's Haven binder automatically.

### Insights (AI Business Insights)

Dashboard commentary written by the AI from three viewpoints: CEO (strategy), Operations (throughput and quality) and Finance (revenue and margin). Refreshes three times a day. Appears on the Dashboard. Included at no credit cost.

### Lead

A quote that has been created but not accepted. Leads appear in the Inspections and Quotes list and can be converted to an order in one step or marked lost with a reason.

### Library (narrative or caption)

See [Collection](#collection-narrative-or-caption) and [Narrative Library](#narrative-library).

### Location Tag

The mobile button that routes everything after a colon in your caption to a location field (for example, `Water meter location: left of driveway near street`). Also used to link a finding that repeats across several places: reuse the exact caption and the app will offer to link the second instance to the first.

### Multi-instance subsection

A subsection that can hold several instances of the same equipment (two HVAC units, three water heaters). Each instance has its own location and specs, and findings are tagged to the right one.

### Narrative

The full prose that explains a finding. The body text on the deficiency card in the report. Distinct from the caption (short title) and the field note (private observation).

### Narrative Library

Your company's collection of reusable narratives, organized into collections and linked to templates. Narratives you save on mobile or in the web Reviewer go to the matching collection. See [Narrative Library](/1nspect/admin-operator/intelligence-hub/narrative-library).

### Order
The master engagement record. Holds the property, the client or clients, services, pricing, scheduling and links to all child inspections, agreements and reports. One order can have multiple inspection phases.

### Phase (multi-phase order)
A child inspection within an order. Used for new construction (pre-pour, pre-drywall, final, 11-month warranty) or any staged project. Each phase has its own date, time, inspector and agreement where needed.

### Polish

The web-only AI pass that refines wording and tone. **Polish Narrative** rewrites a single narrative (2 credits). **Polish Report** rewrites the whole report with before and after review (10 credits per report).

### Property Intelligence

AI-generated summary of a property pulled from its address: age, square footage, foundation type, flood zone, radon zone, termite risk, climate zone and era-typical materials. Shown on the mobile setup screen and on the order in the web admin. Included at no credit cost.

### Public booking form

The embeddable booking page clients use to request an inspection from your website. Shows your services, prices, and inspector availability live, and sends the agreement automatically after booking.

### Quick Quote

The address-first mobile quoting flow (also called the Field Quote). See [Field Quote](#field-quote).

### Realtor portal

The agent-facing branded web page sent to the buyer's agent after the report publishes. Agents sign in with an email magic link and see every inspection your company has done for them. Read-only; no pricing or inspector contact details.

### Reference snippet

A short, curated reference text (for example, a code article, a manufacturer caveat, a safety advisory) attached to a finding. Managed in the Intelligence Hub and selectable from the Capture screen. Appears alongside the narrative in the report.

### Reinspection

A follow-up inspection to confirm whether called-out repairs have been completed. Created from any completed inspection, with a chosen scope (summary, requested repairs or full report) and a fee that can be set per inspector. Has its own rating scale (Repairs Complete, Not Complete, New Deficiency) and before-and-after photo pairs on the report.

### Repair request

The contractor-ready document a buyer or buyer's agent builds from the published report. They pick the deficiencies they want addressed, the AI drafts the request in a plain, actionable voice, and a PDF is produced. No AI credit cost: included on every plan.

### Report Workspace

The web page where you review, edit, polish and publish a report. Found at **Inspections › [inspection] › Report Workspace**. Includes ratings, narratives, photos, pre-publish checks and publishing.

### Review request

The automated email sent to the client after a report is delivered, inviting them to leave a review (Google, Yelp or any link you configure). Set up in **Business Ops › Communications**.

### Schema (template schema)

The structural definition of a template: its sections, subsections and fields. Edited in **Templates › Template Editor**. Distinct from the visual page design, which controls how that schema prints.

### Scan Label / Reading

The mobile button that reads data off a photo (an equipment nameplate, a thermometer, an app screenshot) or routes a typed `field label: value` into the matching field. Always shows a confirm screen before applying. Costs 3 credits (photo) or 1 credit (text only).

### Section
A major division of an inspection (Structural Systems, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Roofing, and so on). Each section gets its own chapter in the report PDF with a color-coded header.

### Smart Comment

A reusable, parameterized comment fragment used during template authoring or report editing. Managed in **Intelligence Hub › Smart Comments**.

### Smart Search

The mobile library-search button. Pulls narratives you have already written and refined from your library. Costs 1 credit per search. Best for your bread-and-butter findings: faster than regenerating and worded the way you already approved.

### SoP (Standards of Practice)

The rulebook your inspections follow. 1nspecT ships with TREC (Texas), InterNACHI and Phase 1 pre-pour. Each company can copy a standard and customize its rating scale in **SoP Manager**.

### SoP Manager

The web screen for browsing standards and rating scales, and for cloning a standard to customize its ratings (including reinspection ratings). Found at **/sop-manager** in the web admin.

### SPO (System Performance Opinion)
A section-level professional opinion that synthesizes all findings within a major system into one assessment (for example, Foundation Performance). Appears in the report summary. The AI drafts it on request; you can also write it by hand. Costs 10 credits.

### Standard of Practice

See [SoP](#sop-standards-of-practice).

### Subsection

The individual inspection areas within a section (for example, "Foundations" within Structural Systems). Each subsection gets a rating row on the PDF and holds any number of fields and findings. Can be marked multi-instance (see [Multi-instance subsection](#multi-instance-subsection)).

### Surcharge

An automatic or manual price adjustment on an order: mileage, age of home, multi-unit, crawlspace, and similar. Surcharges live in **Business Ops › Pricing › Surcharges** and only apply if they are switched Active.

### Supplemental document

A disclosure or reference PDF that attaches to the published report automatically by rule (based on year built, foundation type, property type or selected service). Managed in **Intelligence Hub › Narrative Management**.

### Team inspection

An inspection where two or more inspectors work the same job at the same time. The lead shows a QR code; teammates scan to join. Every finding records who captured it, and ratings and comments are keyed per inspector so they do not overwrite each other.

### Template

The reusable blueprint for an inspection: its sections, subsections, fields, rating scale and cover design. Templates follow a standard of practice (TREC, InterNACHI or custom). Build in **Templates**.

### Tenant

Your company's isolated account in 1nspecT. All of your orders, inspections, templates, narratives and settings live under your tenant. You never see data from other companies.

### Trigger event

An event the workflow engine listens for and can act on. The main triggers are booking confirmed, agreement sent, agreement signed, inspection reminder, inspection completed, payment received, report published, report delivered and review request. Configure the emails that fire on each one in **Business Ops › Communications**.

### VoiceDNA

The AI pass that learns your company's writing voice from sample reports (up to 8). Subsequent AI Generate calls produce narratives that match that voice. Owners and admins run VoiceDNA once per setup; costs 50 credits per extraction.

### Vision Scan

The mobile "co-inspector" mode. Vision Scan is not a standalone button: you arm it, then tap AI Generate, and the whole payload (photo plus caption plus field notes) goes up for analysis. The co-inspector reads the photo and flags additional findings you did not note. Costs 8 credits.

### White-label mode

The company setting that hides 1nspecT branding from your reports, emails, agreements and client portal, leaving only your logo and colors. 1nspecT has no white-label tier at the plan level; white-label mode is included on every plan.

### Workflow engine

The web admin subsystem that fires emails (or other actions) when trigger events happen on orders. Configure at **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow**.
