Google Calendar is the one integration most firms care about on day one. Connect each inspector's Google account and the public booking form will only offer slots they're actually free for. Every inspection you create is written to their calendar automatically. Reschedules update the event; cancellations remove it.

## Per-inspector connection

Google Calendar is connected per inspector. There is no company-wide calendar. Each inspector connects their own Google account. This is the setup covered in [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team).

1. Open **Settings** top-nav › **Users** tab
2. Click **Edit** on the inspector. The Google Calendar connect button appears only when you are _editing_ an existing user, not when creating one.
3. Under **Google Calendar**, click Connect
4. You are redirected to Google in the **same browser tab** (not a new tab); approve 1nspecT's access
5. Google sends you back to 1nspecT and the user shows the connected Google account

Each inspector controls their own connection. Repeat for every inspector.

A one-person firm gets "one calendar for everyone" by having that single inspector connect their own Google account.

## What syncs

Once connected:

- **Availability** — booking reads **every calendar** the connected user owns or can write to (not just the primary) and only offers open slots that fit the estimated duration, within the inspector's **Working Schedule**
- **Event creation** — a new inspection writes an event to the user's **primary** calendar, titled `Order No- {id}`. The description lists the clients, agents, services and fees.
- **Event updates** — rescheduling an inspection updates the event in place
- **Event removal** — cancelling an inspection removes the event

## What does not sync

- **Non-Google calendars** (Outlook, iCal, Apple Calendar) are not supported today
- **Edits to the calendar event in Google** are not pulled back into 1nspecT (one-way: 1nspecT writes to Google, not the other way around)
- **Private calendar events** show as busy for availability (the title and details stay private) but their time blocks the slot

## OAuth scopes 1nspecT requests

- `calendar.events` (create, update and remove inspection events)
- `calendar.readonly` (read your calendars to check availability)

## Troubleshooting

### Clients see slots on days the inspector blocked

The inspector has not connected their calendar yet, or the connection is to a different Google account than where the block is. Check **Settings › Users** on the inspector and confirm the connected email matches where the blocked time lives.

### Inspections aren't appearing on the calendar

Confirm the OAuth is still valid. If the inspector changed Google passwords recently, they may need to reconnect.

### Slots are missing at certain times

Each inspector has a **Working Schedule** (days and hours) in their Users profile. Booking only offers slots inside it. There is no per-user buffer setting. If expected slots are missing, check the Working Schedule and any events on any of their calendars.
