Google Calendar is the one integration most firms care about on day one. Connect each inspector's Google account and the public booking form will only offer slots they're actually free for. Every inspection you create is written to their calendar automatically. Reschedules update the event; cancellations remove it.

## Two levels of connection

### Per inspector (recommended)

Each inspector connects their own Google account. This is the setup covered in [Add your team](/1nspect/getting-started/add-your-team).

1. Open **Settings** top-nav › **Users** tab
2. Edit the inspector
3. Under **Google Calendar**, click Connect
4. Google's OAuth prompt opens in a new tab; approve 1nspecT's access to calendar availability and events
5. Confirmation shows the connected Google account

Each inspector controls their own connection. Repeat for every inspector.

### Single-inspector firms

A one-person firm gets "one calendar for everyone" by having that single inspector connect their own Google account on their user profile. There is no separate company-wide calendar to configure.

(The Primary Booking Calendar box in Business Ops › Company Info is not wired to anything today and will be removed in an upcoming release. Per-inspector connections are what drive availability and event creation.)

## What syncs

Once connected:

- **Availability** — booking checks free/busy on the inspector's calendar and only offers open slots that fit the estimated duration
- **Event creation** — a new inspection writes a calendar event with the property address, client name, service type and estimated duration
- **Event updates** — rescheduling an inspection updates the event in place
- **Event removal** — cancelling an inspection removes the event

## What does not sync

- **Non-Google calendars** (Outlook, iCal, Apple Calendar) are not supported today
- **Edits to the calendar event in Google** are not pulled back into 1nspecT (one-way: 1nspecT writes to Google, not the other way around)
- **Shared calendars** appearing in a connected account are not read for availability; only the primary calendar on the connected account counts
- **Private calendar events** show as busy for availability (the title and details stay private) but their time blocks the slot

## OAuth scopes 1nspecT requests

- Read calendar free/busy (for availability)
- Write calendar events (for creation / update / removal)

1nspecT does not read event content from your calendar; only the busy-time windows are read.

## Troubleshooting

### Clients see slots on days the inspector blocked

The inspector has not connected their calendar yet, or the connection is to a different Google account than where the block is. Check **Settings › Users** on the inspector and confirm the connected email matches where the blocked time lives.

### Inspections aren't appearing on the calendar

Confirm the OAuth is still valid. If the inspector changed Google passwords recently, they may need to reconnect.

### "Scheduling buffer" slots are showing up

Each inspector can set a scheduling buffer (minutes before and after each slot) in their Users profile. If their calendar looks fully booked due to buffer, adjust the buffer value.
