The Dashboard is the home screen of the web admin. It opens on sign-in and shows how your business is doing right now: revenue in the current period, signed pipeline, upcoming inspections, top referring agents, AI hours saved, and the iBOSS Business Insights panel with commentary from your three AI co-advisors.

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Dashboard** (first top-nav item). The URL is `/`.

## Period filter

At the top of the page, pick a time window:

| Window | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | Last 7 days |
| 30 | Last 30 days (default) |
| 90 | Last 90 days |
| all | All time |

Every KPI, chart and panel on the page reads from the selected window.

## KPI tiles

Across the top of the page:

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | Total revenue in the period, with a delta vs. the prior period of the same length |
| Inspections completed | Count of completed inspections, with a prior-period delta |
| Average ticket | Mean dollar value per inspection |
| Collection rate | % of billed revenue actually collected |
| Pipeline and active inspections | Signed pipeline dollar value and the count of active inspections |
| AI hours saved | An estimate of inspector-hours saved by AI drafting in the period |

## Charts

Below the tiles:

- **Revenue trend** — a line chart of revenue across the selected window
- **Pipeline funnel** — new inspections created vs. booked, agreements sent vs. signed, inspections completed vs. published
- **Service mix** — a doughnut of revenue by primary service for the period

## Panels

The bottom row of the page has three panels:

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Action Items | Links that open the Inspections list pre-filtered to things that need attention (unsigned agreements, unpaid orders, drafts waiting for review) |
| Upcoming today | Inspections scheduled in the current day with inspector, time and client |
| Top Agents | Agents by referral volume for the period, with revenue |

## iBOSS Business Insights

An AI-written commentary section with three cards, one per advisor persona (co-CEO, co-COO, co-CFO). Refreshes three times a day. Covered in detail in [iBOSS Business Insights](/1nspect/features/iboss-insights).

## Where numbers come from

Every number on the Dashboard comes from the same data your Reports read from. The Dashboard is the quick-glance view; the [Reports](/1nspect/features/reports-overview) tab is where you go for the full tables and CSV export.
