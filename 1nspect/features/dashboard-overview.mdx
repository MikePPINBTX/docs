The Dashboard is the home screen of the web admin. It opens on sign-in and shows how your business is doing right now: revenue in the current period, signed pipeline, performance metrics, upcoming inspections, top referring agents and the AI Insights panel.

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Dashboard** (first top-nav item). The URL is `/`.

## Banners and alerts

- **Plan-usage banner.** Shows when your plan's inspection or usage allowance is close to its limit.
- **"Emails didn't arrive" alert.** If one or more client emails bounced or failed to deliver, an alert appears at the top with a link to the affected inspection. See [Resend email](/1nspect/features/resend-email) for how delivery status works.

## Period filter

At the top of the page, pick a period:

| Period | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Week | The current week |
| Month | The current month |
| Quarter | The current quarter |
| YTD | Year to date |

The period filter affects the **top row of tiles only**. The trend and mix charts do not follow it.

## KPI tiles

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | Total revenue in the period, with a delta vs. the prior period |
| Inspections completed | Count of completed inspections, with a prior-period delta |
| Average ticket | Mean dollar value per inspection |
| Collection rate | % of billed revenue actually collected |
| Signed pipeline | Signed pipeline dollar value, with its **own 7d / 30d / 90d / All** toggle that is independent of the period filter |

<Note>
  The AI hours saved tile and the NPS card were removed. There is no "IT spent / remaining" tile on the Dashboard; see Settings › Billing for AI credit balance.
</Note>

## Performance Metrics

A row of cards below the tiles:

- **Ancillary attach**: how often ancillary services are added to inspections
- **Quote close**: how many quotes become orders
- **Inspection time**: how long inspections take on site

## Charts

- **Revenue trend**: revenue over time
- **Pipeline funnel**: new inspections created vs. booked, agreements sent vs. signed, inspections completed vs. published
- **Service mix**: a doughnut of revenue by primary service

These charts do not follow the period selector.

## Panels

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Action Items | Links that open the Inspections list pre-filtered to things that need attention (unsigned agreements, unpaid orders, drafts waiting for review) |
| Upcoming | Inspections scheduled for today and tomorrow, with inspector, time and client |
| Top Agents | Agents by referral volume for the period, with revenue |

## AI Insights

An AI-written commentary section with three cards, one per advisor persona. Covered in [AI Insights](/1nspect/features/iboss-insights).

## Where numbers come from

Every number on the Dashboard comes from the same data your Reports read from. The Dashboard is the quick-glance view; the [Reports](/1nspect/features/reports-overview) tab is where you go for the full tables and CSV export.
