When you text a quote from the field (via **Field Quote** on mobile) or send one from the web, the client receives a link to a branded quote page. The page shows the services, the itemized price, and an Accept action. One tap converts the quote to an order.

## Where the client lands

The URL is `/quote/[leadId]` or `/quote/[tenantId]/[leadId]`. The page carries your company branding: logo, colors, name.

## What the client sees

- Your logo and company name at the top
- The property address
- The itemized services with per-line prices and totals
- The grand total
- An **Accept Quote** action

If the quote has been accepted already, the page shows a thank-you panel with the client's first name (if captured) and the message _"Quote Accepted — Thank You, [first name]!"_

If the quote URL is invalid or expired, the page shows a Quote Not Found panel with your branding and a link to your main site.

## What happens on Accept

The quote converts to a signed order:

1. 1nspecT creates the order with the services and price locked in
2. The booking confirmation trigger fires (if your workflow has one)
3. The agreement goes out to the client (per your Contracts workflow)
4. The quote link now shows the accepted state

## When a quote expires

Quotes don't expire automatically today. If you need a quote to age out, delete the lead from the Inspections list. The quote link then returns the Quote Not Found panel.
