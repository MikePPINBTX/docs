Activity Log is the record of workflow and email events on your account in the selected period. It reads the workflow history only, so it shows automated workflow steps and emails, not every business event (it is not a full audit trail of user edits). It is capped at the **200 most recent** events. It is a company-wide report.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Activity Log** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Period dropdown (last 12 months, quarters, YTD). |

## What the report shows

Table with one row per event:

- Timestamp
- Order
- Type
- Event
- Actor

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. Filename: `activity-log-[period-label].csv`.

## When to use it

- **Dispute resolution** — "when did we send the agreement, and when did they sign?"
- **Support cases** — walk back the history on a stuck order to find the step that didn't take
- **Deep investigation** — paired with the Order History on an individual order, the Activity Log gives you the account-wide view
