Activity Log is the chronological record of every business event on your account in the selected period. Who did what, when. The report your support reaches for when a client disputes something, the one your lawyer asks for in discovery, and the one you reach for yourself when you want to understand why an order went the way it did.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Activity Log** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Date-range picker. |

## What the report shows

Table with one row per event, in chronological order:

- Timestamp
- Event type (booking confirmed, agreement signed, inspection completed, report published, payment received, order edited, order cancelled, etc.)
- Actor (which user triggered it, or "System" for automated events)
- Order or inspection ID this event belongs to
- Short description of what happened
- Target object (the thing the event affected)

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. Filename: `activity-log-[period-label].csv`.

## When to use it

- **Dispute resolution** — "when did we send the agreement, and when did they sign?"
- **Support cases** — walk back the history on a stuck order to find the step that didn't take
- **Compliance** — the per-event actor and timestamp satisfies most audit requests
- **Deep investigation** — paired with the Order History on an individual order, the Activity Log gives you the account-wide view
