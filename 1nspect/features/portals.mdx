The client portal is the branded web page your client uses from first touch to final report. Sign the agreement, pay the invoice, see payment methods, download the invoice PDF, and open the published report when it's ready. No account to create: the client reaches the portal through a magic link in their email.

## Where the client lands

The URL is `/client-portal/[portalSlug]`. The portal slug is derived from the client's email plus your tenant, so each client has a stable, personal URL. The portal link is created when the agreement is sent. An email carries the link only if its template includes the portal-link variable, so add it to every Communications template where you want the client to have it.

## Sections on the page

The portal has several sections for every stage of the inspection:

- **Agreement status**: each agreement with a Signed or Signature Required chip
- **Signing modal**: opens from a Sign action; the client reviews and signs in a draw-or-type signature pad
- **Invoices**: one invoice per inspection, with a **Download Invoice** button whose label changes to **Download Receipt** once paid
- **Payment options**: Stripe card payment if online payments are enabled; manual payment handles (Venmo, Zelle, Cash App) shown only when online payments are off
- **Published report**: a link to the report viewer when the report is published (and paid: reports are always locked until the order is paid)
- **Supplemental documents**: any supplementals whose rules match, offered as downloads (they are not merged into the report PDF)

## Signing an agreement

When the client clicks Sign:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review">
    The agreement opens in a Review step. The client scrolls through the full agreement text with their order details filled in via dynamic tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their name">
    Their name is pre-filled from the order. They can edit if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Sign mode">
    Two modes: Draw (sign with finger or stylus on a canvas) or Type (type their name in a signature font). Default is Draw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    The signature image is stamped onto the agreement PDF. The signed PDF is stored on the order; the agreement status chip flips to Signed. The Contract Signed workflow trigger fires once **every** agreement on the order is signed, not after each one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Co-client signing

If the order has a co-client, **one signature is required** to complete the agreement. The signing step has a **Who is signing?** toggle to choose between the client and the co-client.

## Paying online

If online payments are enabled (Business Ops › Payments connected to Stripe), the Pay action opens Stripe Checkout. On completion, the order is marked paid. If the report is not published yet, the Payment Received trigger fires. If the report is already published, the Report Delivered trigger fires instead, so the client gets the report email.

## Paying manually

When online payments are off and you have configured handles, they appear in the Payment section as one line of text. The client sees:

- Your Venmo handle (if configured)
- Your Zelle contact, phone or email (if configured)
- Your Cash App handle (if configured)

The client pays through the respective app, then your admin clicks **Mark as Paid** in the web admin (Inspection Details). Mark as Paid unlocks the report and, if it is already published, emails it to the client and agent.

## Agreement status chips

- **Signed** (green): the client (and co-client, where applicable) have signed
- **Signature Required** (yellow): the agreement is pending signature
- **Sign your agreement to unlock your report**: the report is published but locked until the agreement is signed, under your [Signed agreement policy](/1nspect/features/signed-agreement-policy). **View Report** is hidden until it unlocks.

## Portal link safety

The portal slug is non-guessable. Even so, treat the link as personal: anyone with the link can read the invoices and the published report (once unlocked). Clients should not forward it to anyone except their own co-client or agent.
