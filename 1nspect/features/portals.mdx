The client portal is the branded web page your client uses from first touch to final report. Sign the agreement, pay the invoice, see payment methods, download the invoice PDF, and open the published report when it's ready. No account to create: the client reaches the portal through a magic link in their email.

## Where the client lands

The URL is `/client-portal/[portalSlug]`. The portal slug is derived from the client's email plus your tenant, so each client has a stable, personal URL. 1nspecT emails the link in the booking confirmation and in every subsequent transactional email.

## Sections on the page

The portal has several sections for every stage of the inspection:

- **Agreement status**: each agreement with a Signed or Signature Required chip
- **Signing modal**: opens from a Sign action; the client reviews and signs in a draw-or-type signature pad
- **Invoices**: per-inspection and per-package invoices with per-row PDF download
- **Payment options**: Stripe card payment if online payments are enabled; manual payment handles (Venmo, Zelle, Cash App) if configured
- **Published report**: a link to the report viewer when the report is published (and paid, if your account locks reports until paid)
- **Supplemental documents**: any supplementals that attached to the published report

## Signing an agreement

When the client clicks Sign:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review">
    The agreement opens in a Review step. The client scrolls through the full agreement text with their order details filled in via dynamic tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their name">
    Their name is pre-filled from the order. They can edit if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Sign mode">
    Two modes: Draw (sign with finger or stylus on a canvas) or Type (type their name in a signature font). Default is Draw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    The signature image is stamped onto the agreement PDF. The signed PDF is stored on the order; the agreement status chip flips to Signed; the Contract Signed workflow trigger fires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Co-client signing

If the order has a co-client, each client signs independently from their own portal link. The signed PDF carries both signatures as separate signature blocks.

## Paying online

If online payments are enabled (Business Ops › Payments connected to Stripe), the Pay action opens Stripe Checkout. On completion, the Payment Received trigger fires and the order is marked paid.

## Paying manually

If you accept Venmo, Zelle or Cash App, your handles appear in the Payment section. The client sees:

- Your Venmo handle (if configured)
- Your Zelle contact, phone or email (if configured)
- Your Cash App handle (if configured)

The client pays through the respective app, then your admin marks the order paid in the web admin (**Inspection Details › Mark as Paid**).

## Agreement status chips

- **Signed** (green): the client (and co-client, where applicable) have signed
- **Signature Required** (yellow): the agreement is pending signature

## Portal link safety

The portal slug is non-guessable. Even so, treat the link as personal: anyone with the link can read the invoices and the published report (once unlocked). Clients should not forward it to anyone except their own co-client or agent.
