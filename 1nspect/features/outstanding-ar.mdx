Outstanding A/R is the report you open when you want to see who still owes you money, how long they've owed it, and whether you need to send a follow-up. Orders that were billed but not fully paid, bucketed by how long the balance has been open: 0 to 30 days, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, and 90+.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Outstanding A/R** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Date-range picker (defaults to all unpaid). |

## What the report shows

Stat grid with totals for each aging bucket: 0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, 90+, and the grand total outstanding.

Table with one row per unpaid order:

- Order number
- Client name
- Property address
- Invoice date
- Days outstanding (and the bucket it falls into)
- Amount billed
- Amount paid
- Balance due
- Latest action (last email sent, last mark-paid attempt)

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. The CSV filename is `outstanding-ar.csv`.

## Taking action

From each row you can jump to the order to:

- Resend the invoice email
- Mark as Paid for an off-Stripe payment that landed
- Open the order and apply a discount or write off (via Edit Order)
