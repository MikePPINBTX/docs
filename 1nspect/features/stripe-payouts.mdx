Stripe Payouts is a deposit-centric view of your Stripe money: each row is a payout that landed in your bank, and clicking into it shows the line-item transactions that made up that deposit, with per-customer fees. Use it when your bookkeeper sees a deposit on the bank statement and asks "what was in this one?"

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Stripe Payouts** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Date-range picker. The report uses a slightly wider window than Monthly Reconciliation because the underlying Stripe charges that make up a payout can span a few days before the payout date. |

## What the report shows

At the top, a stat grid for the period: total paid out to your bank, total Stripe fees, number of payouts.

Below, a list of payouts. Each payout row shows:

- Payout date (the date the money hit your bank)
- Expected arrival date
- Payout amount (net, after Stripe fees)
- Transaction count
- Status (paid, in transit, pending)

Click a payout to drill in and see every transaction that rolled up into it, with per-customer order number, charge amount, fee, net.

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. The export includes both the payout summary and the line items.

## Reconcile to your bank

Match each payout row to a deposit on your bank statement. If a deposit on the bank doesn't appear here, check your Stripe account directly (the Stripe Dashboard will have the full detail). If a payout here doesn't appear on the bank, it may still be in transit.
