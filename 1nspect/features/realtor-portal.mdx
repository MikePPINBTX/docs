The Realtor Portal is a branded, agent-facing web page where real-estate agents who refer business to you can see every inspection you've done for them. Agents sign in with an email magic link; no account creation. One place for an agent to see their book of business with your company.

## Where the agent lands

The URL is `/portal/[tenantSlug]`. Your tenant slug is derived from your company name. Share the URL once with your agents, or include it in email signatures and agent-partner materials. The page shows your company name and branding at the top.

## The magic-link sign-in flow

<Steps>
<Step title="Agent enters their email">
The sign-in panel reads: _"Enter the email address your inspector has on file. We'll send you a secure sign-in link."_
</Step>
<Step title="We send the link">
1nspecT sends a Firebase-powered magic link to the email. The link is valid for 90 days.
</Step>
<Step title="Agent clicks the link">
The link opens the portal with the agent authenticated. On subsequent visits inside the 90 days, the agent stays signed in.
</Step>
<Step title="The agent sees their inspections">
The page header reads **Your Inspections** and lists every inspection where the agent is listed as the buyer's agent on your orders.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Per-inspection detail

For each inspection in the list, the agent sees:

- Client name and property address
- Date of inspection
- Status (Scheduled, Assigned, In Progress, Completed or Cancelled), plus a **Report Ready** or **Report Pending** chip
- A link to the agent view of the report (`/report/{slug}/agent`)

There is no repair-request action on the cards. Agents reach the Repair Request Builder from the agent report view.

## What agents do not see

- Pricing — the agent sees the inspection, not the fee
- Inspector direct contact info — contact goes through your office
- Internal notes on the order

## When the magic link doesn't arrive

- Check spam / junk
- The email the agent entered must match the one on at least one order
- The link is one-time; a second request invalidates the first
