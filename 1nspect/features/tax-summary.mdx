Tax Summary is the report you hand your CPA at year-end. Annual revenue grouped by tax category, broken out by month. No fuss, no filtering gymnastics: pick a year, click CSV, send.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Tax Summary** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Year | Dropdown with the current year and the two prior years. |

## What the report shows

Stat grid with the year's totals: total revenue, taxable revenue, non-taxable revenue (pass-through), tax collected.

Table grouped by month, with:

- Month
- Taxable revenue
- Non-taxable (pass-through) revenue
- Tax collected
- Refunds issued
- Net revenue

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. The CSV is organized by month and includes a totals row at the bottom.

## For your CPA

This is a bookkeeping-grade summary, not a tax return. Your CPA will use it alongside your actual Stripe deposits, bank statements, and expense records. The Tax Summary tells them what you collected from clients; it does not calculate your deductions or your tax liability.
