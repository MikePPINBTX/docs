Tax Summary is the year-end revenue report. Pick a year and you get your gross revenue, inspection count, service mix and a month-by-month breakdown. Export to CSV and hand to your CPA.

<Note>
  Despite the name, this report does **not** compute sales tax. It shows revenue; your CPA handles the tax calculation from your deposit records.
</Note>

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Tax Summary** card.

## Filter

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Year | Dropdown with the current year and the two prior years. |

## What the report shows

### Headline stats

- **Gross revenue** — total billed revenue for the year, less refunds
- **Total inspections** — count of completed inspections
- **Service categories** — count of distinct service types sold
- **Avg per inspection** — revenue divided by inspection count

### Monthly table

One row per month:

- Month
- Inspections (count)
- Revenue (dollars)

### By-category table

Revenue broken down by service type:

- Service name
- Inspection count
- Revenue
- Share of total

## Refunds

Each inspection counts as its total minus any refunds, never below $0. The refund comes off in the inspection's completion month and under its service type, not the month you issued it. See [Refunds](/1nspect/features/refunds).

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download the report (monthly table + by-category table) in one file.

## What this is not

- Not a sales tax report. 1nspecT does not compute collected or owed sales tax here.
- Not a profit-and-loss statement.
- Not a 1099 summary.
- Not a refund list. Refunds reduce revenue (see Refunds above), but they don't appear as separate entries.

## For your CPA

Hand the CSV to your bookkeeper or CPA. They'll pair it with your actual Stripe deposits, bank statements, and expense records to produce your year-end books. The Tax Summary tells them what you billed; it does not tell them what you owe.
