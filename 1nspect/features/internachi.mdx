InterNACHI (the International Association of Certified Home Inspectors) is the national home-inspector association that most non-Texas US inspectors certify through. 1nspecT ships an InterNACHI residential template for subscribers who inspect to the InterNACHI Standards of Practice. The AI, the publishing checks and the printed layout follow InterNACHI by default.

## The SoP ID

InterNACHI in the code is `internachi_2024`. Its name on the Standards of Practice page is **InterNACHI** (the shipped version is dated 2024).

## How InterNACHI differs from TREC

The mental model is similar but the mechanics are different:

- **Rating scale** — InterNACHI uses a different rating taxonomy than TREC. Open **SoP Manager** › the InterNACHI card › **View Ratings** to see the exact scale shipped on your account.
- **Section structure** — InterNACHI's sections cover the same systems as TREC but with different section names and some different subsection groupings.
- **Narrative conventions** — InterNACHI narratives have their own tone and hedging conventions. The AI adjusts wording automatically when the template is bound to the InterNACHI SoP.
- **Reinspection ratings** — InterNACHI reinspection uses its own rating scale. See [Reinspection rating scales](/1nspect/features/reinspection-rating-scales).

## What's in the pre-built template

The InterNACHI template ships with the sections InterNACHI's standard requires: roofing, exteriors, structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, interiors, insulation, and so on. Each section's subsections are tuned to the InterNACHI subsection list, not TREC's.

## InterNACHI member ID at signup

If you sign up and select InterNACHI as your Standard of Practice, the signup form asks for your InterNACHI member number. The member ID appears on your subscriber record and can be included on your report cover and emails via dynamic tags. See [Signup](/1nspect/getting-started/welcome).

## Customizing InterNACHI ratings

The InterNACHI primary rating scale is locked on the platform SoP, same as TREC. If you need customized ratings, use **Clone to Customize** in SoP Manager to make your own copy and edit the ratings in your clone. Repoint your template at the clone.

## When to use InterNACHI vs. a state-specific SoP

- **Inspecting in Texas** → use TREC (the state requires it)
- **Inspecting in a state with its own required form** (Florida 4-Point, Louisiana, etc.) → check with your onboarding specialist about state-specific template support; the InterNACHI template is a common base to clone from
- **Inspecting outside the US or in a state without a required form** → InterNACHI is the standard choice
