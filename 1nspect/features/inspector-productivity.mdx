Inspector Productivity gives you the per-inspector view of your team's output: inspection count, revenue, average on-site time, and publish lag (how long from inspection complete to report delivered). Essential for multi-inspector firms.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Inspector Productivity** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Date-range picker. |

## What the report shows

Stat grid with totals and averages: total inspections, total revenue, average on-site time across the team, average publish lag.

Table with one row per inspector:

- Inspector name
- Inspection count (completed in the period)
- Revenue (dollars from inspections they led)
- Average on-site time (hours)
- Average publish lag (hours from inspection complete to report delivered)
- AI credits used (the inspector's share of the AI credit spend)
- Average AI credits per inspection

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. Filename: `inspector-productivity-[period-label].csv`.

## Reading the numbers

- **Count** tells you volume. Pair with revenue to see if the inspector is handling average, high-ticket, or low-ticket work.
- **On-site time** is useful for calibrating your Duration Formula (**Business Ops › Pricing › Settings**) if actuals consistently differ from the schedule.
- **Publish lag** is where backlogs show up. If one inspector's lag runs much longer than others, dig in: is it a tool question, a workflow question, or a review-queue question?
- **AI credits per inspection** shows how heavily each inspector leans on the AI. There's no right answer, but a wide gap is worth a conversation.
