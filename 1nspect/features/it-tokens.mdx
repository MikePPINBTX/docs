AI Credits are the currency 1nspecT uses to meter paid AI work. Each plan includes a monthly allowance; buy more any time. A handful of mobile capabilities are completely free. This page is the authoritative cost reference.

<Note>
  AI Credits were previously called 1nspecTokens or IT. The product is standardizing on "AI credits" everywhere. The Billing page uses the new name; a few UI corners still show the older "IT" label during the transition.
</Note>

## Where you see your balance

**Web admin:** Sign in as an owner or admin and open **Business Ops › Billing** (route `/settings/billing`). Under the Usage card, the AI credits section shows your remaining allowance for the period plus any purchased credits (which never expire).

**Mobile:** Inspectors see credit information only when a call is blocked for insufficient credits. A blocked Vision Scan shows the "Not enough AI credits" message with an **Open Billing** button.

The balance is shared across your whole company.

## What each AI capability costs

| Smart Search | Offline keyword search of your cached library | **Free** |
| Save to Narrative Library | Save a narrative back to your library for reuse | **Free** |
| Finish & Draft Report (batch) | Drafts every blank narrative in the report on the server | 50 credits per report |

## Your plan's monthly allowance

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of credits. Plan credits reset at the start of each period; purchased credits never expire.

| Plan | Monthly allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Solo | 1,500 credits |
| Professional | 3,000 credits |
| Team | 3,000 credits per seat, pooled across the team |

## Typical inspection cost

A standard inspection with moderate AI use draws roughly **95 credits**. Light-AI inspections (mostly library reuse, Smart Search which is free, text-only AI Generate on a few findings) run under 50. Heavy-AI inspections (Vision Scan on nearly every finding, every system opinion) run higher.

## Buying more credits

If you run through your allowance, top up without changing plans.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Business Ops › Billing (owners and admins only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the AI credits card">
    Under Usage, the AI credits section shows the remaining plan allowance for the period and any purchased credits (which never expire).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a credit pack">
    The top-up button is labeled **Buy 1,000 AI credits · \$19**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay through Stripe">
    Credits appear in your balance within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the Solo plan, the same Usage card also offers **Buy 10 inspections · \$49 (valid 120 days)**. It is always shown on Solo accounts, not only near the monthly inspection cap. Professional and Team plans do not need inspection packs.

## How deduction works

- **Pre-flight check:** before a paid call runs, the backend checks your balance. Most calls only need a balance above zero; Vision Analysis and System Performance Opinion need at least 10 credits, and Finish & Draft needs at least 50.
- **Charged on success:** most paid capabilities deduct after a successful call, and failed calls do not deduct. The exception is web **AI Draft & Polish**: its 50-credit drafting pass is charged up front when the run starts. That charge opens a drafting session for the inspection that lasts 2 hours; running AI Draft & Polish again on the same inspection within that window does not charge the 50 again.
- **Mobile free operations:** Smart Search, typed `label: value` reads and Save to Narrative Library do not deduct any credits.

## When your balance is low

Running low does not stop your inspection or your reports. AI is an accelerator, not a dependency.

| What you try | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Vision Scan | Blocked with "Not enough AI credits for Vision Analysis: it uses 10 and you have M" and an **Open Billing** button. Purchased credits never expire. |
| AI Generate | Shows an "AI Unavailable" notice and pre-fills a template narrative you can edit |
| Scan Label / Reading (photo) | Blocked; type the values into the fields directly, which is free |
| Smart Search | Not affected (free) |
| Typing a `label: value` reading | Not affected (free) |
| Publishing the report | Not affected; publishing never calls the AI |
| Taking photos, rating areas, editing | Not affected |

## Making credits last

- **Build your narrative library.** Mobile Smart Search is free, so a strong library covers most repeat findings at zero cost.
- **Type `label: value` captions** instead of photographing the plate when you can read the value yourself. Typed reads are free; photo reads are 4 credits.
- **Save Vision Scan for findings where the photo tells the story.** For routine deficiencies, text-only AI Generate at 6 credits is enough.
- **Use Finish & Draft on the drive back.** One 50-credit batch call is often cheaper than running AI Generate on dozens of findings in the field.
- **Polish the whole report once, not twice.**

## Where to see usage

- **Billing page** (Business Ops › Billing): your current plan, inspection usage this period, remaining AI credits, and seats.
- **Reports** (top-nav Reports item): the Inspector Productivity report shows per-inspector numbers (count, revenue, on-site time, publish lag).
