AI Credits are the currency 1nspecT uses to meter AI work. Every AI call (narrative drafts, vision analysis, polish, label reading, performance opinions, VoiceDNA) draws from your company's monthly allowance. This page is the authoritative reference: what each capability costs, what your plan includes, and how to add more when you need them.

<Note>
  AI Credits were previously called 1nspecTokens or IT. The product is standardizing on "AI credits" everywhere. The Billing page already uses the new name; a few other places (the Dashboard's AI tile, some mobile alerts) still show the older "IT" label. The counter and billing math are the same regardless of which label you see.
</Note>

## Where you see your balance

Your balance lives on the web admin. There is no credit counter on the mobile app on purpose, so field inspectors stay focused on inspecting.

**Web admin:** Sign in as an owner or admin and open **Settings › Billing** (the page lives at `/settings/billing`). The page title is **Billing**. Under the **Usage** card, the **AI credits** subsection shows the credits remaining in this period's plan allowance plus any purchased credits (which never expire).

**Mobile:** Inspectors see credit information only when a call is blocked for insufficient credits. The alert links to the web Billing page (owners and admins only).

The balance is shared across your whole company. Every inspector draws from the same bucket.

The authoritative cost reference. Capabilities marked **Included** draw no credits.

## Your plan's monthly allowance

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of credits. Unused plan credits do not roll over at the end of the period; purchased credits never expire.

| Plan | Monthly allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Solo | Included allowance (see Billing for current numbers) |
| Professional | Included allowance (see Billing for current numbers) |
| Team | Included allowance per seat, pooled across the team (see Billing for current numbers) |

<Note>
  Open **Settings › Billing** for your current plan's exact allowance. That page is the authoritative source.
</Note>

## Typical inspection cost

A single inspection usually draws between 30 and 150 credits. The range depends on how heavily you lean on vision and how much the AI drafts for you.

| Usage pattern | Typical total |
| --- | --- |
| Mostly library reuse, light AI | Under 50 credits |
| Standard: AI Generate on most findings, Vision Scan on 2 to 5, SPOs on major systems | 80 to 150 credits |
| Heavy vision: Vision Scan on nearly every finding, every system opinion | 200 to 300 credits |

## Buying more credits

If you run through your allowance, you can top up without changing plans.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Settings › Billing (owners and admins only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the AI credits card">
    Under **Usage**, the **AI credits** subsection shows your remaining plan credits for the period and any purchased credits. Purchased credits do not expire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a credit pack">
    The top-up button is labeled **Buy 1,000 AI credits** and shows the current price inline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay through Stripe">
    Checkout is handled by Stripe. Credits appear in your balance within seconds and the next AI call draws from the new total.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you are on the **Solo** plan, the same Usage card also lets you buy inspection packs when you approach the monthly inspection cap. The button is labeled **Buy 10 inspections** and shows the price and validity window inline. Professional and Team plans do not need inspection packs.

## How deduction works

- **Pre-flight check:** before a high-cost call runs (Vision Scan at 8 credits, VoiceDNA at 50), the backend checks that your balance covers it.
- **Charged on success:** most capabilities deduct after a successful AI call. If the call fails for a system reason, nothing is charged.
- **Fire-and-forget for small calls:** some 1-credit calls deduct asynchronously so they do not slow the UI.

## When your balance is low

Running low does not stop your inspection or your reports. AI is an accelerator, not a dependency.

| What you try | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Vision Scan | Blocked with an "insufficient credits" alert that links to the Billing page |
| AI Generate | Blocked; you can type the narrative yourself or pull one from Smart Search |
| Smart Search | Blocked; the library autocomplete chips (local, no credits) keep working |
| Scan Label / Reading | Blocked; type the values into the fields directly |
| Publishing the report | Not affected; publishing never calls the AI |
| Taking photos, rating areas, editing | Not affected |

Owners get a cap-alert email when the account reaches 80% and 100% of its allowance, with a link to top up.

## Making credits last

- **Build your narrative library.** Every saved narrative becomes a reusable library entry. After a few months, 60 to 80 percent of your common findings come from the library at no credit cost.
- **Use Vision Scan on findings where the photo tells the story.** If the finding is obvious from the caption, text-only AI Generate at 2 credits is plenty.
- **Finish & Draft at the end of the day.** One batch call is often cheaper than running AI Generate on each finding in the field, and it writes while you drive.
- **Polish the whole report once, not twice.** Polish Report is a single 10-credit call; running it twice doubles the cost without doubling the quality.
- **Save SPOs for the end.** Draft System Performance Opinions after every finding in that system is captured, so the AI has the full picture.

## Where to see usage

- **Billing page** (Settings › Billing): your current plan, inspection usage this period, remaining AI credits, and seats.
- **Inspection Details** (per order): the AI calls that ran against that order.
- **Reports** (top-nav Reports item): usage grouped by inspector and date range.
