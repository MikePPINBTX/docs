All Invoices is the simplest report in the suite: a flat list with one invoice per inspection in the period, with a PDF download on every row and a one-click CSV export of the full list. Built for bulk accounting handoff.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **All Invoices** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Period selector at the top of the report. |

## What the report shows

Stat grid at the top with totals for the period: **Invoices**, **Paid**, **Unpaid** and **Total Amount**.

Table below with one row per inspection:

- Invoice #
- Generated
- Client
- Property
- Amount
- Status (paid or unpaid; there is no partial state)
- PDF

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to export the full list. Column headers match the on-screen table.

## How invoices are generated

There is one invoice per inspection, regenerated in place when the order changes. There are no top-up or incremental invoices. See [Invoices](/1nspect/features/invoices) and [Payments setup](/1nspect/admin-operator/payments-and-contracts) for payment configuration.
