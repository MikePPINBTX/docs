Texas Real Estate Commission REI 7-6 is the standardized property-inspection report form every licensed Texas inspector must deliver. 1nspecT's pre-built TREC template mirrors the official form's section structure and rating model. The AI, the publishing checks, the printed layout, and the rating scale all follow the TREC standard by default. This page is for subscribers on TREC.

## The SoP ID

TREC in the code is `trec_7_6`. Its official name on the Standards of Practice page is **Texas TREC REI 7-6**. The current version follows the TREC form published in 2020 with subsequent revisions.

## The rating scale

TREC uses a four-option rating scale, one per subsection:

| Code | Label | Deficiency? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I** | Inspected | No |
| **NI** | Not Inspected | No |
| **NP** | Not Present | No |
| **D** | Deficient | Yes |

A single subsection can be rated both **I** and **D** — the inspector inspected it and found a deficiency. The dual rating is explicit TREC behavior and is supported on mobile and in the Report Workspace.

## What's in the pre-built template

The TREC 7-6 PPI template that ships with your account carries every section and subsection required by the official form:

- Structural Systems (Foundations, Grading, Roof Covering, etc.)
- Electrical Systems
- Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning Systems
- Plumbing Systems
- Appliances
- Optional Systems (fences, pool and spa, outbuildings — covered when the service is on the order)

Each subsection carries the system-info fields TREC requires and the rating options above.

## What keeps reports TREC-compliant

Beyond the schema itself, several checks run before you can publish a TREC report:

- **The completeness gate** blocks Publish until every required rating is set and every required field is filled
- **The narrative review panel** flags narratives that violate TREC writing rules (unsupported numeric claims, overclaimed causation, dropped hedging)
- **The specialist writing rules** adjust wording per trade (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, gas, fire safety)
- **The computed-not-guessed rule** means the AI only words what your captured readings prove — it cannot invent an amperage or a temperature

See [Pre-publish checks](/1nspect/workflows/pre-publish-checks) for the full gate.

## Customizing TREC ratings

The TREC rating scale is **locked** on the platform SoP (`primaryRatingsLocked: true`) to keep you compliant with the official standard. If you need customized ratings (for example, to add a company-specific rating code), you can:

1. Open **SoP Manager** (top-nav)
2. Click **Clone to Customize** on the TREC card
3. Name your clone (default: `Texas TREC REI 7-6 (Custom)`)
4. Edit the ratings in your clone
5. Point your template at the clone instead of the platform SoP

The platform TREC SoP stays untouched. Your clone is yours to maintain.

## Reinspection ratings on TREC

The TREC reinspection scale is also set by the SoP. Platform TREC uses **Repairs Complete**, **Repairs Not Complete**, and **New Deficiency**. Your custom clone can edit these.

See [Reinspection rating scales](/1nspect/features/reinspection-rating-scales).
