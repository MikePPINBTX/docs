VoiceDNA is the AI feature that learns your company's writing voice from a handful of past reports and uses it on every future AI Generate call. The AI stops sounding generic and starts sounding like you wrote it. One-time setup, 50 AI credits per extraction, admins and owners only.

## Where to find it

**Narrative Management** top-nav › **Intelligence & Library Hub** › the **AI Persona (Voice DNA)** tab (third tab).

The VoiceDNA page is owner- and admin-only. Inspectors see the tab but it's locked for them.

## What's on the page

The page is a stack of separate cards.

### Include Analysis in narratives

Whether narratives include a dedicated Analysis section between Condition and Recommendation.

### Narrative format in reports

How narratives are laid out when the report is displayed. Choose **Labelled parts (Condition / Analysis / Recommendation)**, **Bullet points, no labels** or **One paragraph**. A preview shows the result. This is a display setting: it changes how narratives appear in reports, not how the AI writes them.

### Active Voice DNA Profile

Shows your current profile (if you have one). An **Edit Voice DNA Profile** button opens the writing-preferences editor. Empty state (no profile yet) shows: _"No Voice DNA profile set. Upload reports below to extract your writing style."_

### Writing Preferences

Independent of the extracted profile; works even with no profile set. Saved through its own endpoint. Tune:

- **Tone** (how formal or informal the drafts should read)
- **Bolding usage**
- **Narrative length** (tenant-level axis the AI reads at draft time)
- **Regional terms**
- **Style notes**

The writing preferences layer on top of the extracted voice: the profile sets the voice; the preferences shape how it comes out on each draft.

### Upload area

Where you drop sample reports to extract a profile (see below).

## Extracting a profile

<Steps>
<Step title="Gather sample reports">
Pick up to 8 PDF reports your company has published. More variety (resale, pre-pour, pre-drywall, 11-month warranty) gives the AI a better read on your range.
</Step>
<Step title="Upload the PDFs">
Drag the files into the upload zone on the AI Persona tab. The page shows a progress indicator while uploading.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Extract Voice DNA">
The button reads **Extract Voice DNA (50 AI credits)**. On click, the AI analyzes the uploads and extracts your writing style.
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for extraction">
The progress label reads _"Extracting… (this may take up to 2 minutes)."_ The AI reads narrative structure, sentence patterns, hedging conventions, trade terminology, and the quirks of how you word findings.
</Step>
<Step title="Profile is active">
Once extraction completes, the Active Voice DNA Profile section updates. Every future AI Generate call reads from this profile.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Cost

**50 AI credits** per extraction. The one-time cost is justified by the depth of analysis the AI runs over up to 8 full PDF reports.

## When to re-extract

- You hired new inspectors with a markedly different writing voice and want the average
- You changed your SoP (TREC to InterNACHI, for example) and want the voice recalibrated to the new SoP's language
- The AI output started feeling off and you suspect the profile has drifted

Re-extraction replaces the current profile; the old one is not kept.

## How the profile affects AI output

For every subsequent AI Generate, AI Draft, and Polish call:

- The server reads the tenant's active VoiceDNA profile
- The profile becomes part of the system instruction for the AI call
- The returned narrative matches your voice (sentence structure, vocabulary, hedging, length preferences)

Smart Search is unaffected: it pulls narratives you wrote, so it already sounds like you.

## What VoiceDNA is not

- Not a style transfer for existing narratives (it affects new drafts only; it does not re-polish past reports)
- Not a template editor (it does not change your schema or sections)
- Not a per-inspector profile (it is tenant-wide; one profile per company)
- Not a replacement for Polish (you can still Polish any narrative to refine the AI's take)
