Haven (formerly HomeBinder) is a third-party home-management service that gives homeowners a lifetime binder for their home: maintenance reminders, documents, photos, and inspection reports. When your company has Haven set up and enabled on an order, the published inspection report lands in the client's Haven binder automatically alongside the normal delivery.

## What your client experiences

If the order has Haven enabled, when the report is published the client receives:

- The normal 1nspecT delivery (email with the report link to the mobile-first web viewer and PDF)
- **Plus** a Haven email with their new home binder set up, containing the published report

From the Haven binder, the client can also store receipts, warranty documents, maintenance reminders, and photos for the life of the home. Haven stays active even if they sell or move: Haven is their account, not yours.

## Where it's turned on

- **Tenant level**: enabled in **Business Ops › Integrations**. Covered in [Haven integration](/1nspect/features/haven-integration).
- **Per order**: a per-order **Send to Haven on publish** toggle appears on the Inspection Details page when Haven is enabled at the tenant level. Defaults to on; uncheck to skip for a specific order.

## When it fires

Automatically on Publish, as part of the Report Published workflow trigger. No separate action from the subscriber or the client.

## When it doesn't fire

- The tenant-level switch is off
- The per-order toggle has been unchecked
- The order has no Haven-eligible client email
