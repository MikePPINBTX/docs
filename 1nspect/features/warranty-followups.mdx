For new-construction clients, the one-year builder's warranty is where repeat business lives. An 11-month warranty inspection catches defects before the warranty expires so the builder has to fix them. Clients value it. The firm that reminds them to book it is usually the firm that gets the job. This page is the playbook for setting up the automated warranty reminder.

## The pattern

Automate one or more follow-up emails after the final new-construction inspection, offering the client an 11-month warranty inspection before the builder's warranty expires. Timing, cadence and wording are entirely up to you: pick what fits your voice and your market. Common approaches:

- A single, well-timed reminder a few months before the warranty expires
- A gentle check-in earlier (building rapport) followed by a clearer ask closer to the warranty expiry
- A soft confirmation if the warranty inspection was already baked into the original new-construction order as a scheduled phase

Pick whatever cadence fits; nothing in 1nspecT is hard-coded.

## Setting up a warranty follow-up workflow step

For each timing you want:

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the email template">
Business Ops › Communications › Email Templates. Add a new template with a warm subject and body. Reference the specific property using `$InspectionAddress`, offer the next step with your `$BookingLink`.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the workflow step">
Business Ops › Communications › Workflow. Add a step with:

- **Trigger**: Inspection Completed (the parent new-construction final inspection)
- **Template**: your warranty-reminder template
- **Recipient**: Primary client (and co-client if you want)
- **Delay**: however many days or months after the parent inspection you want the email to send
- **Enabled**: on
</Step>
<Step title="Scope to new construction (recommended)">
If your workflow engine supports it, scope the step to orders where the primary service is new-construction (not resale). Otherwise, every inspection triggers the warranty email, which is noise for resale clients.
</Step>
</Steps>

## When the client books

The 11-month warranty inspection is a separate order. Clients can:

- Click the booking link in the email → book themselves via the public booking form
- Call or email your office → you create the order in the Order Creation Wizard

For the inspection itself, add 11-month warranty as a primary service in **Business Ops › Pricing › Primary Services**. Set its Inspection Type to **11-month warranty** so the AI's narrative framing adjusts (less about new-build quality concerns, more about settling and expiration-driven items).

## Pricing the warranty inspection

Most firms price the 11-month warranty inspection at a discount from a full inspection (it's often shorter in scope). Set the price on the primary service in Pricing Manager:

- Flat Fee pricing is common for warranty inspections
- Or SqFt-Based with a lower per-tier price than the standard resale inspection

## Multi-phase order with warranty built in

For firms that want to lock in the warranty inspection at the time of the original new-construction order, add the 11-month warranty as a phase of the multi-phase order (Pre-Pour → Pre-Drywall → Final → 11-month warranty). The client pays upfront, the warranty inspection is scheduled a year out, and the follow-up email can be a soft confirmation rather than a sales ask.
