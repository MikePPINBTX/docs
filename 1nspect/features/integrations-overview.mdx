1nspecT connects to a handful of outside services for payments, scheduling, delivery, address and property data, and email. Most are either already connected for you (data sources, Resend) or wired through a simple one-time setup (Stripe Connect, Google Calendar, Haven). This page is the map; each integration has its own detail page.

## The seven integrations

| Integration | What it does for you | Where to connect it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stripe Connect** | Card payments paid straight into your own Stripe account, with refunds and payout reconciliation | Business Ops › Payments. See [Payments setup](/1nspect/admin-operator/payments-and-contracts). |
| **Google Calendar** | Each inspector's calendar drives booking availability; inspection events are created and updated automatically | Settings › Users (per inspector). See [Google Calendar](/1nspect/features/calendar-integration). |
| **Haven** (formerly HomeBinder) | Published reports delivered to the client's Haven home binder | Business Ops › Integrations. See [Haven integration](/1nspect/features/haven-integration). |
| **Google Maps and Places** | Address autocomplete, geocoding, mileage surcharge calculation | Always on. See [Google Maps and Places](/1nspect/features/google-maps-places). |
| **Data sources** | Soil (USDA NRCS SSURGO), flood (FEMA NFHL), radon (EPA), termite (IRC R301.2(6)), climate zone (DOE/PNNL) | Always on. See [Data sources](/1nspect/features/data-sources). |
| **Resend email** | Branded transactional and automated emails sent in your company's name | Always on. See [Resend email](/1nspect/features/resend-email). |
| **InterNACHI** | Member ID captured at signup; appears on reports via dynamic tag | Signup form only. See [InterNACHI](/1nspect/features/internachi). |

## What's not supported today

1nspecT does not yet connect to:

- **Outlook or iCal calendars** — Google Calendar only
- **SMS or push notification services** — email automation only
- **QuickBooks or other accounting software** — reconciliation is through the Reports CSV export instead
- **Third-party e-signature services** (DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, Adobe Sign) — 1nspecT's own e-signature is built in

If any of these are on your must-have list, let your onboarding specialist know.
