A Standard of Practice (SoP) is the rulebook your inspections follow. It defines the rating scale, the layout family for printed reports, and the AI narrative language. 1nspecT ships with four SoPs ready to use: Texas TREC, InterNACHI, Phase 1 Pre-Pour, and Phase 2 Pre-Drywall. On Professional and Team plans you can clone any of these into a custom SoP (Solo 0, Professional 1, Team 3).

## The four platform SoPs

| SoP | ID | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Texas TREC REI 7-6** | `trec_7_6` | Texas Real Estate Commission residential property inspection form. See [TREC](/1nspect/features/trec-sop-compliance). |
| **InterNACHI** | `internachi_2024` | International Association of Certified Home Inspectors residential standard. See [InterNACHI](/1nspect/features/internachi). |
| **Phase 1 Pre-Pour Foundation** | `phase_1_pre_pour` | New-construction foundation inspection before the slab is poured. See [Phase inspections](/1nspect/features/phase-inspections). |
| **Phase 2 Pre-Drywall** | `phase_2_pre_drywall` | New-construction framing and systems inspection before drywall. See [Phase inspections](/1nspect/features/phase-inspections). |

## What an SoP controls

Every SoP defines:

- **The rating scale** (for example TREC's I / NI / NP / D) with each rating's label and whether it counts as a deficiency
- **The reinspection rating scale** (for example Repairs Complete / Repairs Not Complete / New Deficiency)
- **The report layout family** (which Section and Summary Blueprints are allowed)
- **The AI narrative language** (what tone, hedging, trade terminology to use when drafting)
- **The specialist writing rules** (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, site and grading, gas and fire safety all get their own)
- **The required items** that gate publishing

## How templates bind to SoPs

Every template points to one SoP through its `sopId` field. Open a template in the Universal Template Builder and the SoP badge on the card shows which one it uses. The template's schema is tuned to that SoP's required items; its layout picker is limited to the Blueprints the SoP allows.

## Seeded templates per subscriber

When you sign up, 1nspecT reads your Standard of Practice choice from the signup form and seeds the matching residential template on your account, plus the two Phase templates:

| You chose at signup | You get |
| --- | --- |
| TREC | TREC 7-6 residential template + Phase 1 Pre-Pour + Phase 2 Pre-Drywall |
| InterNACHI | InterNACHI residential template + Phase 1 Pre-Pour + Phase 2 Pre-Drywall |
| Other | InterNACHI residential template + Phase 1 Pre-Pour + Phase 2 Pre-Drywall (the platform team follows up about support for your standard) |

## Platform vs. tenant SoPs

SoPs come in two kinds:

- **Platform SoPs** — the four shipped standards. Primary ratings are locked on TREC and InterNACHI to keep you compliant with the official standard (and on their clones too). Phase SoPs are not locked.
- **Tenant SoPs** — SoPs you cloned from a platform SoP via Clone to Customize. Clones inherit narrative rules live from the base (#513). Use **Edit Ratings** to change the editable set (the reinspection ratings on TREC and InterNACHI clones). Managed in SoP Manager.

## Clone to customize

From **SoP Manager** (top-nav), pick a platform SoP and click **Clone to Customize**. Give the clone a name (the default appends `(Custom)`), click **Clone**, then use **Edit Ratings** on the clone. Templates can be re-pointed from the platform SoP to your clone.

Custom-SoP cloning is a Professional and Team feature. There is no free tier; on Solo the Standards of Practice page shows every platform SoP as viewable and usable, with a note that customization requires an upgrade.
