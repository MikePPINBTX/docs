1nspecT uses Google Maps and Google Places for every address-related interaction: autocompletion in the booking form, geocoding to pin a property on a map, and distance calculation for mileage surcharges. The integration is always on for every subscriber; there's nothing to connect or configure.

## Where you see it in use

| Where | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Public booking form | Address autocompletes as the client types |
| Order Creation Wizard | Address autocomplete and auto-fill of property details |
| Mobile Field Quote | Address autocomplete and Property Baseline lookup |
| Mileage surcharge calculation | Distance from the inspector's home address (set in Settings › Users) to the property, falling back to the office address; public bookings use the office address |
| Property Baseline | Lat/lng used to look up site conditions (soil, flood, radon, termite, climate) |

## What this means for you

Address fields across the product behave like Google's standard address input. Start typing and 1nspecT suggests valid addresses with their full postal form. Picking a suggestion fills in city, state, and ZIP without re-entry.

## Mileage calculation

For orders where a mileage surcharge applies:

1. 1nspecT geocodes the inspector's home address (set in Settings › Users under Mileage Calculation), or the office address from Company Info if no home address is set. Public bookings always use the office address
2. 1nspecT geocodes the property address on the order
3. The Google Maps distance (driving distance, round trip if your surcharge is set to Round Trip ×2) is calculated
4. The mileage surcharge is applied to the order per your Surcharges tab configuration

See [Primary services](/1nspect/getting-started/primary-services) and [Packages, discounts and surcharges](/1nspect/getting-started/packages-discounts-surcharges) for mileage surcharge setup.

## Rate limits and cost

Google Maps and Places have per-month quotas and per-call costs. 1nspecT pays for these as part of your subscription; you don't pay Google directly, and there's no per-call fee passed to you.

## Fallback when autocomplete isn't available

If the Google service is unavailable (brief outage, network issue), address fields fall back to free text. The client or admin can type the full address manually; it saves correctly but doesn't auto-fill extra details.
