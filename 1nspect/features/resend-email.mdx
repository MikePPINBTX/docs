Every automated email 1nspecT sends (booking confirmation, agreement sent, payment receipt, report delivered, review request, warranty follow-up) goes through Resend, a transactional email service. Emails come from a 1nspecT-managed sender address but carry your company's branding (logo, name, colors). Workflow emails have **no reply-to**, so replies do not land in your inbox. Always on, nothing for subscribers to configure.

## What goes through Resend

- Workflow emails (every trigger in **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow**)
- Agreement emails (sent via Business Ops › Contracts)
- Agent magic-link emails (Realtor Portal sign-in links)
- Client portal link emails
- Report delivery emails
- Review request emails
- Warranty follow-up emails

## What doesn't go through Resend

- SMS messages (not supported today)
- Push notifications (not supported today)
- Haven emails to clients (Haven sends those directly from Haven)

## Who the emails are from

- **From**: a 1nspecT-managed sender address that passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC verification. The display name shows your company name (from Company Info) so clients see the email as coming from you.
- **Reply-to**: workflow emails have no reply-to, so a client replying does not reach your inbox automatically. Put a phone number or contact email in the template body.
- **Content**: your branding (logo, colors, company name) with any template you configured in Email Templates.

## Deliverability

1nspecT works with Resend to maintain strong sender reputation, so your emails reach client inboxes rather than spam. In practice, almost every subscriber sees ~95%+ inbox placement, with the usual exceptions:

- Some corporate email systems quarantine any transactional email from an unfamiliar sender
- Gmail Promotions tab catches some branded transactional emails (clients can mark as Important to pull into the inbox)
- A client who marked a past 1nspecT email as spam will have future emails from your account go to spam too, until they correct it

## Custom-from domains (not yet)

Sending from your own domain (`info@yourcompany.com`) rather than the 1nspecT-managed sender is not available today. If this is critical for your firm, let your onboarding specialist know; it is on the roadmap.

## Delivery tracking

1nspecT records a delivery status for **each recipient** of each email. When an email bounces or fails to deliver:

- The **Communications** tab on the child inspection shows the per-recipient delivery status.
- A **bounce banner** appears at the top of Inspection Details.
- A **bounce notice email** is sent to you.
- The Dashboard shows an **"emails didn't arrive"** alert linking to the affected inspection.

## When an email didn't arrive

- Check the Communications tab on the inspection for the recipient's delivery status; a bounce usually means a mistyped address, so correct it on Client & Realtor Info
- Check spam and promotions folders
- Check the Activity Log to confirm the workflow step ran (it shows workflow and email events, the 200 most recent; if it never shows, the trigger didn't fire — see [First-run gotchas](/1nspect/getting-started/first-run-gotchas))
- To send again, use **Fire Workflow Trigger** or **Send/Resend Contract Bundle** on the Communications tab. There is no per-email Resend button.
