A reinspection has its own rating scale, separate from the original inspection's. 1nspecT's reinspection ratings come from the SoP: each SoP defines a `reinspectionMode.ratings` set. TREC ships with Repairs Complete, Repairs Not Complete and New Deficiency; other SoPs ship with their own. On a cloned SoP you can edit the ratings to match how you want your reinspection reports to read.

## Where reinspection ratings come from

Every SoP has two rating scales under the hood:

- **Primary ratings** (`sop.ratings`) — used on the original inspection
- **Reinspection ratings** (`sop.reinspectionMode.ratings`) — used on reinspections of that inspection

Open **SoP Manager** (top-nav) and click **View Ratings** on any SoP to see its scales. The viewer has no reinspection toggle.

## TREC reinspection ratings

The platform TREC SoP ships with these reinspection ratings:

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RC | Repairs Complete | The deficiency was addressed and now passes |
| RNC | Repairs Not Complete | The deficiency is still present or incompletely addressed |
| ND | New Deficiency | A new issue found during the reinspection |

These match the TREC-expected language on the reinspection report.

## Where ratings appear on the reinspection

- **On mobile**: the reinspection repair checklist shows one card per repair item. For each, pick the rating from the reinspection scale. See [Reinspections](/1nspect/workflows/reinspections).
- **On the printed reinspection report**: each repair item shows before and after photos, the parent rating, and the reinspection rating. The full label ("Repairs Complete" / "Repairs Not Complete") is preferred over short labels on the printed report.

## Customizing reinspection ratings

Primary ratings stay locked on TREC and InterNACHI clones, but **reinspection ratings on a cloned SoP are editable**. The workflow:

<Steps>
<Step title="Clone the SoP">
SoP Manager › the platform SoP you want to customize › **Clone to Customize** (Professional and Team plans). Name your clone.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the clone">
In the Standards of Practice list, find your custom SoP.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit the reinspection ratings">
Click **Edit Ratings** on the clone. Add, rename, remove or re-color the reinspection ratings. For example, add a `PARTIAL` rating with the label `Partially Complete` for repairs that are started but not finished.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and repoint your template">
Save the SoP. Open your template and set its SoP to your custom clone. New reinspections will use your custom reinspection ratings.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Primary vs. reinspection rating scales are separate

The two scales are independent. On TREC and InterNACHI clones only the reinspection set can be edited, because the primary set is inherited live from the base.

## Platform-SoP reinspection ratings

The reinspection ratings on platform SoPs themselves are not editable. You must clone first.
