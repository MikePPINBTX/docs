1nspecT ships two phase-inspection SoPs for new-construction work: **Phase 1 Pre-Pour** (foundation before the slab is poured) and **Phase 2 Pre-Drywall** (framing and rough-in before drywall goes up). Each has its own template, its own rating scale and its own AI narrative framing. Together they cover the two phases most new-build inspectors are hired for.

## Phase 1 Pre-Pour

### The SoP ID

Pre-Pour in the code is `phase_1_pre_pour`. The seeded template is named `{slug} Phase 1 Pre-Pour`, where the prefix is your company slug.

### What it covers

Foundation elements that will be concealed by the slab:

- Formwork (dimensions, alignment, bracing)
- Reinforcing steel (grade, placement, spacing, cover)
- Post-tension cables (if applicable)
- Plumbing rough-in (water, drain, waste, vent)
- Electrical rough-in (conduit, grounding)
- Vapor barrier, insulation
- Pre-pour conditions (weather, temperature, moisture)

### Pricing basis

Pre-Pour services are priced on the **foundation footprint**, not the home's living area. Set the pricing basis on the primary service in Pricing Manager (**Business Ops › Pricing › Primary Services** → the Pre-Pour service → Pricing / duration basis = `Foundation footprint`). See [Primary services](/1nspect/getting-started/primary-services).

### AI narrative framing

The AI uses pre-construction scope language: references to drawings and plans, soils reports, and the project superintendent. Causation language stays hedged because the slab is in-progress, not finished.

## Phase 2 Pre-Drywall

### The SoP ID

Pre-Drywall in the code is `phase_2_pre_drywall`. The seeded template is named `{slug} Phase 2 Pre-Drywall`, where the prefix is your company slug.

### What it covers

Framing and systems that will be concealed by drywall:

- Framing (floor, wall, roof, headers)
- Fasteners (shear-wall nailing, hangers, hold-downs)
- Sheathing (type, nailing pattern, flashing)
- Rough-in plumbing (venting, slopes, connections)
- Rough-in electrical (box fill, circuit routing, grounding)
- Rough-in HVAC (duct routing, sealing, insulation)
- Insulation (where applicable at this stage)
- Window and door installations

### Pricing basis

Pre-Drywall on a multi-unit building is priced on the **building total** (all units added together). Pricing Manager handles this: set the pricing basis to `Building total (all units)` on the Pre-Drywall service.

### AI narrative framing

The AI uses in-progress language: things that are correctly in place, things that need correction before drywall, reservations about systems that cannot be fully evaluated at this stage.

## Rating scales

Both phase SoPs use rating scales appropriate to in-progress construction (not the TREC resale I / NI / NP / D scale). Open each SoP in **SoP Manager** and click **View Ratings** to see the exact scales shipped. Phase SoPs are not locked.

## Reinspection

Phase inspections can carry reinspections like any other inspection. The reinspection rating scale for a Pre-Pour or Pre-Drywall reinspection is set on the phase SoP itself. See [Reinspection rating scales](/1nspect/features/reinspection-rating-scales).

## 11-month warranty inspection

The 11-month warranty inspection (performed before the builder's one-year warranty expires) is not a dedicated phase SoP in 1nspecT today. Set it up as a primary service in Pricing Manager that uses either your TREC or InterNACHI template with the Inspection Type set to **11-month warranty**. The AI adjusts narrative framing automatically.

## Multi-phase orders

One order can carry Pre-Pour, Pre-Drywall, Final and 11-month warranty as separate phases. (The **Bundle Agreement Group** field on primary services is saved but never read, so it does not bundle agreements today.) See [Primary services](/1nspect/getting-started/primary-services) and [The order lifecycle](/1nspect/workflows/booking-to-published-report).
