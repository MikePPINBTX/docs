1nspecT handles refunds through the same channel the payment came in on. A Stripe-paid order refunds through Stripe, straight back to the client's card. A manually-paid order (Venmo / Zelle / Cash App) you refund outside 1nspecT and then record the refund in the web admin.

## When refunds happen

| Trigger | Refund scope |
| --- | --- |
| Cancel Order (full) | Full refund of the order total |
| Cancel Order (one phase, multi-phase order) | Partial refund for the cancelled phase |
| Remove Ancillary on a paid order | Partial refund for the removed ancillary |
| Goodwill refund | Any amount, your choice |

Covered on the order side in [Managing orders](/1nspect/workflows/managing-orders).

## Refunding a Stripe-paid order

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the order">
Inspections top-nav then the order.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the refund action">
Cancel Order (full refund), or Remove Ancillary (partial), or a direct Refund action.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the amount">
1nspecT calculates the refund amount from the cancellation / removal / direct entry. Review before confirming.
</Step>
<Step title="Send to Stripe">
1nspecT calls Stripe's refund API. The refund hits the client's card in Stripe's standard timeline (typically 5 to 10 business days).
</Step>
<Step title="Credit note generated">
A credit note PDF is attached to the order. The order's balance updates. Monthly Reconciliation picks up the refund on the next report run.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Refunding a manually-paid order

Since you own the Venmo / Zelle / Cash App transaction, you send the refund through those apps directly. Then come back to 1nspecT and record the refund:

<Steps>
<Step title="Send the refund in the app">
Open Venmo (or Zelle or Cash App), find the original payment, and send the refund back.
</Step>
<Step title="Record the refund in 1nspecT">
Open the order, use the refund action, enter the refunded amount and the date. The order balance updates and the credit note is attached.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Partial refunds

All of the above support partial amounts. Enter the amount you want to refund (not necessarily the full order total). A partial refund leaves the order with a reduced net paid amount.

## What a refund does on the client's side

For the client:

- They receive the refund through the original payment channel (Stripe to their card, or you Venmo them back)
- The invoice PDF updates to show the credit note alongside the original invoice
- The report link stays unlocked (refunds do not lock a previously-unlocked report)
- An email notification can fire if your Communications workflow has a Refund Issued trigger set up

## Reconciling refunds

- **Monthly Reconciliation** report shows refunds as negative entries in the period, with the method (Stripe or manual)
- **All Invoices** report shows the credit note separately from the original invoice
- **Tax Summary** report deducts refunded tax from the taxable total
