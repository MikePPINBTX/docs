Refunds in 1nspecT are narrow by design: Stripe refunds, issued automatically, from exactly two actions on the order. If you need to refund a manually-paid order (Venmo, Zelle, check), that happens outside 1nspecT and is not recorded in the product.

## When a refund fires

Two paths trigger a Stripe refund:

| Action | When | What refunds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cancel Order** | You cancel the whole order or a single phase from Inspection Details | The paid amount for the cancelled scope |
| **Remove Ancillary** | You remove an ancillary service from a paid order | The ancillary's portion of the paid amount |

Both only issue a refund when the inspection was paid through Stripe. If payment came in by Venmo, Zelle, Cash App or an offline card entry, no Stripe refund happens.

## The `refund.issued` event

Each successful Stripe refund fires the `refund.issued` workflow trigger. You can wire up an email on this trigger in **Business Ops › Communications › Workflow** to notify the client that a refund is on the way.

## One refund per inspection

A single inspection can be refunded through Stripe only once. If you trigger a second refund on the same inspection (for example, remove one ancillary and then cancel the order), 1nspecT does not issue a second Stripe refund; it returns the first refund unchanged. Refund any further amount directly in your Stripe dashboard.

## What's not supported

The product deliberately keeps the refund surface small:

- **No direct Refund button** on the order. You can only refund through Cancel Order or Remove Ancillary.
- **No goodwill refund** for an arbitrary amount. If you want to refund a client for a non-cancellation reason, process the refund directly in your Stripe dashboard.
- **No credit note PDF.** The order's invoice is regenerated in place; it does not spawn a separate credit note.
- **No manual-refund record.** If you send money back through Venmo/Zelle/Cash App/check, there is nowhere in 1nspecT to log that refund. Track it in your bookkeeping.
- **No refund entries in the reports.** Monthly Reconciliation, All Invoices and Tax Summary all read the current state of the inspection, not refund events.

## What the client experiences

For a Stripe refund:

- The money returns to the client's card through Stripe's standard timeline (typically 5 to 10 business days)
- The invoice PDF regenerates. The inspection stays marked paid, even after a full refund
- If you set up the `refund.issued` trigger with an email, the client gets a notice

## Partial refunds

Removing a single ancillary from a paid order yields a partial refund for that ancillary's amount (as long as it is the first refund on that inspection). The order stays active for the remaining services; the balance adjusts.

Cancelling a single phase in a multi-phase order partially refunds that phase.

## For manual-payment refunds

If the client originally paid by Venmo, Zelle, Cash App or an offline card entry:

1. Send the money back to the client through the same channel
2. Keep a note in your own bookkeeping that this inspection was refunded
3. Cancel the order or remove the service in 1nspecT so the record matches reality (no Stripe refund will fire, but the order state updates)
