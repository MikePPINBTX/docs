Some clients want to pay through Venmo, Zelle or Cash App instead of a card. 1nspecT supports that: configure your handles in **Business Ops › Payments**, the client sees them in their portal, and you click **Mark as Paid** in the web admin when the money arrives.

## Configuring your handles

In **Business Ops › Payments**, the **Manual Payment Options** section carries three fields. Fill in only the ones you accept; blank fields are hidden from the client portal. Handles show in the portal as one line of text, and only when online payments are off.

| Service | Field format |
| --- | --- |
| Venmo | Your username without the `@` |
| Zelle | Email address or phone number registered with Zelle |
| Cash App | Your cashtag without the `$` |

See [Payments setup](/1nspect/admin-operator/payments-and-contracts).

## What the client sees

When online payments are off, the client portal shows your configured handles as a single line of text in the Payment section. The client opens the matching app and sends the payment themselves.

## Recording the payment

Once the money arrives:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the order">
Inspections top-nav then the order.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Mark as Paid">
The payment modal opens.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the details">
Three fields: **Amount paid**, **Date paid** and **Payment method** (Cash, Check, Zelle, Venmo or Offline Credit Card). There is no note field, and Cash App is not in the method list.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Record Payment">
The order is marked paid. The report unlocks (reports are always payment-locked until paid), and the Monthly Reconciliation report picks up the payment.
</Step>
<Step title="The report goes out automatically">
If the report is already published, marking it paid emails it to the client and agent (the Report Delivered trigger), the same as a Stripe payment. The dialog tells you this before you confirm, so there is no need to click **Deliver** afterwards. If the report isn't published yet, the Payment Received trigger fires instead.
</Step>
</Steps>

## No partial payments

Mark as Paid always sets the order to paid. There is no partial state, so wait until the full amount has arrived before you record it.

## Reconciling manual payments

Manual payments appear in Monthly Reconciliation under the **Manual only** filter. The report shows gross amount with a Stripe fee of \$0 (because no Stripe fee was deducted). Pair with your Venmo / Zelle / Cash App activity for your month-end books.

## Why not just Stripe?

Stripe is simpler (auto-unlocks reports, no manual marking, auto-reconciles), but some clients prefer manual payment to avoid card fees or because they trust Venmo more than card entry. Supporting both gives you flexibility.
