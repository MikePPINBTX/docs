Some clients want to pay through Venmo, Zelle or Cash App instead of a card. 1nspecT supports that: configure your handles in **Business Ops › Payments**, the client sees them in their portal, and you mark the order paid in the web admin when the money arrives.

## Configuring your handles

In **Business Ops › Payments**, the **Manual Payment Options** section carries three fields. Fill in only the ones you accept; blank fields are hidden from the client portal.

| Service | Field format |
| --- | --- |
| Venmo | Your username without the `@` |
| Zelle | Email address or phone number registered with Zelle |
| Cash App | Your cashtag without the `$` |

See [Payments setup](/1nspect/admin-operator/payments-and-contracts).

## What the client sees

In their client portal, under the Payment section, each configured handle appears as a copy-to-clipboard field. The client taps to copy the handle, opens the matching app, and sends the payment themselves.

## Marking the order paid

Once the money arrives:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the order">
Inspections top-nav then the order.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Mark as Paid">
The Mark Paid modal opens.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the details">
Amount received, date, and an optional note for your records.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
The order is marked paid. The Payment Received trigger fires, the report unlocks (if payment-locking is on), and the Monthly Reconciliation report picks up the payment.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Partial payments

If a client sends only part of the balance, Mark as Paid can record the partial amount. The order shows a balance due; the client portal still shows the handles so they can send the rest.

## Reconciling manual payments

Manual payments appear in Monthly Reconciliation under the **Manual** method filter. The report shows gross amount with a Stripe fee of $0 (because no Stripe fee was deducted). Pair with your Venmo / Zelle / Cash App activity for your month-end books.

## Why not just Stripe?

Stripe is simpler (auto-unlocks reports, no manual marking, auto-reconciles), but some clients prefer manual payment to avoid card fees or because they trust Venmo more than card entry. Supporting both gives you flexibility.
