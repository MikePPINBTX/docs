1nspecT generates invoices automatically as the order moves through its lifecycle. The invoice is a PDF stored on the order, accessible from the client portal and from the [All Invoices](/1nspect/features/all-invoices) report. When payment lands, the PDF is re-stamped PAID.

## When invoices are generated

| Event | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Agreement signed | Initial invoice created for the signed amount |
| Order edited with delta billing (total changes) | A top-up invoice is generated for the delta |
| Add Service on a signed order | An incremental invoice is generated for the added service |
| Payment received (Stripe or Mark as Paid) | The matching invoice is re-stamped PAID with the payment date |
| Refund issued | A credit note is generated |

## Where invoices live

- **On the order**: Inspection Details then the Invoices section. Every invoice generated for the order with per-row PDF download.
- **In the client portal**: the client sees invoices in their portal under the Invoices section, with per-row PDF download.
- **In the Reports tab**: the [All Invoices](/1nspect/features/all-invoices) report lists every invoice in a date range with per-row PDF and bulk CSV export.

## PDF content

Every invoice PDF includes:

- Your company name, logo and address (from Company Info)
- The client name (and co-client, if any) and property address
- The itemized services with per-line prices
- Any discounts applied
- Any surcharges applied
- Taxes (per your Tax Rate in Business Ops then Pricing then Settings)
- Grand total
- Payment status (DUE or PAID with the date)
- Payment instructions (your Stripe payment link, or your manual payment handles)

## PAID stamping

When a payment is recorded against the invoice:

- The invoice PDF is regenerated with a PAID stamp
- The payment date and method are added
- The old DUE version is replaced; the URL stays the same so client-shared links continue to work

## Package invoices

For multi-phase orders (new construction with Pre-Pour, Pre-Drywall, Final), invoices can be generated per phase or as a single package invoice. The default follows your pricing setup and whether phases share a Bundle Agreement Group.

## Credit notes

Refunds generate credit notes rather than editing the original invoice. The original stays as the historical record; the credit note reduces the net amount owed.

## Where sales tax comes from

Your sales tax rate is set in **Business Ops then Pricing then Settings** under Financial Settings. Each service that is marked Taxable on the Ancillary Services tab contributes to the taxable total; non-taxable services (pass-through fees, for example) are excluded.
