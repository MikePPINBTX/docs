Each inspection has **one invoice**. It is not a series of top-up or incremental invoices: when the order changes, the same invoice is **regenerated in place** so it always shows the current total. The invoice is a PDF, available from the order, from the client portal and from the [All Invoices](/1nspect/features/all-invoices) report.

## Where invoices live

- **On the order**: Inspection Details, on the **Overview** tab's pricing card. The card has **View**, **Regenerate** and **Generate Invoice** actions.
- **In the client portal**: the client gets **Download Invoice** and, once paid, **Download Receipt**.
- **In reports**: the [All Invoices](/1nspect/features/all-invoices) report lists one row per inspection with a PDF link.

## How the invoice changes as the order changes

| Event | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Order created or agreement signed | The invoice can be generated from the pricing card (**Generate Invoice**) |
| Order edited, service added or removed, price changes | Billing is recomputed and the invoice is regenerated in place. Click **Regenerate** if you need to force a fresh PDF |
| Price drops below what was already paid | The order is only flagged as needing a refund. Nothing is refunded automatically |
| Payment recorded (Stripe or **Record Payment**) | The order is marked paid and the client can download a receipt |

There are no credit notes and no automatic refunds from a price drop. Stripe refunds happen only from **Cancel Order** or **Remove Ancillary**. See [Refunds](/1nspect/features/refunds).

## PDF content

The invoice PDF includes:

- Your company name, logo and address (from Company Info)
- The client name and property address
- The itemized services with per-line prices
- Any discounts and surcharges applied
- The grand total
- Payment status

The invoice PDF has **no tax line** and **no payment instructions**. It does not carry your Stripe link or your Venmo or Zelle handles; clients find those in the portal.

## No partial states

**Record Payment** always marks the order paid in full. There is no partial-payment state, no running balance on the invoice, and no top-up invoice for the difference.
