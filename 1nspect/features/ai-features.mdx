1nspecT uses AI to write report narratives in your own voice, inside the rules of your inspection standard. Rule-based checks review that writing before anything is published. This page is the catalogue of every AI capability: what it does, where you find it, and the guardrails that keep it accurate. For costs and allowances, see [AI Credits](/1nspect/features/it-tokens).

## How the AI fits into the workflow

Think of the AI as a drafting assistant, not an autopilot. In the field, it writes a narrative or extracts a reading so you can move to the next room. At the desk, it drafts anything left blank, cleans up language and flags anything that does not match the standard. You still approve every word.

A typical path through an inspection uses the AI in three waves:

1. **In the field, as you capture:** AI Generate on tricky findings, Vision Scan on findings where the photo tells the story, Scan Label / Reading for every nameplate and gauge, Smart Search for anything you have already written up before.
2. **On the drive back:** Finish & Draft Report runs one batch call on the server that drafts every blank narrative.
3. **At the desk:** Review flagged narratives, Polish anything you want tightened, draft System Performance Opinions, publish.

## Field capabilities (mobile)

### AI Generate

Writes a finding's narrative from your caption, the field note and the inspection standard. Produces a Condition / Analysis / Recommendation (CAR) narrative that you can edit on the spot.

- **Where:** Capture screen on the finding
- **Best for:** Common findings where your caption and notes already describe the problem
- **Tips:** The more specific your caption, the better the narrative. Make sure the section and subsection are correct before you tap AI Generate: the AI uses that context to calibrate severity and recommended action.

### Vision Scan

A modifier on AI Generate, not a separate button. Arm Vision Scan, then tap AI Generate. The photo goes up with the caption and field note, and the AI drafts the narrative from what it sees in the picture. The Co-Inspector pass runs on the same photo and flags up to three additional findings you did not note.

- **Where:** Capture screen on the finding
- **Best for:** Findings where the photo tells the story better than words can
- **Co-Inspector output:** Accept each suggestion into a new finding or dismiss it. The suggestions are suggestions only. You are the licensed inspector and your judgment is final.

### Scan Label / Reading

Reads values off a photo (a furnace data plate, a thermometer, an app screenshot) or routes a typed `field label: value` into the matching field on the Ratings screen. Always shows a confirm screen before applying.

- **Where:** Capture screen
- **Best for:** Any time you would otherwise type a reading. One screenshot of a Fieldpiece or similar tool extracts every reading on screen in a single call.
- **Match is forgiving:** "Furnace supply air temperature" finds "Furnace Tested Supply Air Temperature" without a perfect match.

### Smart Search

Semantic search across your narrative library. Pulls a narrative you have already written and refined. Faster than regenerating when your library has a close match.

- **Where:** Capture screen on the finding
- **Best for:** Repeat findings where the wording you have already approved is better than anything the AI would write fresh

### Add to Summary

Not AI, but it works hand in hand with the AI. Marks a finding for the report's summary section. Use it on safety items and potentially costly repairs so the client sees them front and center.

### Finish & Draft Report

The batch call. One tap syncs everything from the phone and asks the AI to draft every blank narrative on the server. The inspector gets a notification when the drafts are back.

- **Where:** Review screen
- **Best for:** The drive back to the office. Most inspectors leave light drafting for the field and let Finish & Draft handle the rest.

### System Performance Opinion (on mobile)

Drafts a section-level professional opinion (for example, Foundation Performance). You can write it by hand if you prefer.

### Property Baseline (included)

A pre-built snapshot of the inspected property pulled from its address. In the web admin the panel is titled **Property Baseline** and splits into two subsections: **Site Conditions** (soil map unit, dominant component, shrink-swell potential, FEMA flood zone, EPA radon zone, termite pressure and DOE/PNNL climate zone with recommended attic insulation) and **Construction-era materials** (materials typical for this build year and region, as candidates to look for). Sources: USDA NRCS SSURGO, FEMA NFHL, EPA Radon Zones, IRC R301.2(6) and DOE/PNNL Climate Zones. Appears on the inspection setup screen on mobile and on the order in the web admin. Visible to staff only.

### Offline AI queue

Scan Label / Reading calls that need the cloud (any photo-based scan) queue on the device when you have no signal and finish automatically when you reconnect. AI Generate and Vision Scan need a live connection when you run them.

## Office capabilities (web)

### AI Draft

The web Reviewer version of AI Generate. Fills in every blank narrative on a finding one at a time, or in a batch.

- **Where:** Report Workspace, per finding or in batch across the whole report
- **Best for:** Reviewing a mobile-captured inspection where the inspector left narratives blank for the office

### AI Draft and Polish (single click)

Drafts every eligible finding, fills required performance opinions, then runs Polish Report. One button, one trip.

- **Where:** Report Workspace

### Polish Narrative

Rewrites one narrative for tone and clarity. Keeps the facts, polishes the wording.

### Polish Report

Whole-report polish pass with a before and after review. Flags absolute claims so you can soften anything the standard requires you to hedge.

### Spell check

Fixes spelling and dictation stutters across the report. Limited to spelling so it never changes your findings.

### Vision Analysis (web)

The web equivalent of mobile Vision Scan. Drafts a narrative from a photo attached to a finding.

### Label and nameplate reading

The web equivalent of Scan Label / Reading. Fills equipment fields from a plate photo or an uploaded screenshot.

### Bundle Findings

Merges several related findings into one narrative. Useful when several captures describe facets of the same issue.

### System Performance Opinion

Drafts a professional opinion for a system from every finding in that section. Appears on the report summary.

### VoiceDNA

Learns your company's writing voice from sample reports (up to 8). Future AI Generate calls produce narratives that match that voice. Adjust tone and length with simple dials; narrative format control (bullets, paragraphs, structured, mixed) is on the roadmap. Owners and admins run VoiceDNA.

### Repair Request narratives (included)

When a buyer or buyer's agent builds a repair request from the client-facing report, the AI rewrites each selected deficiency in a plain, actionable, contractor-ready voice. No separate credit cost.

### Business Insights (included)

Dashboard commentary written from three viewpoints: CEO (strategy), Operations (throughput and quality) and Finance (revenue and margin). Refreshes three times a day. Appears on the Dashboard.

## What keeps the AI honest

These are rule-based checks written in code, not AI. They are what let you ship AI-drafted narratives without worrying about the AI inventing details.

### Specialist writing rules

HVAC, electrical, plumbing, site and grading, gas and fire safety findings each get their own writing rules. The AI uses the right voice for the trade and refers the right trade professional for the work.

### Standard-of-practice checks

Every draft is checked against the rules of the selected standard and flagged for review if something is off. Examples:

- Numbers must come from your captured readings. The AI cannot invent an amperage or a temperature.
- Causes cannot be stated as certain. "Possibly caused by" stays "possibly caused by."
- Your hedging stays. If you wrote "appears to," the AI will not promote it to "is."

### Plain-language pass

As narratives are drafted, jargon is replaced with plainer words. Your client reads the report.

### Computed, not guessed

HVAC temperature-split verdicts, equipment service life and roof-covering limitations are calculated from the readings you captured. The AI only words them. If you capture a 24°F supply-return split and the rule says 18 to 22 is normal, the finding reports "above expected range" because the math says so, not because the AI guessed.

### Pre-publish checks

The report cannot be published until the required items are complete. Before publishing, you review every flagged narrative, and every finding without a narrative must be drafted or explicitly signed off (with a record of who signed it off).

## Finding types and the AI

The AI adjusts how it frames findings for each inspection type. The template you choose tells it which type to use.

| Inspection type | What changes in the AI's wording |
| --- | --- |
| Resale | Standard tone: observations, implications, recommendations |
| Pre-listing | Softer framing for seller-side disclosure |
| New-build final | Punch-list voice, builder-ready |
| Pre-pour | Pre-construction scope language, references to drawings and plans |
| Pre-drywall | In-progress scope language, reservations about covered systems |
| 11-month warranty | Builder-warranty voice |
| Historical | Era-aware, references to typical construction of the period |
| Commercial | Business-ready scope language |
| Reinspection | Repair-verification voice (confirming completion, flagging incomplete) |

## Where this does not go

Features in this list are not available even if you have seen them mentioned in product screens or older docs:

- Voice dictation is not built in. The app cleans up stutters and inspection-term typos (for example, "freeze" to "frieze") in whatever your phone keyboard dictates, but there is no 1nspecT voice engine.
- There is no in-app report viewer on the mobile app. Reports are mobile-first web pages and PDFs that open in any browser from the client link.
- The AI does not cite specific building codes. Code compliance depends on permit date, local amendments and the authority having jurisdiction. If your standard requires a code reference, add it yourself.
