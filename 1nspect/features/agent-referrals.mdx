Agent Referral Report is where you see who's actually sending you business, how much they're sending, and how often their referrals convert to signed orders. Essential for firms where agents are the primary source of leads.

## Where to find it

**Reports** top-nav › the **Agent Referral Report** card.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Period | Date-range picker. |

## What the report shows

Stat grid with totals: total referrals, total revenue from referrals, average revenue per referral, overall close rate.

Table with one row per referring agent:

- Agent name
- Agent company / brokerage
- Email
- Referral count (how many leads came in from them)
- Signed count (how many became paid orders)
- Close rate (signed divided by referrals)
- Revenue (dollars from signed orders)
- Average revenue per signed referral
- Last referral date

## Export to CSV

Click the **CSV** button to download. Filename: `agent-referrals-[period-label].csv`.

## Using the report

- Agents with high counts and strong close rates are your core relationships. Make sure they're on your mailing list, your holiday card list, and in your top-agents panel on the Dashboard.
- Agents who used to refer regularly and have gone quiet are worth a direct outreach.
- Agents with high counts but low close rates may be sending you unqualified leads or may be in a market where price is a factor; a conversation can help.
