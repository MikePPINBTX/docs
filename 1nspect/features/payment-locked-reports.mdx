Payment-locked reports are built into 1nspecT. Every published report where the inspection isn't marked paid is locked on **both** the client's link and the agent's link. There is no setting to enable or disable this; it's always on.

## What the client sees when locked

When the client clicks the report link before paying, they land on the **Payment Required** page. The page shows the property address and a **Go to Client Portal to Pay** button. It does not display the amount owed; the client sees the amount on the client portal, which is where payment is collected.

## What the agent sees when locked

The agent view at `/report/[slug]/agent` shows a different page: **Report Locked Pending Payment**, with the property address and a message that the report will be available as soon as payment is received and to check back shortly. There is no pay button; agents are not sent to the client portal.

## What unlocks the report

Only two events unlock the report, in both links at once:

- A successful **Stripe payment**, confirmed by the Stripe webhook
- A manual **Mark as Paid** action in the web admin that flips the inspection's `isPaid` to `true`

Unlock happens within seconds of the payment event. What gets emailed depends on how the payment came in:

- **Stripe payment on a report that is already published:** the `report.delivered` trigger fires (instead of `payment.received`), so a delivery email goes out as the client regains access.
- **Mark as Paid:** only the `payment.received` trigger fires. No report email goes out. The office must click **Deliver** to email the report to the client.

## Which inspections are locked

Every inspection where `isPaid !== true`. There is no per-order override, no per-template setting, and no owner-only bypass. If the inspection is marked unpaid, the report is locked.

## For manually-paid orders

If the client paid you by Venmo, Zelle, Cash App or an offline card entry, use **Mark as Paid** on Inspection Details to flip the inspection to paid. The report unlocks immediately, but the client is not emailed the report; click **Deliver** to send it.

<Warning>
  Mark as Paid always sets the inspection to fully paid (`isPaid: true`). There is no partial-payment state in 1nspecT, so even recording a partial payment unlocks the report.
</Warning>
