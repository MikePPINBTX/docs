Payment-locked reports mean the client's report link stays locked behind the payment page until the invoice is paid. The report is published, the client knows it's ready, but they cannot read it until the payment lands. On payment, the link unlocks automatically.

## What the client sees when locked

When the client clicks the report link before paying, they land on the **Payment Required** page. The page shows the amount due and a Pay action that opens Stripe Checkout (if Stripe Connect is set up) or shows the manual payment handles. On successful payment, the client is redirected to the report viewer automatically.

## What the agent sees when locked

The agent view at `/report/[slug]/agent` carries its own locked state: **Report Locked Pending Payment**. The agent can either wait for the client to pay or forward the payment page to the client.

## Enabling payment-locked reports

The setting is at the company level, not per order. In the web admin, open **Business Ops › Company Info** (or Business Ops › Payments depending on how your account is configured) and find the payment-locking toggle. Enable it to lock every new report until paid.

## Per-order override

There is no per-order override today. If your account has payment-locking on, every report is locked until paid. If a specific client needs the report ahead of payment, mark the order paid manually (or process the payment first).

## What unlocks the report

Any of these events unlocks the client report link:

- A successful Stripe card payment (webhook from Stripe)
- A manual **Mark as Paid** action in the web admin
- An admin changing the order's payment status directly

Unlock happens within seconds of the payment event. The Report Delivered workflow trigger fires at the same time.

## When to use it, when not

Use it when:

- Your workflow is pay-at-delivery (standard for many firms)
- Clients tend to pay only after they see the report
- You want to collect before the client has what they need

Skip it when:

- You collect at agreement sign-time (so payment is already in before publishing)
- Your workflow is bill-later (invoice sent, paid by check, don't want to inconvenience clients)
- You have a specific client relationship that pays on terms

## Legal and accessibility

Payment-locking does not change your legal obligation to deliver the report. The published report is created and stored at the same time regardless of lock state; locking only controls the client's web access.
