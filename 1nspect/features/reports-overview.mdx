The Reports top-nav item is where your bookkeeper, your CPA and your office manager live. Nine reports with CSV export: the money, the pipeline, the team, the audit trail, and your annual tax summary. Each report has its own filter and columns; every one exports to CSV so your bookkeeper can carry it into whatever accounting software they use.

## Where to find it

In the top bar, click **Reports** (eighth top-nav item). The page header reads **Reports**.

The left side of the page lists the nine report cards. Click a card to open the report in the main panel.

## The nine reports

| Icon | Report | What question it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💵 | [Monthly Reconciliation](/1nspect/features/monthly-reconciliation) | Did our billed revenue match the money that landed in the bank, with Stripe fees per order? |
| 🏦 | [Stripe Payouts](/1nspect/features/stripe-payouts) | What orders made up each Stripe deposit, with fees per customer? |
| 🧾 | [All Invoices](/1nspect/features/all-invoices) | Every invoice in a flat list, with per-row PDF download and bulk CSV export |
| ⏳ | [Outstanding A/R](/1nspect/features/outstanding-ar) | Who still owes us money, bucketed by aging |
| 🥧 | [Service Mix](/1nspect/features/service-mix) | What's our revenue and inspection count by service type? |
| 🤝 | [Agent Referral Report](/1nspect/features/agent-referrals) | Which agents are referring, with revenue and close rate per agent |
| ⏱️ | [Inspector Productivity](/1nspect/features/inspector-productivity) | For each inspector: count, revenue, average on-site time, publish lag |
| 📜 | [Activity Log](/1nspect/features/activity-log) | Full chronological audit trail of business events in the period |
| 🧾 | [Tax Summary](/1nspect/features/tax-summary) | Annual revenue grouped by tax category for year-end accounting |

## Common patterns across reports

Every report has the same shape:

- A header with the report title and description
- A period filter (date range picker, or year picker for Tax Summary)
- A stat grid at the top with the key totals for the period
- A table with the per-row detail
- A **CSV** export button for the full data as currently filtered
