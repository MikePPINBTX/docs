The public booking form is the branded web page where your clients can request an inspection without talking to anyone. It opens at `app.1nspect.app/book/[your-slug]` with your logo, your colors, your services and your live prices. Clients walk through the flow, pick a time and submit. A booking creates a **lead** for your office to review; it does not create an order or send an agreement by itself.

## Where the client lands

Your public booking URL is set from your Company Info slug: **Business Ops › Company Info**. The URL looks like `https://app.1nspect.app/book/your-company-name`. Share the link in your email signature, business cards, agent-partner materials, SMS, and anywhere else clients might find you.

## What the client walks through

<Steps>
  <Step title="Property address">
    Address autocomplete suggests matches as the client types. The Property Baseline lookup is a gated button the client taps to pre-fill property details (year built, square footage, foundation type). Site conditions are hidden from the public.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Service selection">
    Primary services and ancillaries from your Pricing Manager. Only services with Internal Only off appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live price">
    The price updates as the client picks services, with tiered base pricing, auto-applied surcharges (mileage, age), and any discounts the client qualifies for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client details">
    Name, email, phone. A co-client row is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Date and time">
    Available slots for the inspector the client picks, from that inspector's Working Schedule and Google Calendar for the estimated duration. Taken slots are greyed out. Booking warns on a clash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submission creates a **lead** with status `pending_review` and fires the **Quote Submitted** trigger (per your Communications workflow). No order is created and no agreement is sent. Your office converts the lead to an order, and the agreement goes out from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's protected

- **reCAPTCHA** on the submit action to block spam bots
- **Rate limits** per IP so a single source can't flood the form
- **Hidden spam trap** (a honeypot field that bots fill in and people don't)

## Embedding on your website

Open **Business Ops › Company Info** and find the **Public Booking Form** section. It gives you an iframe of your booking page at `/book/{slug}`. Paste it into any HTML page on your site and adjust `height` to fit your layout.

The embedded form inherits your branding the same as the standalone page.

## Internal Only ancillaries

An ancillary marked **Internal Only** in Pricing Manager stays hidden from the booking form. Use this for crew-only extras you add to orders from the admin side only.

## Agent-booked inspections

The public booking form has no agent fields; it does not capture the client's agent. Your office adds the agent (Realtor Information) when it converts the lead to an order. Agents who send you regular business can use the Realtor Portal to sign in with a magic link and see the inspections you've done for their clients, but they can't book from it. See [Realtor portal](/1nspect/features/realtor-portal).
