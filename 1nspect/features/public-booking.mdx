The public booking form is the branded web page where your clients can request an inspection without talking to anyone. It opens at `app.1nspect.app/book/[your-slug]` with your logo, your colors, your services and your live prices. Clients walk through the flow, pick a time, submit, and the agreement sends automatically.

## Where the client lands

Your public booking URL is set from your Company Info slug: **Business Ops › Company Info**. The URL looks like `https://app.1nspect.app/book/your-company-name`. Share the link in your email signature, business cards, agent-partner materials, SMS, and anywhere else clients might find you.

## What the client walks through

<Steps>
  <Step title="Property address">
    Address autocomplete suggests matches as the client types. Picking one pre-fills the property details (year built, square footage, foundation type) via the Property Baseline lookup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Property questions">
    Beds, baths, foundation type, year built, square footage. The client can edit the auto-filled values if the public records are off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Service selection">
    Primary services and ancillaries from your Pricing Manager. Only services with Internal Only off appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live price">
    The price updates as the client picks services, with tiered base pricing, auto-applied surcharges (mileage, age), and any discounts the client qualifies for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client details">
    Name, email, phone. A co-client row is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Date and time">
    Available slots from each inspector's Google Calendar for the estimated duration. Taken slots are greyed out. Booking warns on a clash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submission creates the order. 1nspecT fires the Booking Confirmed trigger (per your Communications workflow), which typically sends the agreement to the client in the next few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's protected

- **reCAPTCHA** on the submit action to block spam bots
- **Rate limits** per IP so a single source can't flood the form
- **Address validation** against Google Places so junk addresses can't make it through

## Embedding on your website

Open **Settings** (top-nav) › the **Company** tab › the **Embed Your Wizard** section. The snippet looks like:

```html
<iframe
  src="https://app.1nspect.app/quote?embed=true"
  width="100%"
  height="800"
  frameborder="0"
  title="Inspection Quote Wizard">
</iframe>
```

Click the copy action to put it on your clipboard (an alert confirms _"Embed code copied to clipboard"_). Paste it into any HTML page on your site. The default height is 800px; adjust `height` to fit your layout. The iframe is responsive on `width="100%"`.

The embedded form inherits your branding automatically; it looks the same as the standalone page.

## Internal Only ancillaries

An ancillary marked **Internal Only** in Pricing Manager stays hidden from the booking form. Use this for crew-only extras you add to orders from the admin side only.

## Agent-booked inspections

The booking form captures the booking agent if the client enters agent details. For agents who book regularly, the Realtor Portal is a better path: agents sign in with a magic link, see every inspection you've done for them, and can book from there. See [Realtor portal](/1nspect/features/realtor-portal).
