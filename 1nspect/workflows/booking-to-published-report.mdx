Every order in 1nspecT follows the same path: a quote or booking becomes an order; an agreement goes out and gets signed; the inspection happens; the report is drafted, reviewed, and published; the client pays and receives the delivered report; the order is closed. This page is the map.

## The path at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quote or booking">
    A quote or booking comes in from the public booking form, from an admin in the Order Creation Wizard, or from the Field Quote on mobile. A public booking or a field quote creates a **lead** (pending review) and fires Quote Submitted; your office converts the lead to an order. An admin in the Order Creation Wizard creates the order directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agreement sent">
    The matching service agreement is sent to the client automatically (per your Communications workflow). Multiple agreements are bundled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agreement signed">
    The client opens the client portal and signs the agreement. The signature is stamped on the PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspection scheduled">
    The inspection is on the inspector's Google Calendar. Reminders fire per your workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture">
    On the day of the inspection, the inspector opens the job on mobile, captures findings, rates areas, and runs AI as they go (or defers to Finish & Draft at the end).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report drafted">
    Narratives are drafted by the AI (either in the field or batched by Finish & Draft) and arrive in the Report Workspace in the web admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report reviewed">
    An admin opens the Report Workspace, runs the Draft pass and Polish (or polishes manually), works through pre-publish checks, and resolves anything flagged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report published">
    Publish writes the final PDF to storage, locks the inspection, and generates the client and agent report links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report delivered">
    When the order is already paid, Publish fires `report.published`, which sends your configured delivery emails. If the order is unpaid, Publish fires the payment-pending variant instead, and the report stays locked until the invoice is paid. The **Deliver** button sends the report manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment received">
    Stripe (or a manual **Record Payment**) records the payment and unlocks the report link. Reports are always locked until the order is paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review requested">
    The review-request email goes out on the dedicated **Review Request** trigger, which fires on every publish with your Send Offset (for example `+2d`), with your Google or Yelp link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order closed">
    An admin picks Order Closed by hand. Nothing sets it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who does what

| Role | What they do on this path |
| --- | --- |
| Client | Fills in the booking form (public or via your link), signs the agreement, pays, reads the published report, optionally leaves a review |
| Agent | Receives the agent report link, builds a repair request if their standard allows |
| Inspector (field) | Runs the inspection on mobile, captures findings, drafts in the field or lets Finish & Draft handle it |
| Admin / Owner (office) | Reviews the drafted report, resolves pre-publish flags, publishes, delivers, follows up on payment |
| 1nspecT | Fires every automated email on its workflow trigger, calculates the AI draft, generates the PDF, stores it, serves the report link |

## Where each stage lives

| Stage | Where it happens |
| --- | --- |
| Quote and booking | **Inspections** top nav (Order Creation Wizard), public booking page, or mobile Field Quote |
| Agreement bundling and send | **Business Ops › Contracts** |
| Agreement sign | Client portal (sent by email to the client) |
| Capture | 1nspecT mobile app |
| Report drafting (field) | Capture screen → AI Generate, Vision Scan, Finish & Draft Report |
| Report reviewing | **Inspections › this order ›** Report Workspace |
| Publishing | Report Workspace › Publish |
| Delivery | Report Workspace › Deliver |
| Payment | Stripe (card), or **Record Payment** on the order (manual) |
| Review request | Fires on the **Review Request** workflow trigger |
| Order close | Manual only, from the order |

## Multi-phase orders (new construction)

New-construction orders bundle several phase inspections under one order. Each phase has its own date, inspector, inspection and report. (The **Bundle Agreement Group** field on primary services is saved but not used today.) See [Primary services](/1nspect/getting-started/primary-services) for setup.

## Reinspections

A reinspection is a special child of a completed inspection, created on the parent order from the Overview tab. It inherits the parent's template and system info, seeds a repair checklist from the deficiencies, and is priced and scheduled on its own. The parent's signed contract is not voided. See [Reinspections](/1nspect/workflows/reinspections).

## Mid-stream edits

Clients add a service, properties come in bigger than expected, dates slip. The order flexes:

- **Edit Order** changes clients, co-clients, property, services, pricing. Billing is recomputed and the order's single invoice is regenerated in place; a price drop only flags a refund, and Stripe refunds happen only from Cancel Order or Remove Ancillary.
- **Add Service** adds a service to a signed order without voiding the agreement. A reusable Resend Bundle action re-sends the right agreements where needed.
- **Reschedule** moves an inspection against the inspector's availability.
- **Cancel Order** cancels the whole order or one phase.

See [Managing orders](/1nspect/workflows/managing-orders).

## When something is blocked

Each stage has blockers 1nspecT enforces so a half-finished state cannot ship:

- The agreement must be signed before the inspection can start on the phone, and it must have been sent for the job to appear there at all
- Required pre-publish items must be resolved before Publish will accept the report
- The report link stays locked until the order is paid (always on)
- A booking must have an inspector assigned before it can go to the mobile app

For the specific error you see at each stage, see [First-run gotchas](/1nspect/getting-started/first-run-gotchas) and the [Troubleshooting](/1nspect/troubleshooting/common-issues) section.
