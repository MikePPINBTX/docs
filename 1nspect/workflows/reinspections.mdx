A reinspection confirms whether called-out repairs have been completed. In 1nspecT a reinspection is a new phase added to the parent order, linked to the original inspection, that inherits the parent's template and system info and seeds a repair checklist from the findings. It is priced and scheduled on its own. The parent's signed contract is not voided.

## Where to create one

Open the parent inspection: **Inspections** top nav › the order › the **Inspections** tab › the completed inspection. On that inspection's **Overview** tab, click **Create Reinspection** in the Quick Actions card. The button appears once the inspection is completed or its report is published, and not on the order itself or on another reinspection.

The dialog is titled **Create Reinspection — [inspection number]** and opens with an inline note explaining what will happen.

## Scope presets

The **Start from** field pre-seeds the checklist with one of three sets. Pick the one that matches what you agreed to re-check:

| Preset | What it seeds |
| --- | --- |
| Report summary — flagged items | Every finding on the parent inspection that was flagged into the report summary |
| Requested repairs — from repair request | Every finding the client (or buyer's agent) selected in the latest repair request |
| Entire report — all findings | Every reportable finding on the parent inspection |

The number in parentheses tells you how many items each preset includes.

## Refine the checklist

Under **Findings to reinspect**, every documented finding on the parent inspection is listed with its rating chip (D for deficiency), its caption, and the section and area it came from. The scope preset you picked pre-checks a starting set. Check or uncheck items individually to get to exactly what you want re-checked.

Two quick actions above the list:

- **Select all** — checks every finding
- **Clear** — unchecks everything

A chip at the bottom shows the live selection count. The dialog will not let you create a reinspection with zero items selected.

## Pricing and payer

The reinspection fee starts from your service pricing (the Reinspection pricing model on the primary service in Pricing Manager). The fee is **editable** in the dialog before you create the reinspection. A **Payer** option lets you choose who is billed: **Client**, **Realtor** or **Complimentary**.

See [Primary services](/1nspect/getting-started/primary-services) for setup.

## Scheduling

Schedule the reinspection like any other inspection: date, time, inspector. Google Calendar availability applies.

## Agreement

The original signed agreement is not voided. The **Require a signed reinspection agreement** checkbox is on by default only when a reinspection agreement exists in **Business Ops › Contracts** (an agreement with "Reinspection" ticked under when to send it). Leave it checked to send that separate reinspection agreement to the client, or uncheck it to skip. If no reinspection agreement exists, the checkbox is off and disabled.

## Mobile capture

On the mobile app, a reinspection shows up as its own Assigned Inspection with the parent inspection's template and system info already loaded. The capture flow is tuned to the task:

- The repair checklist shows one card per repair item, with the original photo and narrative visible (also available offline).
- Each item has its own rating, with an after photo. The rating options come from the reinspection rating set in your SoP.
- If something new comes up, use the separate **＋ Add New Deficiency** button to record it.
- A completeness check confirms every item has been rated before you finish.

## AIPI for reinspections

If the narrative collection the parent inspection used has a reinspection-specific AIPI set up, the reinspection report prints that AIPI in place of the standard one. Use the reinspection AIPI for scope limitations specific to the reinspection (one-visit scope, no new findings scope, follow-up fee structure). See [AIPI](/1nspect/getting-started/aipi).

## Report

The reinspection report shows before-and-after photos side by side for each repair item, the rating transition (D → Complete, D → Not Complete, D → New Deficiency), and any new findings captured during the reinspection.

## Saved drafts

Drafts save only when you click **Save draft**; closing the dialog without it does not save anything. The next time you open the dialog on the same inspection, you'll see a restore notice listing how many items are in the saved draft. Changing the Start-from preset replaces the saved draft with the new preset's starting set.
