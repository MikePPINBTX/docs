Before a report is published, 1nspecT runs rule-based checks (not AI) against the standard of practice the inspection follows. Flags surface in the Report Workspace so you can act on them before Publish. Required items block Publish entirely until resolved. This page describes what the checks do, how to read them, and how to resolve the common ones.

## Where the checks live

Open the Report Workspace: **Inspections** top nav › the order › the inspection › Report Workspace.

The workspace has two tabs across the top:

- **Findings** — every finding on the inspection, with ratings, narratives, photos and the Narrative Review Panel
- **Legal Preamble** — the SoP-required legal preamble

The pre-publish checks surface on the Findings tab.

## The Narrative Review Panel

As you work through the report, the Narrative Review Panel evaluates each narrative against the SoP's writing rules and surfaces any issue:

- Numbers that don't come from captured readings
- Causes stated as certain where the SoP requires hedging
- Jargon that should be plain-language
- Hedging dropped by the AI that your field note included
- Specialist rules for HVAC, electrical, plumbing, site and grading, gas and fire safety
- A passing finding with no Recommendation when your field note asks for an action (see below)
- A narrative written by the backup AI model, whose headline tag needs a second look (see below)

Each flag links to the finding so you can jump straight to it, fix the wording, and move on.

### Passing finding is missing the action your note asks for

This flag is for review only and never blocks Publish. It appears when a passing finding tagged **Acceptable Performance** has no Recommendation, but your field note asks for an action ("recommend", "replace", "repair", "service", "monitor", "adjust", "clean" and similar). The message reads: _"This passing finding has no Recommendation, but the field note asks for an action. Add the Recommendation the note asks for."_

If the note doesn't ask for an action, the draft gets _"No action is required at this time."_ as the Recommendation automatically.

### Drafted by the backup AI

If the main AI model is unavailable when a narrative is drafted, a backup model writes it instead, so the draft still arrives. This is rare. The finding then shows the flag **Drafted by the backup AI — check the Safety / Structural tag**, with the message: _"Drafted by the backup AI model while Gemini was unavailable. Check the headline tag — especially whether it should be a Safety or Structural Deficiency — before publishing."_

The backup model is less reliable at choosing the headline tag, so check that the tag matches the finding, especially for Safety and Structural Deficiencies. Fix the tag if it's wrong. The flag is for review only and never blocks Publish.

The flag shows only in the web Report Workspace, not in the mobile app.

## Completeness gate

Publish is a hard gate. Before Publish will accept the report, the completeness gate checks:

- Subsection ratings (every subsection that requires a rating has one)
- Required fields
- The FPO check
- Every finding has either a narrative, or an explicit "publish without narrative" sign-off

Unresolved narrative violations from the review panel also **block** Publish until you acknowledge them. Flags marked for review only, such as [Passing finding is missing the action your note asks for](#passing-finding-is-missing-the-action-your-note-asks-for) and [Drafted by the backup AI](#drafted-by-the-backup-ai), never block Publish.

Any missing items are listed as **publish blockers**. Each blocker shows a **Section → Subsection** label so you know exactly where in the report the missing item belongs. Resolve all of them and Publish will go.

## Findings without a narrative

If a finding has no narrative when you try to Publish, the Publish action refuses with a clear message:

> _"Cannot publish — N finding(s) have no narrative. Draft them (AI Draft & Polish), or choose 'Publish without narrative' for each one in the review panel."_

Two ways to resolve:

1. **Draft the narrative.** Run **AI Draft & Polish** (drafts every eligible finding, then Polish Report) or draft the finding individually.
2. **Publish without narrative.** For a finding that doesn't need a narrative (an observation that's already documented in a related finding, say), open the review panel for that finding and sign off on Publish without narrative. The sign-off is recorded against the caller on the server, so there's an audit trail of who approved publishing without a narrative on which finding.

## Aged-systems check

Separately from narrative review, 1nspecT runs an aged-systems check that looks at equipment age fields (HVAC units, water heaters) and flags any equipment past its typical service life that doesn't have a related finding. This is informational, not a publish blocker: it's a prompt to confirm you intended to publish without flagging the aged equipment.

## Preview PDF before publishing

Use **Preview PDF** to generate the PDF without locking the inspection. It warns you upfront if any publish blockers exist, but it still generates the PDF so you can walk the client through a draft, catch anything the review panel missed, or review layout. The real Publish action is blocked until the required items are resolved.

(Not to be confused with **Publish Report**, which does publish the report to the client and locks that version.)

## What Publish actually does

Once every required item is resolved, Publish:

- Locks the inspection (no more edits without a Republish)
- Uploads the final PDF to secure storage
- Generates the client's permanent report URL slug and the agent's view
- Saves a version record for this publish (version number and publish time)
- Fires the matching workflow trigger (`report.published` when the order is paid, which sends the configured delivery emails, or the payment-pending variant when it is unpaid)

See [Publishing and delivery](/1nspect/workflows/publishing-and-delivery) for what happens after Publish.
