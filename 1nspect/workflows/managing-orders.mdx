Orders rarely stay unchanged after they are placed. Clients add services, properties come in bigger than expected, dates slip. 1nspecT has a dedicated action for each of the common mid-stream edits, and each one keeps the agreement, the billing math and the inspector's calendar in sync automatically.

## Where to find the actions

Open the order: **Inspections** top nav, click into the order. You land on the Inspection Details page. The action buttons live along the top of the page, under the order number and property address.

## Edit Order

Use **Edit Order** when you need to change the property fields or the services on the order. This covers:

- Clients and co-clients
- Property address, square footage, year built, foundation type
- Primary and ancillary services on the order
- Discounts and surcharges
- Detached structures (add, edit, remove)

### Delta billing

Edit Order triggers delta billing: 1nspecT re-prices the order against your current pricing rules and figures out the difference. If the new total is higher, a top-up invoice is generated. If it is lower, a refund is issued. You see the delta before you commit the save.

## Add Service

Use **Add Service** when the client wants to add an inspection or an ancillary to an already-signed order, without voiding the original agreement. The action adds the service to the order and triggers the right agreement bundle (via **Resend Bundle** below) for anything that needs its own agreement signed.

### Resend Bundle

**Resend Bundle** re-sends the applicable agreements for the order, picking the right agreement (or bundle) for the current state of the order. Use it after an Add Service, or when you need to resend the original agreement because the client lost the email.

## Cancel Order

Use **Cancel Order** on the order header for the full multi-select cancel flow. You can:

- Cancel the whole order
- Cancel one specific inspection (phase)
- Cancel one ancillary service only

Cancellation cascades: the inspection is removed from the inspector's calendar, any pending agreement is voided, and (if the order was paid) a refund flow starts in Stripe.

## Reschedule

Moves one inspection to a new date and time. 1nspecT checks the inspector's Google Calendar availability for the new slot. The calendar event is updated automatically. If your workflow has an Inspection Rescheduled trigger, the matching email fires.

## Mark as Paid

Use **Mark as Paid** when a client pays by Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, a check, or any other channel outside Stripe. The modal captures the amount, the date, and a note for your records. The order is marked paid and (if your account locks reports until paid) the report link unlocks for the client.

## Create Reinspection

The Overview tab has a **Create Reinspection** action that spawns a reinspection as a new phase on the same order. Covered in detail in [Reinspections](/1nspect/workflows/reinspections).

## Order Summary and History

The right side of the Inspection Details page shows two cards:

- **Order Summary** — total amount, amount paid, amount due
- **Order History** — a chronological log of actions on this order (created, agreement sent, signed, paid, edited, cancelled)

## Inspections in this Order

The left side lists every inspection in the order (one for a single-phase order, several for new construction). Click into any inspection to open its own detail page and its Report Workspace.

## What you cannot change

Some fields are locked after certain milestones to protect the agreement and the published report:

- Signed contract wording — once an agreement is signed, the signed PDF is frozen. New agreements go out as separate documents.
- Published report content — once published, the report is a locked PDF plus web viewer. Changes require a Republish (which generates a new version).
- Order history — append-only. You cannot delete history entries.
