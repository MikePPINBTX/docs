Orders rarely stay unchanged after they are placed. Clients add services, properties come in bigger than expected, dates slip. 1nspecT has a dedicated action for each of the common mid-stream edits. Some of them change the price, and a price change voids the signed agreement so the client can re-sign on the new terms.

## Where to find the actions

Open the order: **Inspections** top nav, click into the order. You land on the order's Inspection Details page. The actions are split across three places:

| Where | Actions |
|---|---|
| **Order header** (top right, under the total and amount due) | **Edit**, **Edit Order**, **Add Service**, **Mark as Paid**, **Issue Refund** (once paid), **Cancel Order** (plus **Mark Complete** or **Reopen Order** when they apply) |
| **The child inspection's Overview tab › Quick Actions card** (open the inspection from the order's **Inspections** tab) | **Change Status**, **Edit Order**, **Mark as Paid**, **Mark as No Charge** (unpaid only), **Issue Refund** (once paid), **Assign Inspector**, **Reschedule**, **Create Reinspection**, **Cancel This Inspection** |
| **The child inspection's Communications tab** | **Send Contract Bundle** / **Resend Bundle** |

**Edit** on the order header toggles in-place editing of the order's tabs (client info, scheduling); **Save Changes** saves it. **Edit Order** opens the full Edit Order dialog described below.

## Edit Order

Use **Edit Order** when you need to change the property fields or the services on the order. This covers:

- Clients and co-clients
- Property address, square footage, year built, foundation type
- Ancillary services on the order (add or remove)
- Discounts and surcharges
- Detached structures (add, edit, remove)

### Billing recompute

Edit Order re-prices the order against your current pricing rules and **regenerates the order's single invoice in place**. There are no top-up invoices. If the new total is lower than what was paid, the order is only flagged as needing a refund; nothing is refunded automatically. Use **Issue Refund** to give the difference back (see [Refunds](/1nspect/features/refunds)).

### Edit Order voids the contract when the price or services move

If the edit moves any fee line or changes the services, in either direction, 1nspecT voids the contract on the order and on every phase. The order's Overview tab then shows a **Contract voided** banner with a **Send Updated Contract Bundle** button. Send it so the client can re-sign on the updated terms. An edit that changes no price or service leaves the signed agreement standing.

## Add Service

Use **Add Service** on the **order header** when the client wants to add another **primary** service (an extra phase, or an 11-month warranty inspection) to an order. It is added as its own inspection on the order, with its own agreement and its own bill, and the original signed agreement is **not** voided.

Add Service adds primary services only. To add an ancillary service, use **Edit Order** (which voids the contract on a price change, see above).

Tick **No charge (comped)** in the Add Service dialog to add the service at $0. The manual discount field is hidden while it's ticked. See [No charge (comped)](#no-charge-comped).

### Resend Bundle

The agreement bundle is sent from the child inspection's **Communications** tab. The button reads **Send Contract Bundle**, or **Resend Bundle** once a bundle is out for signature. It is disabled once the agreement is signed. While contracts are out, the order's Overview tab also shows a "Contracts sent" banner with its own **Resend Bundle** button, which resends the bundle for the whole order. Use it when the client lost the email.

## Cancel Order

Use **Cancel Order** on the order header for the cancel flow. From there you can pick which phases to cancel. To cancel just one inspection on a multi-phase order, use **Cancel This Inspection** in that inspection's Overview Quick Actions card.

Cancellation cascades: the inspection is removed from the inspector's calendar and any pending agreement is voided. A Stripe refund is issued automatically only when there was a Stripe payment.

## Remove Ancillary

To remove a single ancillary service, click the small **X** next to that service's line in the fee breakdown on the inspection's Overview tab. This opens the **Remove Ancillary** modal (not Cancel Order). It refunds through Stripe when the order was paid by Stripe, and, like Edit Order, it voids the contract because the price moves, so send an updated bundle afterwards. The X is hidden once the inspection is completed, published or cancelled.

## Issue Refund

Use **Issue Refund** (order header, or the inspection's Quick Actions card) to give some or all of a payment back. It appears once the inspection is paid. A card payment is refunded through Stripe. Any other payment is recorded as a refund you return yourself. You can refund more than once, up to the amount paid, and the report never locks again. See [Refunds](/1nspect/features/refunds).

## Reschedule

**Reschedule** is in the inspection's Overview Quick Actions card. It opens **Reschedule Inspection**, which moves that inspection to a new date and time. 1nspecT checks the inspector's Google Calendar availability for the new slot.

In the time grid, a struck-through time means the inspector is busy on their Google Calendar at that time. That includes personal events and all-day events marked **Busy** (an all-day event blocks the whole day), on any calendar the inspector owns or can edit. Events marked **Free**, cancelled events and invitations the inspector declined don't block a time. The inspection's own current time shows as open, so you can move it earlier or later on the same day, for example from 11:00 to 9:00, without entering a custom time.

When you click **Reschedule**:

- The calendar event is updated.
- The old **Inspection Reminder** is cancelled (its Communications row shows *skipped*, reason *rescheduled*), and a new one is queued for the new date.
- The **Inspection Rescheduled** trigger fires. Existing accounts were given a default Inspection Rescheduled email (to the client, co-client and buyer's agent) in November 2026. If yours has none, add a step on that trigger.
- History records *Rescheduled from the old date to the new date* under your name.

Quotes can be rescheduled too. They have no reminders.

## Mark as Paid

Use **Mark as Paid** (on the order header, or in the inspection's Quick Actions card) when a client pays outside Stripe. The button is hidden once the order is paid. The dialog has three fields: **Amount paid**, **Date paid** and **Payment method** (Cash, Check, Zelle, Venmo or Offline Credit Card), and you confirm with **Record Payment**. There is no note field. The order is always marked paid in full (there is no partial state) and the report link unlocks for the client, since reports are locked until paid.

## No charge (comped)

Use **No charge (comped)** when you're doing an inspection for free, for example a warranty courtesy visit or a make-good. A comped inspection costs $0 and counts as paid, so its report isn't locked. There are three ways to comp:

| Where | When |
| --- | --- |
| **No charge (comped)** checkbox above **Confirm & Create Order** in the [Order Creation Wizard](/1nspect/admin-operator/order-creation-wizard) | Every inspection on the new order is comped. |
| **No charge (comped)** checkbox in **Add Service** | The service you're adding is comped. |
| **Mark as No Charge** in the inspection's Overview Quick Actions card | An existing, **unpaid** inspection. |

**Mark as No Charge** on an existing inspection:

- **Only shows on an unpaid inspection that isn't already comped.**
- **Sends nothing by itself.** It voids an agreement that was already sent or signed. Then send the $0 agreement by hand from the inspection's **Communications** tab.
- **Doesn't email a report that's already published.** Unlike **Mark as Paid**, it doesn't deliver the report. Send it yourself if the client is waiting.
- **Won't comp a paid inspection.** Use **Issue Refund** instead.
- **Won't comp a whole multi-phase order.** Comp each phase from its own inspection.
- **Adds a History row**, for example *Marked as No charge (comped) — $450.00 waived*.

When you comp in the Order Creation Wizard, the agreement that goes out automatically for a single-phase order already shows $0.

What changes on a comped inspection:

- **The agreement** shows the normal price, then a **No charge** line, for a total of $0.00.
- **Issue Refund** is hidden, since nothing was paid.
- **Reports:** [Monthly Reconciliation](/1nspect/features/monthly-reconciliation) leaves out $0 inspections, and [Tax Summary](/1nspect/features/tax-summary) counts them as inspections with $0 revenue.

## Create Reinspection

The completed child inspection's Overview Quick Actions card has a **Create Reinspection** button that spawns a reinspection as a new phase on the same order. It appears once the inspection is completed or published. Covered in detail in [Reinspections](/1nspect/workflows/reinspections).

## The order's tabs

The order page has four tabs:

- **Overview** — the contract banners and the **Order Summary**: order number, created date, property, the inspections, the fee breakdown, and Total Fees, Paid and Balance Due
- **Client & Realtor Info**
- **Inspections** — every inspection in the order (one for a single-phase order, several for new construction). Click into any inspection to open its own detail page and its Report Workspace.
- **History** — **Order History**: the order's own events plus every inspection's, newest first. See [History](/1nspect/admin-operator/inspection-details#history).

## What you cannot change

Some things are locked after certain milestones to protect the agreement and the published report:

- Signed contract wording — once an agreement is signed, the signed PDF is frozen. If a later edit changes the price or services, the contract is voided and you send a new bundle for the client to re-sign; the old PDF is not rewritten.
- Published report content — once published, the report is a locked PDF plus web viewer. Changes require a Republish (which generates a new version).
